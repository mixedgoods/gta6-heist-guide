## Executive Summary

A surgical factual accuracy and editorial trust audit was conducted across `/safehouses-laundering`, `/escape-routes`, and `/crew-database`. All three canonical pages, their URLs, search/filter utilities, character dossier modals, and interactive laundering simulator tools were **100% preserved**, while every unverified statistic, hypothetical fee, fan-created property price, community-reported evasion tactic, and simulated character perk was audited and transparently labeled.

### Summary of Key Findings & Corrections
1. **`/safehouses-laundering` (Safehouses & Cash Laundering Simulator):**
   - **Verified Story Mode Reality vs. Pre-Launch Fan Simulation:** In verified GTA 6 Story Mode gameplay (documented on `/properties` and `/gta-6-money-guide/`), standalone residential houses cannot be purchased—players unlock **4 Story Safehouses** via chapter progression (starting with Jason's Key Lento stilt house provided rent-free by Brian Heder), alongside **8 purchasable garages**, **4 story garages**, **1 Ultimate Edition bonus garage**, and **7 contraband fences** (such as Yasmani's jewelry fence, Brian's Lockup keypad `7700`, Moses's untracked car drops, and Randy).
   - **Correction Applied:** Added a prominent **Gameplay Verification & Simulation Disclosure** banner at the top of `/safehouses-laundering`, updated the simulator UI and JSON-LD `FAQPage`/`SoftwareApplication` schema, and relabeled the 12 property catalog cards (8 static HTML cards + 4 hydrated Rockstar-lore cards from `gta6_master_intel.json`) so that all `$240,000–$18,500,000` prices, `6%–22%` laundering fees, `+5% / +10%` reinvestment bonuses, and `1–48 hour` clearance timers are explicitly identified as **Fan-Made Sandbox Simulations (Not Confirmed Rockstar Mechanics)** while accurately crediting Rockstar-confirmed lore locations (*Brian's Boat Works & Marina*, *Jack of Hearts / Only Raw Records*, *Vice City International Airport*, and *Jason's Key Lento Safehouse*).
2. **`/escape-routes` (Leonida Escape Routes & Evasion Tactics):**
   - **Unsupported Percentages & Radar Lock Claims Removed:** Removed all four fabricated `Heat Clearance: 100% / 92% / 95% / 88%` numerical ratings and the unsupported `< 100 feet military radar missile lock` claim in "Pelican Valley".
   - **Correction Applied:** Added a verification disclosure banner and separated each route card into **Confirmed Elements** (Vice City Metrorail/underpass corridors, Port Gellhorn coastal canals, Grassrivers wetlands & swamp airboats, and northern river gorges/helicopters) versus **Community Evasion Tactic / Tactical Theory** (using narrow passages to block patrol cruisers, road-to-boat transitions, shallow marsh traversal, and low-altitude terrain masking).
3. **`/crew-database` (GTA 6 Characters & Crew Roster):**
   - **Official Rockstar Cast vs. Community/Fan Simulator Dossiers:** Separated the **10 Rockstar-Confirmed Featured Characters** (*Lucia Caminos, Jason Duval, Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Brian Heder, Dre'Quan Priest, Raul Bautista, Real Dimez [Bae-Luxe & Roxy],* and *Stefanie*, plus *Lori Heder*, officially named in Brian Heder's Rockstar bio as his third wife) from **Trailer 1 Parody Cameos** (*Leonida Joker*), **Community/File References** (*Phil, Aunt Tee, Donnie, Wyman, Raymond, Rudi*), **Fan-Created Syndicate Archetypes** (*Petra Navarro, Valentina*), and **Series Lore / Wiki Entries** (*Jack Howitzer, Niko Bellic, Jimmy De Santa*, etc.).
   - **Removed False `OFFICIAL 3D MODEL` Badges & Relabeled RPG Stats:** Replaced all 14 false `OFFICIAL 3D MODEL` badges (and `official GTA 6 3D models` meta keywords/schema claims) with accurate source badges (`ROCKSTAR TRAILER / BIO`, `ROCKSTAR OFFICIAL BIO`, `TRAILER 1 CAPTURE`, `NAMED IN ROCKSTAR BIO`, `TRAILER 1 PARODY CAMEO`, `COMMUNITY / FAN DOSSIER`, `FAN-CREATED DOSSIER`). Removed fabricated numerical skill percentages (`96% (Elite)`, `94% (Max Radius)`, `96% (Pro Wheelman)`, `94% (Zero Recoil)`, `94% (+30s Delay)`, `+10% Cash Value`, `+40% Durability`, `+25% Speedboat Accel`, `95% (Fast Cut)`, `98% (City-Wide)`, `+35% Durability`, etc.) from the cards and explicitly relabeled all Heist Planner role bars, perks, and crew percentage cuts (`4%–8% Sim Cut`) in both `public/crew-database.html` and `GTA6.CharacterModal` (`public/assets/main.js`) as **Fan-Made Heist Simulator Estimates (Unconfirmed by Rockstar)**.
