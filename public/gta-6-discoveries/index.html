How GTA 6 Discoveries Are Submitted and Verified

This hub is a curated log, not an open comment feed. Entries above come from our editorial verification log. Player finds follow a separate, moderated route so nothing unreviewed ever appears on this page.

How to submit a discovery

  • Open the submission form. Four fields are required: a title, a category, a description of what you found, where it is and how to reproduce it (20 to 4,000 characters), and a confirmation that it is your own observation.
  • Optional details help reviewers: region, platform, game version, exact location, map coordinates, and external screenshot or video links. File uploads are disabled; links are reviewed privately and never embedded automatically.
  • You receive a reference number on submission. Keep it to ask for a correction or removal through the contact page.

What happens after you submit

  • Every submission is reviewed by a person. Pending, rejected and “needs more evidence” submissions stay private and are never listed publicly, shown on the map or counted toward 100% completion.
  • Approved entries are published on the Community Discoveries page with an evidence label.
  • Your contact email, if you give one, is never shown publicly. Credit is shown only if you choose it.

Evidence levels

  • Community Reported — accepted for publication, not independently verified by us.
  • Corroborated — supported by multiple genuinely independent observations or credible evidence. Several sites repeating one original find do not count as independent.
  • Independently Verified — reproduced by an authorized GTA6HeistGuide reviewer.

Our editorial standards are explained on the About page. Looking for tested exploits instead? Check the glitch and patch tracker.
