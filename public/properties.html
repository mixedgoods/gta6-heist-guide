How the GTA 6 Property Database Works

This database separates what you can actually own from what you can only use. In GTA 6 Story Mode the 4 safehouses are unlocked by story progression and cannot be purchased, the 8 purchasable garages are the only real estate you can buy (prices are not yet confirmed), and the 7 fence contacts are the characters who buy stolen goods and vehicles. Each record shows its region, how it is acquired, and whether it has a verified pin on the interactive Leonida map.

How to use the database

  • Use the search box and category filters in the database above to narrow the list by record type (Story Safehouse, Purchasable Garage, Story Garage, Bonus Garage, Fence, Vehicle Buyer) or by region.
  • Open a record to see its unlock requirement, parking capacity, interior features, any missable warning and its verification status.
  • Records with a map pin open directly on the interactive map so you can plan a route between a garage and a fence before a heist.
  • Unverified regional leads (currently 2) are listed for transparency but are not counted as fence contacts.

Acquisition rules worth knowing

  • Houses cannot be bought in Story Mode; the four story safehouses unlock through Chapters 1 to 4. The Chapter 2 motel base closes after the “Checking Out” mission.
  • The Paradise Garage in Watson Bay is a bonus garage included with the Ultimate Edition; see the Ultimate Edition bonuses guide for when it unlocks.
  • Brian’s Lockup is a keypad-gated fence; its code is covered in the safe codes guide.
  • Five fences buy stolen goods (Brian, Donnie, Dunk, Tyrone, Yasmani) and two buy vehicles (Moses, Randy). Compare their payouts in the fence payout comparison.
