⭐ ULTIMATE EDITION BONUS POST-PROLOGUE UNLOCK

How to Claim Jason's Duval Signature ES9 & Ultimate Edition Bonuses

Ultimate Edition items unlock after the prologue and must be claimed in-game rather than spawning automatically in your pocket. Follow the three steps below at Key Lento and Watson Bay.

STEP 01
Reach Jason's House (Key Lento)

Clear the prologue sequence to unlock your Chapter 1 stilt-house safehouse in Mariana County.

STEP 02
Inspect Safehouse / Trunk Locker

Check the safehouse weapon storage or vehicle trunk to retrieve the Duval Signature ES9 sidearm.

STEP 03
Check Paradise Garage & Vehicles

Claim the Dinka Enduro and Crest Kayak at Jason's House, plus Paradise Garage (Watson Bay) in Vice City.
