1. Confirmed Story Mode Heist Payouts

GTA 6 Story Mode features two major multi-stage Story Heists. Because Story Mode launched without a mission replay menu, you should lock a dedicated manual backup in our 15-Slot Manual Save Planner before launching either finale if you want to test alternate routes or protect a clean run.

CHAPTER 3 FINALE · MISSION #71

The Cartel Heist (Sinfrontera National Bank)

Baseline: $120,000 (Reported up to $150,000)

Led by Raul Bautista in Vice City, The Cartel Heist requires 5 mandatory setup missions: Security Expert, Heist Planning, Ride or Die, Safe Word, and Hidden Charges.

Baseline vs. Max Payout: Clearing the main vault stacks before the ~11-minute cell-tower blackout timer expires pays a confirmed $120,000 baseline cut. Several launch walkthroughs report takes up to $150,000 when hitting secondary vault stacks and bonus objectives cleanly—treat the $120,000 figure as the confirmed baseline.

Crew Cut Rule: Fixed narrative split managed by Raul Bautista—there are no GTA V-style selectable NPC gunman/driver percentage sliders.

CHAPTER 4 · STORY HEIST #2

VCIA Takedown (Vice City International Airport)

Confirmed Baseline: $250,000

The highest single Story Heist reward in GTA 6, targeting the high-security cargo and currency vault at Vice City International Airport after 4 mandatory setups: Close Surveillance, Delivery Method, Team Building, and Heist Plans in Place.

Baseline vs. Max Payout: Executing the stealth approach, terminal timing sequence, and loading-bay bag swap cleanly delivers the full $250,000 score. Completing Chapter 4's airport arc also progresses you toward That's Where The Money Is..., which unlocks the 8 repeatable Large Bank Vaults.

Crew Cut Rule: Scripted crew operation alongside Kordell and Raul's team; no customizable percentage-cut hiring screen.

3. Crew Cuts, Fence Deductions & Mission-Specific Financial Rules

Maximizing your net cash in GTA 6 depends less on crew-hire spreadsheets and more on avoiding five verified Story Mode deductions and lockouts:

  • No Selectable NPC Heist Crew Cuts: Unlike GTA V, you do not hire 5%–15% gunmen, drivers, or hackers before Story Heists. Both The Cartel Heist and VCIA Takedown use scripted narrative crews with fixed reward structures.
  • Fence Cuts & Regional Buyers: Stolen jewelry, watches, and contraband must be converted to cash through specific contacts rather than sold at regular stores:
    • Yasmani (Little Cuba, Vice City — Ch. 3): Primary jewelry and marked-bill fence offering the highest corroborated in-person payout rate ($10,000–$25,000 typical per standard jewelry store); using his remote pickup service incurs a driver cut.
    • Dunk (Tanglewood Lane, Port Gellhorn — Ch. 2): Unlocks after Slam Dunk; buys stolen goods and mid-tier contraband.
    • Donnie's Trailer (Key Lento — Ch. 1) & Tyrone (Vice City): Early and mid-game stolen-goods fences listed in our Properties & Fences Database.
  • Dye Pack Jammer (Mandatory for Banks): Unlocked during Chapter 3's Credit Check. Always equip the Dye Pack Jammer before robbing any of the 14 Small Banks or 8 Large Bank Vaults to prevent dye-pack value loss on stolen cash stacks.
  • $20,000 Free-Roam Pocket Cash Cap: Jason and Lucia cannot hold more than $20,000 in pocket cash during free roam. Always deposit earnings at physical ATMs or BuckMe terminals right after a robbery or heist payout.
  • Chapter 1 Lockout & Chapter 2 Cash Milestone: During Chapter 1's Buck Me (Fleeca Bank, Key Lento), install the BuckMe app and transfer cash into Family Savings ($5,000 Ch. 1 goal)—Family Savings is protected against Wasted/Busted losses and carries past Chapter 1's lockout (Tourist Trap / Just Like Paradise), whereas unspent liquid pocket cash is lost. In Chapter 2, bank $15,000 before launching the finale, Checking Out.

4. Verified Money-Making Activities Beyond Bank Vaults

Between major robbery unlocks, four corroborated Story Mode activities generate reliable cash with lower police escalation:

Randy's Wish List (Ch. 3 — Vice City / Balsero)

$30,000 – $35,000 / Car

Unlocks in Chapter 3 after Steal a Zorrusso for Randy. Delivering all 16 high-end vehicles (including the Pegassi Infernus, Piccia Furia, Bravado Banshee, and Declasse Mamba GT) pays $30k–$35k per vehicle and unlocks the Advanced Tracker Immobilizer + Supply & Demand trophy. View Vehicle Guide →

Moses Untracked Car Boosting (Ch. 2 — Port Gellhorn)

$1,000 – $2,000 / Car

Unlocks during Chapter 2's Moses mission at Watkins Auto Parts in Port Gellhorn. Scan parked cars with the WAiNK app to find untracked models and deliver them to Moses with zero police heat.

Kenton's Underwater Salvage (Ch. 1 — Leonida Keys)

~$1,400 / Shipwreck Dive

Unlocks after Chapter 1's Island Dreams with Scuba Gear and the Metal Detector. Covers 33 underwater discoveries and 41 named treasures across 7 sets with zero wanted-level risk. View Collectibles Guide →

GTA 6 Heist Payouts Frequently Asked Questions

How much does The Cartel Heist pay in GTA 6?

The Cartel Heist (Chapter 3 finale at Sinfrontera National Bank) has a confirmed baseline reward of $120,000. Some launch walkthroughs report up to $150,000 when securing secondary vault stacks and bonus objectives cleanly.

How much does VCIA Takedown pay in GTA 6?

VCIA Takedown (Chapter 4 Story Heist #2 at Vice City International Airport) pays a confirmed baseline reward of $250,000, making it the largest single Story Heist payout in the campaign.

What is the best repeatable money-making method in Story Mode?

In the endgame (after Chapter 4's That's Where The Money Is...), the 8 Large Bank Vaults pay the highest legitimate repeatable take at $65,000 to ~$205,000 per vault. Earlier in the campaign, combine Randy's Wish List ($30k–$35k/car), Small Banks ($5k–$45k), Jewelry Stores ($10k–$25k typical), and Moses car drops ($1k–$2k).

Can you replay Story Heists in GTA 6 to farm cash?

No. GTA 6 launched without a mission replay menu. Use our 15-Slot Manual Save Planner to keep a backup save before launching The Cartel Heist or VCIA Takedown if you want to retry a run.

Why did I lose money after Chapter 1 or hit a $20,000 cash limit?

Free-roam pocket cash caps at $20,000—deposit excess cash at physical ATMs or BuckMe terminals. Additionally, unspent liquid pocket cash is lost at the end of Chapter 1, so move funds into BuckMe Family Savings during Buck Me or buy permanent gear before finishing Chapter 1.
