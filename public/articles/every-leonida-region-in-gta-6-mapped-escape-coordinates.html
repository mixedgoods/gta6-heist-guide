2. Geographic Relationships, Counties & Map Connections

Understanding how Leonida's regions border one another makes story routing, robbery chains, and collectible sweeps much faster—especially because GTA 6 has no mission replay menu and requires physical travel to ATMs, fences, and gun stores.

How the 6 Regions Connect

  • East-to-South Coastal Spine (Vice City ↔ Leonida Keys): Vice City sits along the eastern Atlantic shoreline in Vice-Dale County. Heading south out of the southern metro and Mariana coast puts you onto the long causeways and coastal bridges linking the Leonida Keys archipelago (Key Lento, Gloriana Key, Goose Key, and Permit Keys).
  • East-to-West Wetland Corridor (Vice City ↔ Grassrivers ↔ Port Gellhorn): Directly west and southwest of Vice City's outskirts, dense urban strips give way to the Grassrivers wetlands in Mariana County (including Hamlet, Pelican Valley, Gullet Marsh, and Cottonmouth Lake). Crossing west through or north around the marshland brings you into Kelly County and the Gulf-facing coastal city of Port Gellhorn.
  • Central Inland Hub (Ambrosia & Lake Leonida): Positioned in the agricultural heartland north of the wetlands and between the eastern and western coasts, Ambrosia surrounds the Lake Leonida and Lake Tallaloko waterways. Its flat rural highway grid connects Port Gellhorn's industrial outskirts to northern Vice-Dale and Leonard County.
  • Northern Frontier (Ambrosia & Kelly County ↔ Mount Kalaga National Park): Continuing north into Lummox County transitions from flat farmland and industrial towns (such as Millport) into the elevated forested ridges, rivers, and canyons of Mount Kalaga National Park and Kalaga Reservoir.

Confirmed Counties of Leonida

Vice-Dale County & Mariana County: Vice-Dale covers Vice City, Vice Beach, VCIA, and surrounding urban/suburban areas (including the South Vice-Dale County radio tower easter egg). Mariana County spans the southern wetlands, Grassrivers marshland, and southern coastal settlements.
Kelly, Leonard & Lummox Counties: Kelly County encompasses Port Gellhorn, its surrounding bays, and western outskirts (including the Starlet Motel area). Leonard County bridges central rural territory, while Lummox County holds Mount Kalaga National Park and northern industrial outposts like Millport.

EAST COAST METRO · 71 MAP PINS

1. Vice City (Vice-Dale County)

Leonida's primary urban center and economic heart, balancing pastel Art Deco beachfronts with dense commercial towers, docks, and tight-knit residential districts.

  • Confirmed Districts & Landmarks: Ocean Beach, Vice Beach, Washington Beach, Downtown Vice City, Little Cuba, Balsero, Watson Bay (Paradise Garage), Port Vice City, Vice City International Airport (VCIA), Sinfrontera National Bank, Penmarch Isle, Breakwater Quarry, and Turtle Head Power Station.
  • Story Heists & Robberies: Anchors both major Story Heists—The Cartel Heist ($120,000, Ch. 3) and VCIA Takedown ($250,000, Ch. 4)—plus endgame Large Bank Vaults (Lombank Downtown & Ocean Beach).
  • Fences & Properties: Yasmani's Jewelry Fence (Little Cuba), Randy's high-end Wish List vehicle drops (Balsero, $30k–$35k/car), Tyrone (stolen goods), plus Chapter 3–4 safehouses and garages.
  • Verified Map Pins: 9 verified Vice City Portal LPL locations (Substations #1, #2, #4, #5, #7, #9, #11, #12 + Reactor 2) and 1 verified coastal Dolphin pin (10 verified pins total).

2. Leonida Keys

A sun-bleached chain of tropical islands linked by long over-water bridges south of the mainland, serving as the primary setting for Chapter 1 and underwater salvage.

  • Confirmed Islands & Landmarks: Key Lento (Jason's House, Brian's Lockup 7700, Moonstone Boutique 0420, Fleeca Bank for Buck Me), Gloriana Key, Goose Key, Permit Keys (shipwreck & Assassin Nipper .38), and Badger R&D Radio Tower.
  • Missions & Progression: Chapter 1 storyline including Lab Fees, Buck Me (BuckMe Family Savings), Island Dreams (unlocks Scuba Gear, Metal Detector & Kenton's Treasures), and the Chapter 1 cutoff at Tourist Trap.
  • Collectibles & Wildlife: Primary launchpad for Kenton's Treasures (33 underwater discoveries / 41 named relics, ~$1,400/dive). Verified wildlife pins: Green Iguana, Sea Turtle, and Lionfish.
  • Verified Map Pins: Badger R&D Radio Tower, LPL Substation #3 (Key Lento), LPL Substation #8 (Gloriana Key), plus 3 verified marine/island animal pins.

3. Grassrivers (Mariana County)

Leonida's untamed sawgrass marshes and mangrove backwaters between Vice City and the western coast—best navigated by airboat or high-clearance off-road vehicles.

  • Confirmed Areas & Landmarks: Pelican Valley, Gullet Marsh, Cottonmouth Lake, Sunrise RV Park (Kingfishers gang safe code 6769), Crab Lab boathouse (door code 0212, Crabshack VF5), Wetman Bay, and the backwoods Hamlet border.
  • Wildlife & Secrets: Verified predator pins for Alligator and Snake in our Animals Compendium. Also home to the confirmed Skunk Ape encounter around Cottonmouth Lake / Gullet Marsh (Secrets Guide).
  • Robberies & Gear: Rural convenience stores, gang-operation hideouts, 3 catalogued Ammu-Nation stops, and hidden Unique Weapon Variants (Swamp Bait Akulus & Cruel Huntsman's SM-69 in Wetman Bay).

WEST COAST · 23 MAP PINS

4. Port Gellhorn (Kelly County)

A gritty, working-class Gulf Coast city of motels, strip malls, pawn shops, and industrial docks that serves as Jason and Lucia's primary Chapter 2 base of operations.

  • Confirmed Locations & Fixed Safe Codes: Three corroborated Kolt & Associates gang-op safes—Kingpin Bowling (0300), Brooks Boatworks near Draper Island docks (6823), and La Cascara Motel (6969)—plus Gellhorn International Raceway and the Starlet Motel outskirts.
  • Fences & Early Cash: Moses at Watkins Auto Parts (buys WAiNK-scanned untracked stolen cars for $1,000–$2,000 after Moses) and Dunk at Dunk's Trailer on Tanglewood Lane (stolen goods fence after Slam Dunk).
  • Verified Map Pins: LPL Substation #6 (Port Gellhorn) and LPL Substation #10 (East Port Gellhorn), plus 6 catalogued properties/fences and 3 Ammu-Nation stores.

Leonida Regions Frequently Asked Questions

What are all 6 confirmed regions in GTA 6's State of Leonida?

The six confirmed primary regions are Vice City (east coast metro), the Leonida Keys (southern tropical archipelago), Grassrivers (southern interior wetlands), Port Gellhorn (western Gulf Coast city), Ambrosia (central agricultural and industrial heartland), and Mount Kalaga National Park (northern wilderness and highlands).

Which counties make up the State of Leonida?

Corroborated launch-game counties include Vice-Dale County (Vice City & Vice Beach), Kelly County (Port Gellhorn & western bays), Mariana County (Grassrivers & southern wetlands), Lummox County (Mount Kalaga National Park & Millport), and Leonard County (central rural territory).

How are the 6 Leonida regions connected on the map?

Vice City runs along the east coast and connects south via coastal highway bridges to the Leonida Keys. Grassrivers sits directly west and southwest of Vice City, separating the eastern metro from Port Gellhorn on the western coast. Ambrosia and Lake Leonida occupy the central inland basin, and Mount Kalaga National Park forms the elevated northern border.

Which region has the best early-game and endgame money routes?

Chapter 1 starts in the Leonida Keys (Lab Fees, Buck Me savings, and Kenton's underwater dives). Chapter 2 shifts to Port Gellhorn (Moses car drops, Dunk's fence, and 3 fixed Kolt & Associates gang safes). Endgame cash centers on Vice City via The Cartel Heist ($120,000), VCIA Takedown ($250,000), and Large Bank Vaults (up to ~$205,000). See our GTA 6 Heist Payouts & Money Guide for full figures.

Why were the old "escape canal coordinates" removed from this article?

Those entries came from pre-launch speculation before GTA 6's November 19, 2026 release. We removed unverified coordinate claims and fictional heist targets so this guide reflects only confirmed regions, landmarks, and verified map data.
