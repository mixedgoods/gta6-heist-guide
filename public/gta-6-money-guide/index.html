Cash, Contraband & "Buck Me" Family Savings Rules

In Chapter 1's "Buck Me" mission (Fleeca, Key Lento), Jason & Lucia use the BuckMe app's "Buck Yourself" transfer to lock Checking cash inside Family Savings (safe from Wasted/Busted loss) — separate from Chapter 1's later Point of No Return mission Tourist Trap (Save Planner).

CASH PROTECTION

1. "Buck Me": Checking → Family Savings

Open the BuckMe app on your phone → select Family Savings → trigger "Buck Yourself" to move Checking funds into locked Family Savings. Keep enough liquid cash in Checking for guns and upgrades, and handle Chapter 1's separate Tourist Trap cutoff with a dedicated manual save.

CASH & CONTRABAND

2. Contraband Fences & $20,000 Pocket Cap

Free-roam pocket cash caps at $20,000. Load contraband crates into 4-bag vehicle trunks, offload to Brian's Lockup (7700) or Yasmani (5% jewelry cut), and deposit clean cash regularly at physical ATMs.

KEYPAD CODES

3. Separate Lab Fees Doors (0420 vs. 7700)

0420 opens the Moonstone Boutique back-room laboratory keypad (acid pickup), while 7700 opens Brian's Lockup keypad (acid drop-off during Lab Fees).

SAVE DISCIPLINE

4. Lock Clean Saves on Slot #14

Never test money loops or cheat codes on your main story file. Keep Slot #14 clean for trophies and check live exploit status in our Glitch & Patch Tracker.
