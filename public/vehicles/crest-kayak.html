Frequently Asked Questions

Where can you find the Crest Kayak in GTA 6?

It spawns right at Jason's House dock in Key Lento (Leonida Keys) and along coastal shorelines.

Does the Crest Kayak have an engine?

No—it is human-powered, giving you silent water launches through shallow coves and mangroves.

Can two players ride the Crest Kayak?

No—it is a single-seat sit-on-top kayak with a bow dry-hatch compartment.
