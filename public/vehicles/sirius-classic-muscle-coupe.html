Heist Role, Trunk Strategy & Acquisition Tips

Two-Seat Duo Specialist

Built strictly for 2 seats with a rear trunk, the Sirius Classic Muscle Coupe is tailored for Jason-and-Lucia free-roam scores rather than four-person crew extractions.

Unverified Manufacturer Note

While the 'SIRIUS' decklid badge and Wyman's World appearance are confirmed in official Rockstar Press Kit imagery, the in-game parent manufacturer and store price remain unconfirmed.

  1. Watch salvage yards, garage showcases (Wyman's World), and rural street spawns across Port Gellhorn and Ambrosia.
  2. Treat the Sirius Classic Muscle Coupe as a dedicated two-seat duo car—perfect for Jason and Lucia runs that do not require extra crew seating.
  3. Feather the throttle on wet Port Gellhorn industrial pavement and Ambrosia dirt turnoffs to keep its rear-wheel-drive muscle chassis planted.
  4. Use your rear trunk to store heavy shotguns or rifles before entering Starlet Motel or Port Gellhorn pawn shop zones.
