Tactical Heist & Free-Roam Role

Deployed during elevated wanted levels across Vice City, Grassrivers, and Port Gellhorn, the VCPD Air Support Helicopter tracks fleeing vehicles across open highways and swamps. Break its vertical line of sight using Downtown parking decks, underground storm drains, or dense bridge underpasses.
