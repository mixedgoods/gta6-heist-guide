Frequently Asked Questions

Core intelligence regarding GTA 6 heists, weekly meta updates, and payout compliance.

How many heists are in GTA 6? +
GTA 6 features 2 major story heists (The Cartel Heist & VCIA Takedown), plus 8 smaller planned jobs (including Credit Check / Fleeca, Coast Guard Heist, and Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs) and 22 repeatable free-roam bank vaults (8 large and 14 small) across Leonida.
Can you replay heists in GTA 6? +
No mission replay is available at launch. To test alternate approaches or optimize cash cuts, make a manual save in a separate slot before Heist Planning and prior to each finale mission. Track your slots in our 15-Slot Manual Save Planner.
How much do The Cartel Heist and VCIA Takedown pay? +
The Cartel Heist: Sinfrontera National Bank pays $120,000–$150,000 (rig 3 cell towers for maximum vault time). VCIA Takedown: Vice City Airport Heist pays approximately $250,000 upon successful cargo plane escape.
What is the best repeatable heist for fast money in GTA 6? +
The 8 Large Bank Vaults are the best repeatable fast-money method, paying $65,000–$205,000 each run (unlocked after Ch. 4's That's Where The Money Is…). Lombank Downtown and Ocean Beach pay the highest returns.
How do you stop dye packs and launder marked bills in GTA 6? +
Always equip a Dye Pack Jammer, stash cash bags in your vehicle trunk to prevent bullet puncture, lose police heat, and fence marked bills to Yasmani in Little Cuba for the top payout rate (5% cut). Always deposit into a BuckMe ATM since loose pocket cash caps at $20,000.
What are the verified GTA 6 safe codes? +
Verified safe codes include Kingpin Bowling: 0300 and Pelican Valley: 0072. Additional safe codes can be decrypted throughout Leonida's 39 illegal gang operations and robbery hubs.
