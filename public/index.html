Can you replay story missions in GTA 6? +
No, GTA 6 does not have a mission replay menu at launch. Because the game relies on a single volatile autosave that automatically overwrites, you must use the 15 manual save slots to lock checkpoints before major decisions, points of no return (such as Chapter 1's Tourist Trap), and story heists. What is the code for Brian's lockup in GTA 6? +
The verified safe code for Brian's Lockup in GTA 6 is 7700. Enter this combination into the keypad lockbox to claim early-game cash, weaponry, and contraband without triggering police heat. Full combinations are cataloged in our Early Cash & Safe Codes Guide.
How many big heists are in GTA 6? +
GTA 6 features 2 major story heists: The Cartel Heist: Sinfrontera National Bank (an 8-mission operation in Chapter 3 paying $120,000–$150,000) and the VCIA Takedown: Vice City Airport Heist (Chapter 4 finale paying $250,000). Across Leonida's free-roam world, there are also 8 Big Banks (paying up to $205,000), 10 Banks, 16 Jewelry Stores, 99 Towable ATMs, and 40 Safes.
Does GTA 6 have 60 FPS or GTA 6 Online at launch? +
No 60 FPS performance mode or GTA 6 Online is live at launch. GTA 6 runs at a target 30 FPS on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S at launch. GTA 6 Online has no confirmed release date yet and is expected in 2027; it is not live at launch. How do you protect your money before Chapter 2 in GTA 6? +
Before triggering the Chapter 1 point of no return (Tourist Trap), players must deposit their $5,000 Family Savings goal into the BuckMe banking system. Any unbanked loose cash left in Jason or Lucia's pockets will be wiped when entering Chapter 2. Additionally, loose pocket cash is always capped at $20,000 during free-roam.
