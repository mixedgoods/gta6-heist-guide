Frequently Asked Questions

Verified launch answers for GTA 6 save slots, safe codes, story heists, frame rates, and cheats.

Can you replay story missions in GTA 6? +
No, GTA 6 does not have a mission replay menu at launch. Because the game relies on a single volatile autosave that automatically overwrites, you must use the 15 manual save slots to lock checkpoints before major decisions, points of no return (such as Chapter 1's Tourist Trap), and story heists. Track your slots in our 15-Slot Manual Save Planner.
What are the Lab Fees keypad codes (0420 vs. 7700)? +
Keep both Lab Fees doors separate: 0420 unlocks the Moonstone Boutique back-room laboratory keypad in Key Lento (acid pickup), while 7700 unlocks Brian's Lockup keypad further south in the Leonida Keys (acid drop-off). Browse all verified codes in our Money & Safe Codes Hub.
How many big heists are in GTA 6? +
GTA 6 features 2 major story heists: The Cartel Heist: Sinfrontera National Bank (Chapter 3 paying $120,000) and the VCIA Takedown: Vice City Airport Heist (Chapter 4 finale paying $250,000). Across Leonida's free-roam world, there are also 8 Big Banks (paying up to $205,000), 14 Small Banks, 20 Jewelry Stores across Leonida, 99 Towable ATMs, and 40 Safes.
Does GTA 6 have 60 FPS or GTA 6 Online at launch? +
No 60 FPS performance mode or GTA 6 Online is live at launch. GTA 6 runs at a target 30 FPS on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S at launch. GTA 6 Online: No official release date announced by Rockstar Games (some industry reports have speculated a 2027 window, which is unconfirmed speculation, not an official Rockstar announcement). To optimize your console setup, storage, HDR/VRR, and capture workflow, explore our GTA 6 Settings & Hardware Hub.
Do cheats disable trophies in GTA 6? +
Yes, activating cheats immediately disables trophies/achievements and saving for that session. To experiment with any of the published button or phone cheats without ruining your 100% completion progress, keep a dedicated manual save slot (e.g., Slot 15) as an isolated sandbox slot, and keep Slot 14 locked as your clean story file. Browse all codes in our Cheat Unlock Tracker.
How does "Buck Me" (Checking → Family Savings via Buck Yourself) work? +
During the early Chapter 1 mission "Buck Me" at Fleeca in Key Lento, Jason and Lucia install the BuckMe fintech app and use "Buck Yourself" to move funds from Checking → Family Savings (permanently locked and safe from Wasted/Busted loss). Keep Buck Me separate from the later Chapter 1 Point of No Return mission Tourist Trap, which you can track in our 15-Slot Manual Save Planner.
