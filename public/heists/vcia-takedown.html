⚡ RECOMMENDED HEIST SETUP & FUEL

Mandatory Setups & Prep Checklist

  • Close Surveillance: Scout VCIA cargo terminals, security camera cones, and airport patrol shift changes to identify blind spots.
  • Delivery Method: Secure your airport-disguised infiltration transport or cargo ramp access vehicle.
  • Team Building: Finalize your heist crew roster and assign tarmac suppression vs. vault breach responsibilities.
  • Heist Plans in Place: Lock in your stealth vs. loud entry plan and secondary runway getaway staging before launching the finale.

Step-by-Step Tactical Walkthrough

1. Lock a Manual Save Slot Before Starting

GTA 6 has no mission replay menu at launch. Save in a dedicated manual slot (Slot 8–10) before launching VCIA Takedown so you can replay both stealth and loud approaches.

2. Infiltrate the VCIA Terminal Perimeter

Use your Close Surveillance blind spots to slip past the outer airport gate. Suppress exterior cameras before crossing the apron.

3. Breach the Airport Cargo Vault

Coordinate Jason and Lucia to bypass the terminal keypad and secure the high-value international currency pallets.

4. Hold the Tarmac Against VCPD & Airport Security

Once the alarm triggers, move between luggage tugs and cargo containers for hard cover. Prioritize patrol choppers and armored SUVs.

5. Execute the Runway Extraction

Fight to your pre-staged Delivery Method getaway craft/vehicle and clear the VCIA perimeter via the coastal flight path or southern highway tunnel.

6. Clear Heat & Bank the Payout

Swap vehicles outside the airport search zone, drop your wanted stars, and deposit your cash at the nearest physical ATM to avoid the $20,000 pocket cash cap.

🤝 Jason & Lucia Duo Tactics

Use Lucia for silent terminal camera takedowns and terminal keypad overrides while Jason anchors heavy cover on the cargo ramp with an armor-piercing rifle for incoming airport security trucks.

🔇 Stealth vs. Loud Tradeoffs

Stealth roof/service infiltration gives you extra uninterrupted looting time inside the cargo vault and delays the 4-star tarmac response. Going loud upfront triggers immediate airport lockdown and armored VCPD barricades on the runway.

🏧 Fencing & Physical ATM Banking

Split loose bills from marked contraband. Fence any high-value cargo items at Brian's Lockup (Code 7700) or Yasmani's, and deposit all clean cash at physical ATMs right after losing heat.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the setup missions for VCIA Takedown in GTA 6?

The 4 setup missions are Close Surveillance, Delivery Method, Team Building, and Heist Plans in Place.

What does completing VCIA Takedown unlock?

Completing Chapter 4's VCIA Takedown progresses you toward 'That's Where The Money Is...', unlocking the 8 repeatable Large Bank Vaults across Leonida.

Is stealth better than loud for VCIA Takedown?

Yes—entering stealthily buys critical minutes inside the VCIA vault before the 4-star tarmac response spawns.
