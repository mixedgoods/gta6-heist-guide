GTA 6 Armored Trucks & Gang Ops Guide (Mobile Cash + 40 Gang Hideouts)

⚡ QUICK-ANSWER WALKTHROUGH SUMMARY

Armored Trucks & Gang Ops are repeatable free-roam combat scores across Leonida, combining roaming Gruppe-style cash trucks with 40 fortified Gang Hideouts. Intercept armored trucks away from downtown VCPD precincts, blow the rear doors cleanly without destroying the truck, loot gang contraband stashes, fence marked goods at Brian's Lockup (7700), and bank your wallet cash at physical ATMs.

Mandatory Setups & Prep Checklist

  • Carry Sticky Bombs or Heavy Shotgun: Needed to pop armored truck rear doors fast without detonating the entire vehicle.
  • Use an Armored SUV or Fast Coupe: Gives you cover during gang hideout firefights and quick off-road escape in Grassrivers or Port Gellhorn.

Step-by-Step Tactical Walkthrough

1. Ambush Armored Trucks in Low-Traffic Zones

Wait until a roaming cash truck enters a highway shoulder, industrial zone, or Port Gellhorn backroad before boxing it in.

2. Blow the Rear Doors Cleanly

Place a single sticky bomb directly on the rear lock seam (never on the cab or fuel tank) to pop the doors and spill the cash cases.

3. Clear 40 Gang Hideouts for Contraband & Cash

Approach gang compounds from high ground or flanking cover. Clear lookouts first to prevent reinforcements, then sweep the stash room for cash boxes and contraband.

4. Load Contraband & Break Pursuit

Grab both cash and fenceable contraband crates before VCPD or rival gang backup arrives.

5. Fence Contraband & Deposit Wallet Cash at ATMs

Sell drugs, jewelry, and marked bills at Brian's Lockup (Code 7700), then deposit all clean wallet cash at physical ATMs.

🤝 Jason & Lucia Duo Tactics

For armored trucks, one protagonist blocks the cab and suppresses the guards while the other plants the rear-door charge and bags the cash cases. In gang hideouts, crossfire from opposite flanks to flush enemies out of cover.

🔇 Stealth vs. Loud Tradeoffs

Gang hideouts reward stealth takedowns on outer sentries before going loud on the main stash house. Armored trucks are inherently loud—speed of breach and immediate off-road escape are everything.

🏧 Fencing & Physical ATM Banking

Gang hideouts drop a mix of clean bills and illegal contraband. Always swing by Brian's Lockup (Code 7700) to liquidate contraband, then hit a physical ATM before starting your next op.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many gang hideouts are in GTA 6?

There are 40 gang hideouts scattered across Leonida's regions that reward cash, weapons, and fenceable contraband.

How do you open armored trucks in GTA 6 without blowing them up?

Place a single sticky bomb directly on the rear lock seam (or shoot the rear door lock with high-caliber rounds) rather than using grenades or RPGs.
