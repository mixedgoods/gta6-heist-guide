The Cartel Heist Walkthrough (Chapter 3 — Story Heist #1)

⚡ QUICK-ANSWER WALKTHROUGH SUMMARY

The Cartel Heist (Mission #71) is GTA 6's first major Story Heist in Chapter 3, given by Raul Bautista against Sinfrontera National Bank for a $120,000 payout. Complete all 5 setups (Security Expert, Heist Planning, Ride or Die, Safe Word, Hidden Charges), rig the cell towers to buy ~11 minutes before VCPD arrives, shoot guns out of guards' hands in the lobby for Professional rating, bag the big stacks of bills first, and defend the bus to the freeway escape.

Mandatory Setups & Prep Checklist

  • Security Expert: Recruit and brief your bank security insider so you get floor layouts and silent alarm bypass timings.
  • Heist Planning: Lock in your entry approach, vault floor roles for Jason & Lucia, and bus extraction corridor with Raul Bautista.
  • Ride or Die: Acquire and stage the heavy getaway transport and backup freeway intercept vehicles.
  • Safe Word: Secure the vault override credentials and comms jammer triggers needed for the Sinfrontera lower level.
  • Hidden Charges: Plant remote explosives on the surrounding cell towers so you can trigger the ~11-minute police comms blackout.

Step-by-Step Tactical Walkthrough

1. Blow the Cell Towers (~11 Min Timer)

Trigger the pre-planted charges from Hidden Charges to knock out local cell towers. This starts your ~11-minute blackout window before VCPD dispatch can coordinate a full perimeter.

2. Take the Lobby (Keep Professional Rating)

Enter Sinfrontera National Bank fast. To preserve your Professional rating and avoid lethal civilian escalation, shoot weapons directly out of the security guards' hands rather than executing them.

3. Push Down to the Vault Floor

Move Jason and Lucia down the stairwell to the basement vault corridor. Watch the corner sightlines and neutralize the armored vault guard quickly.

4. Clear Building & Roof

Sweep the upper mezzanine and roof access to stop snipers and security spotters from calling in early helicopter support.

5. Crack the Vault (Bag Big Stacks First)

Breach the main Sinfrontera vault door. Always prioritize the large high-denomination stacks of bills on the center tables first before grabbing loose bundles to maximize your $120,000 take.

6. Hold Off Police

When VCPD breaches the front doors, hold the lobby choke points using cover. Switch between Jason (heavy rifle suppression) and Lucia (flanking & revive cover).

7. Smoke Out the Street

Deploy smoke canisters across the main bank steps to blind VCPD cruisers and break sniper line-of-sight before stepping onto the asphalt.

8. Get Nicole Out

Escort Nicole through the smoke screen toward the extraction vehicle. Keep her shielded on the far side of parked police cruisers.

9. Defend the Bus

Board the armored extraction bus and man the firing ports. Focus fire on police cruiser tires and engine blocks to prevent PIT maneuvers.

10. Escape on the Freeway

Hit the Vice City freeway interchange, ditch the tailing interceptors using overpass exits, and cool down your wanted stars to bank your $120,000 payout.

🤝 Jason & Lucia Duo Tactics

Keep Jason on primary vault-corridor suppression and heavy rifle fire while Lucia handles precision hand-shots in the lobby, rapid bag-filling, and flanking VCPD breach teams.

🔇 Stealth vs. Loud Tradeoffs

Shooting guns out of guards' hands in Step 2 preserves the Professional rating and delays heavy SWAT escalation. Going loud too early burns your ~11-minute cell tower timer before the big stacks are bagged.

🏧 Fencing & Physical ATM Banking

After escaping on the freeway, deposit your $120,000 share at a physical ATM or BuckMe safehouse terminal immediately. Remember loose pocket cash is capped at $20,000 in free-roam—never carry unbanked heist cash into side missions.

During Step 2 (Take the Lobby), shoot the guns directly out of the security guards' hands instead of killing guards or harming hostages.
