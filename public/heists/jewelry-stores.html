GTA 6 Jewelry Stores Robbery Guide (20 Locations — Unlocks Ch. 3)

⚡ QUICK-ANSWER: PAYOUT RANGE, UNLOCK & FENCE

Jewelry Store robberies (20 locations) unlock in Chapter 3 after Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs. Post-launch guides report roughly $10,000–$25,000 per standard store—note that this is a reported, variable range (not a guaranteed flat payout) depending on boutique tier, display-case haul, randomized back-office safe contents, and fencing rates. Delivering your first job to Yasmani unlocks the Snap Gun for silent back-door entry (ideal for the Sneakthief achievement). Sell bagged gems directly to Yasmani in Little Cuba; getting wasted or busted before fencing forfeits all bagged loot. See our Safe Codes Database and Money Guide.

Mandatory Setups & Prep Checklist

  • Complete 'Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs': Unlocks full jewelry store heist mechanics and establishes Yasmani as a top-tier low-cut gem fence.
  • Bring Glass Cutters / Silenced Sidearm: Keep a suppressed pistol to shoot out interior security cameras without alerting the street.

Step-by-Step Tactical Walkthrough

1. Stealth Entry & Camera Sweep

Enter during low-foot-traffic hours (late evening) and disable or avoid interior CCTV cones before drawing weapons.

2. Pacify Security & Staff

Neutralize the boutique guard quietly and secure the clerk so the silent alarm isn't tripped.

3. Crack the Back-Office Safe Combo

Search the manager's desk/clipboard for the safe combination to claim loose diamonds and high-carat watches.

4. Sweep Display Cases Into Duffel Bags

Fill both Jason's and Lucia's duffel bags with the highest-tier diamond cases first.

5. Erase the CCTV Server

Before exiting, interact with the back-room security terminal to wipe CCTV footage—this prevents delayed VCPD heat and vehicle plate flags.

6. Transport Duffel Bags to Fence & Bank

Load duffel bags cleanly into your getaway car, lose any pursuit, sell the jewels at Yasmani's or Brian's Lockup (7700), and deposit the cash at an ATM.

🤝 Jason & Lucia Duo Tactics

Split the floor: Lucia clears the back-room safe combo and wipes the CCTV terminal while Jason controls the showroom floor and bags the front diamond displays.

🔇 Stealth vs. Loud Tradeoffs

Stealth + CCTV erasure is vastly superior—it lets you walk out with 100% of the display cases and safe diamonds before VCPD even rolls a unit.

🏧 Fencing & Physical ATM Banking

Always transport duffel bags straight to Yasmani (lowest cut for gems) or Brian's Lockup (Code 7700). Deposit payouts at physical ATMs immediately so you stay under the $20,000 pocket cash cap.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many jewelry stores can you rob in GTA 6 and how much do they pay?

There are 20 robbable jewelry store locations across Leonida (part of the 35 Smash 'n' Grabs), unlocked in Chapter 3 after completing Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs. Corroborated post-launch guides report $10,000–$25,000 per store depending on security tier—sell stolen gems directly to Yasmani in Little Cuba for top rates. Compare with Pawn Shops or check our Fences Database.

How do you unlock the Snap Gun and Sneakthief achievement?

Delivering your first item during Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs rewards the Snap Gun for quietly popping locked back/side doors. For the Sneakthief trophy/achievement, enter through a back or side door, disable cameras, crack the safe, and exit undetected without alerting police.

Why should you erase CCTV footage during jewelry store robberies?

Wiping the back-room CCTV server stops VCPD from tracking your getaway plates and lowers post-heist heat. Note that free-roam boutique safe codes are randomized—check our Safe Codes Database for intimidation and Focus Mode (L3+R3) rules.
