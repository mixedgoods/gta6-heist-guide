⚡ RECOMMENDED HEIST SETUP & FUEL

Mandatory Setups & Prep Checklist

  • Stage a Fast Escape Car: Park facing the road with the driver door clear—never trap yourself in a tight gas station pump bay.
  • Equip a Mask & Switch Plates: Put on a bandana or mask before entering so witnesses don't tag your identity on VCPD dispatch.

Step-by-Step Tactical Walkthrough

1. Scout for Cameras & Armed Clerks

Glance above the counter for a CCTV dome and check if any customers are holding phones before drawing your weapon.

2. Intimidate Without Shooting

Aim at the clerk and use intimidation commands to speed up register emptying. Firing shots escalates from 1-star to 2-star VCPD response instantly.

3. Duo Crowd Control

Have your partner watch the entrance and keep patrons down while you empty the cash drawer and check the back office for a second safe box.

4. Clean Exit & Line-of-Sight Break

Exit immediately, duck into side alleys or Port Gellhorn backroads, and remove your mask once out of police sight to drop a star.

5. Hit Physical ATMs Every 2–3 Stores

Bank your wallet cash at physical ATMs regularly—especially in Chapter 1 so you secure the $5,000 BuckMe Family Savings target before Tourist Trap.

🤝 Jason & Lucia Duo Tactics

One protagonist intimidates the clerk and bags the till while the second protagonist watches the door, deters heroic armed customers, and calls out incoming VCPD cruisers.

🔇 Stealth vs. Loud Tradeoffs

Silent intimidation (no gunfire) keeps police response minimal and lets you chain 3–4 nearby shops in Port Gellhorn or Vice City suburbs before laying low.

🏧 Fencing & Physical ATM Banking

Register cash is clean wallet cash—deposit it directly at physical ATMs or safehouse BuckMe terminals. Keep loose cash well below the $20,000 pocket cap.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many shops can you rob in GTA 6?

There are 72 robbable convenience stores, gas stations, and fast-food spots across Leonida, available right from the start of Chapter 1.

Do masks actually help during store robberies in GTA 6?

Yes—wearing a mask prevents instant identity recognition, and removing it after breaking line-of-sight speeds up wanted-level cooldown.
