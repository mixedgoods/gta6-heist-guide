GTA 6 Pawn Shops Robbery Guide (Unlocks Ch. 2 After Coffee Break)

⚡ QUICK-ANSWER WALKTHROUGH SUMMARY

Pawn Shop robberies (15 locations) unlock in Chapter 2 after Coffee Break as part of Leonida's Smash 'n' Grab rotation. Clearing registers, display cases, and back-room safes typically nets $2,500–$6,500 fenced (with corroborated reports up to $10,000–$12,000 on full safe clears). Sell bagged valuables to Dunk in Port Gellhorn (Chapter 2) or Yasmani in Little Cuba (Chapter 3).

Looking for the Fast Cash safe code? Fast Cash is a Little Cuba (Vice City) pawn shop whose safe code is randomized on every visit—there is no universal fixed PIN. Aim at the clerk and press the threat prompt (Square / X) until "Safe Code?" appears, or use Focus Mode (L3+R3) in the back office. See our Complete GTA 6 Safe Codes Database →

Mandatory Setups & Prep Checklist

  • Complete 'Coffee Break' (Ch. 2): Finish this Chapter 2 story mission to unlock pawn shop interior robbery prompts and fencing values.
  • Bring a Trunk-Equipped Car: Use a 4-door sedan or SUV with trunk storage so you can stash extra duffel bags and swap weapons quickly.

Step-by-Step Tactical Walkthrough

1. Disable the Under-Counter Alarm First

Pawn shop owners frequently reach for a silent panic button. Control the clerk immediately upon entry before smashing any glass cases.

2. Prioritize High-Value Cases

Target luxury watch trays, gold chains, and locked firearm displays first—they give the highest dollar-per-slot value in your duffel bag.

3. Crack the Back-Room Safe (Fast Cash & All 15 Shops)

Pawn shops—including Fast Cash (Little Cuba, Vice City), Hamlet Pawn, and Avery Pawn Shop—have a randomized back-room safe combination on every visit. Aim at the employee and press the threat prompt (Square on PS5 / X on Xbox) until "Safe Code?" appears, or activate Focus Mode (L3+R3) in the back office to spot sticky notes and desk clues. See our Safe Codes Database for fixed vs. randomized rules.

4. Escape & Avoid Roadblocks

Stash your duffel bag in the getaway car trunk if needed and use alleyways to break 2-star pursuit. Remember: getting wasted or busted before reaching a fence forfeits bagged loot.

5. Fence Stolen Goods & Bank the Cash

Drive stolen pawn goods to an active stolen-goods fence—Dunk at Dunk's Trailer in Port Gellhorn (Chapter 2) or Yasmani in Little Cuba (Chapter 3, better rates on jewelry)—then deposit clean cash at a physical ATM. See our GTA 6 Money Guide and Fences Database.

🤝 Jason & Lucia Duo Tactics

Assign one protagonist strictly to clerk/alarm control and door watch while the other systematically clears the highest-value glass cases and back-room office.

🔇 Stealth vs. Loud Tradeoffs

Controlled smash-and-grab without shooting keeps heat at 2 stars. Firing shotguns or explosives inside triggers immediate VCPD tactical units.

🏧 Fencing & Physical ATM Banking

Stolen pawn valuables cannot be deposited at ATMs directly—you must sell them to one of Leonida's 7 fences first, then deposit the fence payout at a physical ATM.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do Pawn Shop robberies unlock in GTA 6?

All 15 robbable pawn shops (including Fast Cash in Little Cuba, Hamlet Pawn, and Avery Pawn Shop) unlock for Smash 'n' Grab robberies in Chapter 2 after completing the story mission Coffee Break.

What is the Fast Cash pawn shop safe code in GTA 6?

Fast Cash (Little Cuba, Vice City) does not have a universal fixed safe code—its back-room safe combination is randomized per playthrough and per visit. Aim at the employee and press the threat prompt (Square / X) until "Safe Code?" appears, or scan the back office with Focus Mode (L3+R3). Full rules are in our Safe Codes Database.

How much do Pawn Shop robberies pay and where do you fence stolen items?

Typical hauls average $2,500–$6,500 fenced, with higher-tier locations and full back-room safe clears reported up to $10,000–$12,000. Sell stolen watches, jewelry, and electronics to Dunk in Port Gellhorn (Chapter 2) or Yasmani in Little Cuba (Chapter 3). Note: Randy is a Wish List vehicle buyer, not a pawn-goods fence.
