There are 8 major bank vaults across Leonida paying up to ~$205,000 per score. Wipe the CCTV room, choose silent thermal drilling over explosives to buy extra vault time, and stage a trunk-equipped disguise swap car two blocks away.

⚡ RECOMMENDED HEIST SETUP & FUEL

Mandatory Setups & Prep Checklist

  • Complete 'That's Where The Money Is...': Unlocks all 8 repeatable Large Bank Vault targets across Vice City, Port Gellhorn, and Ambrosia.
  • Stage a Clean Swap Vehicle: Park a second clean vehicle with disguises in the trunk 2–3 blocks away in an underground garage or alley.
  • Bring Dye Pack Jammer + Thermal/Drill Kit: Pack both your Dye Pack Jammer and heavy vault breach tools in your primary car trunk.

Step-by-Step Tactical Walkthrough

1. Recon Guard Rotations & CCTV Room

Identify the rear security office first—wiping CCTV prevents VCPD from locking onto your getaway vehicle description.

2. Choose Stealth Drill vs. Explosives

Silent thermal drilling takes longer to open the vault door but keeps SWAT off your back for precious minutes. Explosives blow the door instantly but trigger an immediate 4–5 star perimeter lockdown.

3. Deploy Dye Pack Jammer & Clear High-Value Stacks

Once inside the main vault, trigger your Dye Pack Jammer and prioritize gold/bearer-bond trays and high-denomination cash pallets to approach the ~$205,000 cap.

4. Wipe CCTV Before Extraction

Ensure the security room server is wiped so your identity and initial getaway car aren't permanently flagged.

5. Execute the Disguise & Trunk Swap

Break initial line-of-sight using tunnels or storm drains, pull into your staged parking garage, swap outfits from the trunk, and switch into your clean getaway car.

6. Bank Your $205K Score Immediately

Hit physical ATMs or a safehouse terminal right after dropping your stars—never roam with $205K exposed above the $20,000 loose pocket cash cap.

🤝 Jason & Lucia Duo Tactics

Coordinate tightly: Jason brings heavy armor and holds the vault foyer choke point against SWAT breachers while Lucia operates the thermal drill, runs the Dye Pack Jammer, and empties the highest-value vault trays.

🔇 Stealth vs. Loud Tradeoffs

Stealth drilling + CCTV wipe maximizes net payout (~$205,000) with minimal armor cost. Explosives are faster to breach but immediately summon 4–5 star SWAT vans and police helicopters.

🏧 Fencing & Physical ATM Banking

Deposit clean bills at physical ATMs immediately and fence any marked bills, bonds, or bullion at Brian's Lockup (7700) or Yasmani's.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do Large Bank Vaults pay in GTA 6?

The 8 repeatable Large Bank Vaults pay up to ~$205,000 per successful run when you clear the top-tier stacks with a Dye Pack Jammer.

When do Large Bank Vaults unlock in GTA 6?

They unlock at the end of Chapter 4 after completing the story mission 'That's Where The Money Is...'.

Is stealth or explosives better for Large Bank Vaults?

Stealth thermal drilling paired with a CCTV wipe gives you much more time inside the vault before SWAT arrives, making it easier to hit the full ~$205,000 payout.
