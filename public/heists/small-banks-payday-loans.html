GTA 6 Small Banks & Payday Loans Guide (14 Locations + Dye Pack Jammer)

The single most important tool for these hits is the Dye Pack Jammer—activate it before bagging teller stacks and mini-vault bundles so exploding dye packs don't ruin your stolen cash.

Mandatory Setups & Prep Checklist

  • Complete 'Credit Check' (Ch. 3): Unlocks all 14 Small Banks & Payday Loan robbery nodes and introduces the Dye Pack Jammer mechanic.
  • Equip the Dye Pack Jammer: Verify your Dye Pack Jammer is in your active heist loadout before stepping through the bank doors—without it, up to 50% of your cash can be ruined by dye packs.

Step-by-Step Tactical Walkthrough

1. Park a High-Torque Getaway Vehicle Outside

Small banks trigger a fast 3-star VCPD response. Face your car toward the nearest highway on-ramp or alleyway.

2. Control Tellers & Lobby Security

Neutralize the lone branch guard quickly and keep tellers away from the under-desk silent alarm.

3. Activate Your Dye Pack Jammer

Trigger the Dye Pack Jammer before grabbing teller drawer stacks or mini-vault bundles. Keep it active until every stack is safely inside your duffel bag.

4. Crack the Branch Mini-Vault

Drill or override the rear cash room door while your partner holds the lobby glass against early VCPD arrivals.

5. Break 3-Star Pursuit & Deposit at ATMs

Break sightlines via alleys or storm drains, swap vehicles if your plates were scanned, and deposit your clean cash at physical ATMs.

🤝 Jason & Lucia Duo Tactics

Jason holds the front lobby and suppresses arriving VCPD cruisers while Lucia runs the Dye Pack Jammer, drills the branch mini-vault, and bags the clean bills.

🔇 Stealth vs. Loud Tradeoffs

Silent entry delays the 3-star countdown long enough to empty both teller drawers and the rear mini-vault cleanly. Loud entry forces you to fight VCPD inside the lobby while still bagging.

🏧 Fencing & Physical ATM Banking

Clean jammed bills can be deposited straight into physical ATMs. If any bundle gets ink-stained because the jammer lapsed, take those marked bills to Brian's Lockup (Code 7700) to fence at a discount.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you stop dye packs from ruining cash in GTA 6 small banks?

Unlock and equip the Dye Pack Jammer (introduced after Chapter 3's Credit Check) and activate it before bagging teller and vault stacks.

How many small banks are in GTA 6?

There are 14 small banks and payday loan locations across Leonida.
