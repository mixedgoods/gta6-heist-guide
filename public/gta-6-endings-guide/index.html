⚡ DIRECT ANSWER: HOW TO GET THE BEST ENDING IN GTA 6 (“TWO YEARS LATER”) VERIFIED TITLE CARD: “TWO YEARS LATER”

Unlike GTA V where a single prompt at the end decided the outcome, Grand Theft Auto VI evaluates three cumulative playthrough trackers alongside your final decision on the Leonida Keys bridge during “The End of the Road” (plus an optional pre-finale deal exit in “Protective Custody”, creating 5 documented endings). To unlock the verified best ending—titled “Two Years Later”—you must satisfy all four conditions below before entering the Chapter 5 finale:

1. Final Bridge Decision
Choose “Swim for It”

Press Triangle (PS5) / Y (Xbox) at the Leonida Keys bridge roadblock and remain unarmed. Never pick “No Surrender”.

2. Relationship Status
4/4 “Hot Together”

Max out Jason & Lucia's shared bond meter (Burning Heart icon). Levels 1–3 lock you into the prison-visit epilogue.

3. Criminal Profiles
Professional / Hardened

Both Jason and Lucia must stay out of Psycho and never cross into the permanent Irredeemable tier.

4. BuckMe! Family Savings
$2,000,000 Deposited

Fund the shared Family Savings account to its $2,000,000 cap (grows to ~$2.5M with interest for lawyer Abe).

Every Documented GTA 6 Ending & Consequence Explained

Click any ending card below to expand or collapse its exact cutscene sequence, character fate, and epilogue consequences.

Trigger Conditions: Reach the Leonida Keys bridge blockade at the climax of “The End of the Road” and select “No Surrender” (Square on PS5 / X on Xbox) with less than $2,000,000 stored in your BuckMe! Family Savings account. Your Relationship tier and Criminal Profile do not prevent this outcome once No Surrender is pressed.

What Happens & Consequences: Refusing to be separated or taken alive, Jason and Lucia share a final kiss inside the cab. Jason floors the accelerator and rams headlong into the police roadblock while control switches to Lucia, who leans out the window firing back at the swarming VCPD and state troopers. The shootout is unwinnable: heavy police fire riddles the vehicle, and both Jason and Lucia die side-by-side holding hands in the front seat.

Epilogue Impact: After the credits roll, the epilogue shows their young son, Tomás, being raised safely in modest surroundings by Lucia's relatives (her mother Mia and extended family), knowing his parents only through a framed photograph on the dresser.

Why Sources Document Two Variants Here: Major gameplay guides classify the fifth slot in two complementary ways—either as the $2,000,000+ Family Savings epilogue variation of “No Surrender” or as the pre-finale Protective Custody / Mendez's Deal compromise that skips the Leonida Keys bridge entirely. Here are the verified facts for both:

  • “No Surrender” with $2,000,000+ in BuckMe! Family Savings: Jason and Lucia still perish together in the bridge barricade shootout. However, because money locked in the BuckMe! Family Savings account is legally ring-fenced for Tomás and immune to police asset forfeiture, the epilogue changes: the $2,000,000+ nest egg transfers to Lucia's relatives, enabling them to move Tomás out of Leonida into a secure, upscale home with his financial future guaranteed.
  • The Pre-Finale Deal Branch (Protective Custody / The Tower): If you accept the authorities' compromise deal during the Chapter 5 setup sequence prior to The End of the Road, you bypass the Leonida Keys bridge blockade completely, triggering an alternate standoff and truncated conclusion that locks you out of the Two Years Later family reunion.

Trigger Conditions: Select “Swim for It” (Triangle on PS5 / Y on Xbox) on the bridge while either protagonist has a Psycho or Irredeemable Criminal Profile, while your relationship sits at 1/4 Running on Empty with chaotic behavior, or if Jason exits the car holding a firearm.

What Happens & Consequences: Even though you picked Swim for It, your violent Criminal Profile or drawn sidearm ruins the deception. Jason steps onto the bridge asphalt with a pistol in his hand, and the blockade officers immediately open fire—killing Jason beside the door and shooting Lucia inside the car before she can climb over the bridge rail.

Why This Catches Players Off Guard: Many players assume picking Swim for It automatically saves Lucia. If either character's Criminal Profile has permanently fractured into Irredeemable (or sits at Psycho entering the finale), law enforcement refuses to accept a surrender and triggers this fatal outcome regardless of how much money is in Family Savings.

Trigger Conditions: Select “Swim for It” (Unarmed) with a controlled Criminal Profile (Professional, Hardened, or Violent—below Psycho), but with less than $2,000,000 in BuckMe! Family Savings or a Relationship tier below 4/4 (Levels 1–3: Running on Empty, Partners in Crime, or Crazy in Love).

What Happens & Consequences: Recognizing the blockade trap, Jason tells Lucia to keep her weapon holstered. Both step out with their hands raised, and Jason positions himself to draw police attention while Lucia slips over the bridge railing, dives into the Leonida Keys channel with the hard drive, and escapes cleanly. Jason surrenders on the asphalt and takes the full legal prosecution so Lucia can go free.

Epilogue Impact: Because the couple did not lock the full $2,000,000 Family Savings fund (or lacked the 4/4 Hot Together commitment), their lawyer Abe cannot broker an early release. The epilogue shows Lucia living free in Liberty City raising Tomás on her own, concluding with a bittersweet visitation scene where Lucia and Tomás speak to an orange-jumpsuit-clad Jason through prison glass.

Verified Name & Trigger Conditions: Known across the community by its on-screen epilogue title card, “Two Years Later” (also called the Best Ending or Happily Ever After). Requires all four verified milestones:

  1. Decision: Choose “Swim for It” (Triangle / Y) and ensure Jason steps out unarmed.
  2. Relationship: Maxed at 4/4 “Hot Together” (Burning Heart icon).
  3. Criminal Profile: Both Jason and Lucia at Professional or Hardened (clean record; never Psycho or Irredeemable).
  4. Family Savings: Full $2,000,000 cap deposited into the shared BuckMe! Family Savings account before the Chapter 5 finale point of no return.

What Happens & Consequences: As in the unarmed Swim for It sequence, Jason shields Lucia on the Leonida Keys bridge so she can dive into the water with the hard drive while Jason peacefully surrenders to police custody. Instead of ending at the prison visitation glass, the screen cuts to the “Two Years Later” title card at a roadside diner.

The “Two Years Later” Epilogue Payoff: Lawyer Abe meets with Lucia and confirms that the protected $2,000,000 BuckMe! Family Savings fund (which has accrued interest to $2,500,000 over the two-year gap) covered the legal defense, restitution, and bail terms—coupled with Jason's disciplined Criminal Profile—to secure Jason's release. Jason walks out of prison a free man, embraces Lucia and their son Tomás, and the family drives north out of Leonida together to start a clean life in Liberty City.

CHAPTER 5 FINALE WALKTHROUGH

The Final Mission: “The End of the Road” & Point of No Return

Chapter 5 (“Top of the World, Ma”) is a tight 7-mission endgame sprint across Vice City and the Leonida Keys. Once you trigger the legal summit sequence around “Meet Abe” and “Protective Custody”, free-roam ATM access, side robberies, and relationship dates are locked out until the credits roll.

1. Pre-Finale Cutoff (Meet Abe / Protective Custody)

Before stepping into lawyer Abe's marker in Chapter 5, open your phone or visit a physical ATM to verify your BuckMe! Family Savings shows the full $2,000,000 cap and hold D-Pad Right to confirm 4/4 Hot Together. Save manually in Slot 13.

2. Escape South to the Leonida Keys Bridge

During “The End of the Road”, Jason and Lucia flee south carrying the hard drive while state helicopters and tactical units close the highway corridors, trapping your vehicle at a full bridge barricade in the Leonida Keys.

3. The Split-Second Bridge Choice & Unarmed Rule

When the prompt appears on the bridge, press Triangle (PS5) / Y (Xbox) for “Swim for It”. Do not press weapon-draw or fire triggers during the exit transition so Jason steps out with empty hands raised.

Ending Requirements Deep Dive: Relationship, Criminal Profile & $2,000,000 Savings

REQUIREMENT 1 • SHARED BOND

Jason & Lucia Relationship: 4/4 “Hot Together”

Jason and Lucia share a four-tier romance meter checked by holding D-Pad Right or viewing the Jason & Lucia pause tab:

  • 1/4 Running on Empty (Gray Broken Heart): Cold, strictly platonic crime partners. High risk of botched bridge coordination.
  • 2/4 Partners in Crime / Not So Hot (Pink/Gray Broken Heart): Neutral default if you skip Chapter 1 errands. Locks out the Two Years Later reunion.
  • 3/4 Crazy in Love (Pink Heart): Affectionate bond, but still results in Ending 4 (Prison Visitation) if not pushed to 4/4.
  • 4/4 Hot Together (Burning Heart): Mandatory for the Two Years Later best ending.

REQUIREMENT 2 • BOTH CHARACTERS

Criminal Profile Requirements (Jason & Lucia)

Unlike the shared relationship heart, Jason and Lucia have independent 5-tier Criminal Profiles tracking how restrained or unhinged they act during crimes:

  • 1. Professional (Yellow Icon): Clean, stealthy, non-lethal disarms. Qualifies for Best Ending.
  • 2. Hardened (Orange Icon): Default starting state. Qualifies for Best Ending.
  • 3. Violent (Purple Icon): Borderline. Rehabilitate back to Hardened before Chapter 5 to guarantee lawyer Abe's release deal.
  • 4. Psycho (Light Gray Icon): Police shoot on sight on the bridge (Ending 3). Still reversible if you disarm guards and avoid civilian kills before Chapter 5.
  • 5. Irredeemable (Dark Gray Shattered Icon): Permanent point of no return. Cannot be reversed and permanently locks out the best ending.

REQUIREMENT 3 • BUCKME! APP

The Verified $2,000,000 Family Savings Cap

Introduced during Chapter 1's “Buck Me” mission at Fleeca in Key Lento, the BuckMe! app features a shared Family Savings account via the “Buck Yourself” transfer tool:

  • Why $2,000,000 (and why sources also cite $2,500,000): The player-deposited cap in BuckMe! Family Savings is $2,000,000. During the two-year time skip, accrued interest raises the payout to $2,500,000 when lawyer Abe settles Jason's release.
  • Pocket Cash & Checking Do NOT Count: Loose pocket cash (capped at $20,000) and personal Checking accounts are frozen/seized when Jason is arrested on the bridge.
  • Fastest Way to Hit $2,000,000: Combine The Cartel Heist ($120K), VCIA Takedown ($250K), the 8 Large Bank Vaults (up to $205K each), and 20 Jewelry Stores.

Frequently Asked Questions — GTA 6 Endings

How do you get the best ending in GTA 6 (Two Years Later)?

To unlock the “Two Years Later” best ending in GTA 6, reach 4/4 Hot Together relationship status, keep both Jason and Lucia at a Professional or Hardened Criminal Profile, deposit the full $2,000,000 cap into the shared BuckMe! Family Savings account before the Chapter 5 point of no return, and choose “Swim for It” (unarmed) at the Leonida Keys bridge roadblock during “The End of the Road”.

Does pocket cash or Checking count toward the $2,000,000 GTA 6 ending requirement?

No. Loose pocket cash and personal BuckMe! Checking balances are subject to seizure upon Jason's arrest. Only money transferred into the shared BuckMe! Family Savings account via “Buck Yourself” or a physical ATM counts toward the $2,000,000 requirement (which accrues interest to ~$2,500,000 when lawyer Abe secures Jason's release).

Can you still get the best ending if Jason or Lucia becomes Irredeemable?

No. While the first four Criminal Profile tiers (Professional, Hardened, Violent, and Psycho) are fluid and can be improved through non-lethal disarms and low-casualty robberies, the fifth tier—Irredeemable—is permanent for the rest of that save file and locks you out of peaceful surrender and the Two Years Later reunion.
