About the GTA 6 Interactive Leonida Map

This is a free interactive map of Leonida, the state in Grand Theft Auto VI, with 185 verified pins across heist targets, banks and vaults, jewelry and pawn shops, Ammu-Nation stores, properties and fences, wildlife habitats and Easter eggs. Pins link to the full guide for each location, and your progress is saved in your own browser.

How to use the map

  • Search by name or code (for example a safe code, a fence or an animal) in the sidebar search box.
  • Use the category rows to show or hide pin types; “All” and “None” toggle every category at once.
  • Jump to a region with the region buttons above the map, or press “Full Map” to reset the view.
  • Click a pin for its payout, difficulty, security notes, tips and escape route where known, then mark it as found. “Next Unfound” flies to the nearest pin you have not ticked and “Hide Found” clears completed pins.
  • Drop your own custom pins and export or import a JSON backup from the Backup button. Personal pins and progress never leave your browser.

Map regions and their guides

  • Vice City and Vice Beach — the main city and its beachfront.
  • Port Gellhorn — docks, ATMs, fences and the shipyard.
  • Leonida Keys — the island chain, including Key Lento.
  • Grassrivers — swamp wetlands and airboat routes.
  • Mount Kalaga — mountain and national park hunting grounds.
  • Lake Leonida and Ambrosia — the inland lake and industrial district.
