Frequently Asked Questions: GTA 6 Radio & Audio

Everything you need to know about stations, physical controls, and DMCA protection.

How do I change radio stations in GTA 6?

Hold Left on the D-Pad (or hold Q on PC) while in any vehicle or wearing on-foot headphones. Use the right analog stick or mouse to navigate the station radial wheel.

Can I skip songs on GTA 6 radio stations?

Live radio broadcasts operate on real-time synchronized schedules and cannot be skipped. However, custom user mixtapes stored on Jason or Lucia's smartphone allow full track skipping and repeat playback.

Where is 'The Vault Radio' unlocked?

The Vault Radio is an underground electronic/house station unlocked by infiltrating The Vault nightclub in Vice City Downtown and scanning the gas mask wall artwork with your camera phone.
