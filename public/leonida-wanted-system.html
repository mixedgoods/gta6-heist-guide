How Leonida Counter-Forensics & Witness Memory Work

Four mechanics every Jason & Lucia heist getaway depends on after robbing banks, jewelry stores, or armored trucks.

1. ANPR CAMERAS

Avoid Flagged Plates Under Bridge Gantries

Vice City causeways, toll booths, and major intersections mount ANPR scanners. Driving a flagged getaway vehicle under a scanner immediately refreshes your heat timer and pings your direction to VCPD.

2. VEHICLE SWAP (-38%)

Switch Rides Out of Line-of-Sight

Wait until your minimap stops flashing before ditching your hot vehicle. Entering an unflagged car unseen resets VCPD vehicle BOLO tracking—cruisers can pass right beside you if you obey traffic flow.

3. DISGUISE SWAP (-28%)

Discard Masks & Swap Jackets

Keep spare jackets and hats stored in your personal car trunk. Pulling off your heist mask and changing outerwear out of sight breaks facial/clothing witness matches and slashes cooldown time.

4. CCTV & WEATHER (-15%)

Wipe Store Servers & Use Storm Cover

Wiping back-room CCTV servers inside the 20 Jewelry Stores and 8 Large Bank Vaults delays initial witness identification, while tropical storms and night fog shrink helicopter visual cones.

How do ANPR license plate cameras work in GTA 6? +
ANPR toll gantries across Vice City bridges and major highways scan plates on flagged vehicles. Passing under a camera in a hot getaway car immediately resets your wanted cooldown and broadcasts your heading to nearby VCPD interceptors.
How much faster do you lose cops by swapping cars and clothes? +
Breaking line of sight and switching into an unflagged vehicle cuts police search cooldown by roughly 38%. Discarding your robbery mask and swapping jackets out of sight drops witness BOLO matching by another 28%.
