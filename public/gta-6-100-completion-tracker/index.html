GTA 6 100% Completion, Checklist & Trophy FAQ

How to get 100% completion in GTA 6?

To reach 100% completion in GTA 6 and unlock the “A Long Road” Gold trophy, finish all 35 completion requirements tracked by the game: 12 Story & Tasks goals (including all 91 main story missions and 100 optional tasks), 20 Exploration & Activities goals, and 3 Discovery goals. Check off each item in our GTA 6 100% completion checklist above to track your exact progress.

What counts toward GTA 6 100% completion?

All 35 requirements fall into three groups. Full-completion requirements demand 100% of their pool: 91 of 91 Story Missions, 39 of 39 Gang Operations, 22 of 22 Bank Jobs, 7 of 7 Payday Loan Jobs, 8 of 8 Large Bank Vaults, 33 of 33 Underwater Discoveries, 2 of 2 Unique Playlists, 42 of 42 Shooting Range Golds, 42 of 42 Top Fun Drone Time Trials across 14 locations, 15 of 15 Bret’s Target Decks, 4 of 4 Video Games Played, and fencing $100K Narcotics, $100K Jewelry, $250K Marked Bills, and $200K Vehicles. Partial-threshold requirements only require a subset: 100 Optional Tasks, 34/35 Smash ’N’ Grabs, 50/91 Stick Ups, 5/7 Kenton Sets, 15/18 Collectible Card Sets, 100/108 WAiNK Locations, 30/34 Magazines/Books/Comics, 15/18 Meditations, 100/125 Cocaine Flamingos, 70/79 Discovered Styles, 30/40 Races Won, 10/13 Race Challenge Golds, 5/7 Pool Opponents Defeated, 2/3 Mini Golf Locations Played, 90/100 Basketball Hoops Scored, 300/397 Stunts, 70/80 Challenges, 200/277 Vehicles Scanned, 50/66 Standard-Issue Weapons, and 100/255 Animals Studied.

Where is the GTA 6 completion checklist?

In-game, open the pause menu and navigate to Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. That menu only unlocks after you beat the final Chapter 5 story mission (The End of the Road). Before finishing the campaign, use this interactive GTA 6 100% completion tracker to view and log all 35 requirements.

Does story progress count toward 100%?

Yes. Completing all 91 main story missions across Chapters 1 through 5 is Requirement #1 on the checklist, and every robbery, collectible, WAiNK scan, race, challenge, wildlife study, or vehicle scan you complete during the story automatically carries over when the Progress > Stats > 100% Completion menu unlocks.

Is anything missable for 100% completion vs. the GTA 6 Platinum trophy?

The 35 in-game 100% completion requirements (which unlock the “A Long Road” Gold trophy) and the complete 55-trophy Platinum journey (“Vice Got a Price!”) are two separate progression tracks. Nothing on the 35-item 100% completion checklist is missable—every requirement can be completed in post-story free roam, and 100% completion only requires 15 of the 18 open-world meditation spots. For the 55-trophy Platinum journey, the “Positive Mental Attitude” Bronze trophy (#28, “Meditate at every spot across Leonida”) involves an unmarked Chapter 1 balcony meditation with Lucia right after Down to Earth that cannot be replayed once you leave Chapter 1; existing community reporting identifies this Chapter 1 balcony interaction as potentially missable, though strict trophy necessity remains unconfirmed. As a precaution, lock a manual backup before Tourist Trap (Slot 03 in our 15-Slot Save Planner). Note that Jason and Lucia relationship levels, Criminal Profile tiers, and specific Chapter 5 ending branches are narrative systems—no trophy requires a specific relationship level, Criminal Profile tier, or ending choice (Finishing The End of the Road on any branch unlocks “You Gotta Know When to Get Off”, and “Actions Speak Louder” simply counts 500 cumulative profile-shifting actions in either direction). See our GTA 6 Endings Guide and Jason & Lucia Relationship Guide if you want to explore narrative outcomes separately.

How many stunts are required for 100%?

You must complete 300 of the 397 stunts across Leonida for 100% completion (Requirement #30). Any combination of the 130 Stunt Jumps, 202 Under the Bridges, 40 Knife Flights, and 25 Base Jumps counts toward the 300 total.

GTA 6 250 versus 300 stunts: what is the difference?

Reaching 250 stunts unlocks the “Cunning Stunts” Silver trophy (#15), whereas the in-game 100% completion checklist requires 300 stunts (#30). Players who stop as soon as the trophy pops remain 50 stunts short of 100%. Use the 250 vs. 300 stunt calculator above to track both milestones simultaneously.

How many animals must be studied?

You need to study 100 of the 255 animals in Leonida using binoculars (Requirement #35). Hunting or killing wildlife is not required for this goal. Species you check off in our GTA 6 Animals Database synchronize automatically with the Database Reconciliation counter on this page.

How many vehicles must be scanned?

You must scan 200 of the 277 base-model vehicles using the in-game WAiNK phone app (Requirement #33; standout vehicles are tracked separately). Scanning your first 100 base models also unlocks the “Whip It Good” Bronze trophy (#35). Vehicles marked in our GTA 6 Vehicles Database synchronize directly with this tracker.

How many challenges count toward 100%?

You must complete 70 of the 80 in-game Challenges across 8 categories of 10 (Requirement #31), allowing you to skip up to 10 challenges. Completing 50 Challenges also unlocks the “One Last Thing” Bronze trophy (#20).

How to unlock A Long Road?

The “A Long Road” Gold trophy (#14, “Attain 100% Completion”) unlocks automatically when all 35 requirements in Progress > Stats > 100% Completion reach 100%. In this tracker, checking off all 35 completion items automatically marks the GTA 6 A Long Road trophy complete toward your “Vice Got a Price!” Platinum.

How many trophies and achievements are available?

The GTA 6 PS5 trophy list features 55 trophies (1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, and 46 Bronze), all earnable in single-player Story Mode. Because Xbox Series X|S does not use a Platinum tier, there are 54 GTA 6 Xbox achievements totaling 1,000 Gamerscore, matching trophies #02 through #55 in our GTA 6 trophy checklist.

Does the progress tracker save after refreshing?

Yes. All checked requirements, trophy cards, stunt totals, and database items save immediately to your browser’s localStorage (key: gta6hg_progress_v1). Your progress stays intact after refreshing or closing the browser and syncs across open tabs on the same device. To back up your data or move it to another device, click Export Backup and Import Backup in the progress toolbar.
