GTA 6 Jason & Lucia Relationship Guide — How to Reach Hot Together 4/4

Complete walkthrough for all four Jason and Lucia relationship tiers (Running on Empty, Partners in Crime, Crazy in Love, and Hot Together 4/4), the five Chapter 1 “Coming Home” errands, Jason's 5 house repairs, character switching rules, and the independent 5-tier Criminal Profile system. Partner activities, meditations, and co-op vaults also count toward our GTA 6 100% completion checklist and 55-trophy tracker.

⚡ DIRECT ANSWER: HOW TO REACH HOT TOGETHER (4/4) & CHECK YOUR STATUS HUD SHORTCUT: HOLD D-PAD RIGHT

In Grand Theft Auto VI, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos share a dynamic 4-tier Relationship Meter that tracks their romantic closeness separately from their individual 5-tier Criminal Profiles. Hold Right on the D-Pad during free roam (or open the Pause Menu → Jason & Lucia tab with L1 / LB) to check your current heart icon. Completing Jason's 5 stilt house repairs and all 5 optional “Coming Home” errands in Chapter 1 starts you immediately at 3/4 Crazy in Love; from there, shared dates, D-Pad affection prompts, replying to texts, sleeping in the same bed, and pulling off robberies together push you to 4/4 Hot Together—a mandatory prerequisite for the “Two Years Later” best ending.

TIER 1 OF 4 • GRAY BROKEN HEART
Running on Empty (1/4)

Romance has completely fizzled out due to neglect or abandoning your partner. Cold, strictly platonic dialogue; locks out good endings.

TIER 2 OF 4 • PINK/GRAY BROKEN HEART
Partners in Crime (2/4)

Also labeled Not So Hot in strained prompts. Default starting tier if you skip the Chapter 1 house repairs and Coming Home errands.

TIER 3 OF 4 • SOLID PINK HEART
Crazy in Love (3/4)

Healthy, affectionate romance unlocked right in Chapter 1 by finishing Jason's house repairs and all 5 pre-release errands.

TIER 4 OF 4 • BURNING HEART ICON
Hot Together (4/4)

Maximum bond. Spontaneous affection, high goodwill buffer against minor mistakes, and unlocks the Two Years Later best ending.

CHAPTER 1 • PRE-RELEASE WINDOW

All 5 Optional “Coming Home” Errands (Key Lento Walkthrough)

💾 Track Chapter 1 in Save Planner →

During Chapter 1's “Coming Home” main mission, Jason has a free-roam preparation window in Key Lento before driving to Hamlet Correctional Facility to pick up Lucia. While all five errands below are optional, completing every single one (alongside Jason's 5 house repairs) launches their relationship at 3/4 Crazy in Love right out of the gate instead of 2/4 Partners in Crime:

ERRAND 01 OF 05 • NORTHERN KEY LENTO (MANATEE'S REST BAR)

1. Get Lucia's Clothes (From Her Friend Andy)

Answer the incoming phone call from an unknown number (Lucia's friend Andy), then drive to the Manatee's Rest bar in northern Key Lento. Speak with Andy, pick up the bundle of Lucia's civilian clothes, and stow them inside the trunk of Jason's car so she has fresh clothes waiting outside Hamlet Correctional.

ERRAND 02 OF 05 • KEY LENTO SUPERMARKET / MINI-MART

2. Pick Up Groceries & Carry Them Inside

Stop at the marked Key Lento supermarket, collect the items highlighted with pink objective markers, and load the grocery bags into your car's trunk. Drive back to Brian Heder's stilt house and physically carry the grocery bags inside the kitchen to stock the safehouse before heading to the prison.

ERRAND 03 OF 05 • KEY LENTO FLOWER STAND (~$25)

3. Buy Flowers (Red Roses Automatic Delivery)

Visit the roadside flower stand in Key Lento and purchase the bouquet of red roses (~$25) (smaller budget bouquets are also sold, but red roses grant the full welcome reaction). Purchasing the flowers automatically delivers them to the safehouse table for Lucia's homecoming.

ERRAND 04 OF 05 • SOUTHERN KEY LENTO (THE SIREN'S CHEST JEWELRY STORE — ~$475)

4. Buy Lucia a Necklace (High-Priority Chapter 2 Carryover Item)

Drive to The Siren's Chest jewelry store in southern Key Lento and buy the custom gold necklace (~$475). Why you should never skip this: In addition to giving a major relationship boost when Lucia returns, this necklace carries forward as a useful plot and collateral item in Chapter 2.

ERRAND 05 OF 05 • SOUTHERN KEY LENTO (BARNACLE BEAUTIES SALON)

5. “Get The Chop” (Haircut & Beard Trim)

Walk right next door from The Siren's Chest into Barnacle Beauties hair salon in southern Key Lento and give Jason a fresh haircut or beard trim, then drive to Hamlet Correctional Facility on time before the release window expires.

CHAPTER 1 • BRIAN HEDER'S KEY LENTO STILT HOUSE

Jason's 5 Key Lento House Repairs (Complete Before “Coming Home”)

Right after completing “Gone Fishing” in Chapter 1—and before you leave Key Lento to pick Lucia up in “Coming Home”—Jason can perform 5 optional DIY house repairs around Brian Heder's waterfront stilt house. Completing all 5 repairs grants a large initial heart boost when Lucia walks through the door:

REPAIR 01

Exterior Front Steps

Hammer and secure the loose wooden boards on the front exterior staircase leading up to the stilt deck.

REPAIR 02

Leaky Roof Patch

Climb the ladder beside the front entrance and patch the damaged roof shingles before the coastal rain hits.

REPAIR 03

Porch Railing by Gym

Fix the broken wooden porch railing adjacent to Jason's outdoor weight bench and workout deck.

REPAIR 04

Interior Bedroom Wall

Plaster and smooth the damaged drywall section inside Jason and Lucia's shared bedroom.

REPAIR 05

Paint Bedroom Wall

Roll fresh paint over the repaired bedroom wall, choosing between Tropical Green, Stormy Blue, or Pastel Peach.

DO'S & DON'TS

How to Increase Relationship in GTA 6 (And What Drains Hearts)

+ GAINS HEARTS (TOWARD 4/4 HOT TOGETHER)

Activities That Increase Relationship

  • Chapter 1 Preparation Head Start: Finishing all 5 Key Lento house repairs and all 5 Coming Home errands before picking Lucia up from prison.
  • Everyday Affection Prompts: Using contextual D-Pad prompts while walking together to hold hands, share a quick kiss, hug, or give compliments.
  • Fast Text Replies & D-Pad Check-Ins: Answering your partner's texts and phone calls promptly and sending quick check-ins when operating separately across Leonida.
  • Sleeping in the Same Bed: Returning to the active safehouse at night and sleeping in the same bed at the same time.
  • Shared Dates & Recreation: Going out to bars, nightclubs, strip clubs, diners, basketball courts, pool tables, gyms, or scenic boat/helicopter tours together.
  • Pulling Off Duo Robberies & Escapes: Successfully robbing convenience stores, pawn shops, jewelry stores, or banks as a two-person team and clearing wanted heat together.
- DRAINS HEARTS (TOWARD 1/4 RUNNING ON EMPTY)

Actions That Decrease Relationship

  • Extended Solo Neglect: Spending many consecutive in-game days apart without visiting the safehouse, hanging out, or sending a phone check-in.
  • Ignoring Phone Calls & Texts: Letting partner text messages sit unread or hanging up on check-in calls while free-roaming.
  • Abandoning Your Partner Mid-Pursuit: Driving off and leaving Jason or Lucia stranded behind during a police chase or active stickup.
  • Failing Joint Criminal Jobs: Botching duo robberies or getting your partner Busted/Wasted during a shared free-roam score.
  • Friendly Fire / Vehicular Injury: Accidentally or intentionally hitting your partner with a vehicle, explosion, or melee attack in free roam.
DUAL-PROTAGONIST FREE ROAM

Joint Activities & Switching Between Jason and Lucia

GTA 6 upgrades Rockstar's character-switching wheel by allowing you to choose whether to play as Jason or Lucia solo or bring your partner along in Joint / Accompanying Mode:

🥂 Best Joint Activities for Fast Heart Gains

When hanging out together, take your partner to marked Vice City or Keys date spots—bars, nightclubs, strip clubs, and restaurants—or challenge them to basketball, eight-ball pool, shared gym workouts, or scuba/boat/helicopter excursions. Finishing a date and sleeping in the same safehouse bed locks in a reliable heart progression tick.

🔄 Solo vs. Duo Switching Without Losing Hearts

When you switch between Jason and Lucia while they are in different parts of Leonida, the inactive character continues their own daily routine. If you spend several in-game days solo-hunting collectibles or 170+ animals, reply to every text message they send and tap the D-Pad check-in prompt so your meter never decays from Hot Together.

🏦 Duo Robbery Synergy Bonus

Bringing your partner along for Jewelry Stores, Pawn Shops, and Small Banks lets one operative hold crowd intimidation while the other cracks safes and bags loot—boosting both your Relationship meter and your $2,000,000 Family Savings goal simultaneously.

GTA 6 Criminal Profile Guide: All 5 Levels & How It Differs From Relationship

Rockstar replaced Red Dead Redemption 2's single moral Honor bar with the Criminal Profile system. Because players frequently conflate the Relationship Meter with the Criminal Profile when preparing for the Chapter 5 endings, here is the exact mechanical distinction:

Feature Relationship Meter (Romantic Bond) Criminal Profile (Tactical Restraint vs. Violence)
Who It Tracks 1 Shared Meter for Jason & Lucia together as a couple. 2 Independent Meters (Jason and Lucia are tracked separately; one can be Professional while the other is Psycho).
Total Tiers 4 Tiers: Running on Empty (1/4) → Partners in Crime (2/4) → Crazy in Love (3/4) → Hot Together (4/4). 5 Tiers: Professional → Hardened → Violent → Psycho → Irredeemable.
What Moves It Dates, Chapter 1 errands & house repairs, affection prompts, texts, sleeping together, and duo jobs. How you execute crimes: stealth, crowd intimidation, and weapon-hand disarms vs. civilian murders and police massacres.
Can It Lock Permanently? No. You can always raise Relationship back up through dates and joint activities before Chapter 5. Yes — Tier 5 (Irredeemable) is permanent. Tiers 1–4 are fluid, but hitting Irredeemable can never be undone on that save file.
How to Check in Menu Hold D-Pad Right or Pause → Jason & Lucia tab (center heart icon). Hold D-Pad Right or Pause → Jason & Lucia tab, then press D-Pad Left (Jason) or D-Pad Right (Lucia).

Frequently Asked Questions — Relationship & Criminal Profile

What are the 4 relationship levels in GTA 6?

From lowest to highest, the four relationship levels are 1/4 Running on Empty (Gray Broken Heart), 2/4 Partners in Crime / Not So Hot (Pink and Gray Broken Heart), 3/4 Crazy in Love (Solid Pink Heart), and 4/4 Hot Together (Burning Heart).

What are the 5 optional Coming Home errands in Chapter 1?

Before driving to Hamlet Correctional Facility in “Coming Home”, Jason can complete five errands in Key Lento: (1) Get Lucia's Clothes from Andy at Manatee's Rest, (2) Pick Up Groceries at the Key Lento supermarket and carry them inside, (3) Buy red roses (~$25) at the flower stand, (4) Buy a gold necklace (~$475) at The Siren's Chest, and (5) Get a haircut (“Get The Chop”) at Barnacle Beauties.

How do you raise your Criminal Profile back to Professional or Hardened?

As long as a character has not crossed into the permanent 5th tier (Irredeemable), you can rehabilitate a Violent or Psycho profile back up to Hardened and Professional by using stealth, intimidating hostages without harming them, and shooting weapons out of security guards' or police officers' hands rather than executing them.
