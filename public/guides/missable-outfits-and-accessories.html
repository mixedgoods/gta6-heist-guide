Pre-Cutoff Wardrobe & Relationship Safety Steps

  1. Complete all Jason Key Lento errands before Hamlet Prison: Buy the $475 necklace at The Siren's Chest, repair the roof/steps, grab groceries, and get a fresh haircut before triggering Coming Home.
  2. Grab the free sunglasses during Day Release: Follow the optional yacht interaction with Mattia and Kat during Day Release before leaving the vessel.
  3. Leave Easter egg outfits for free roam: Items like the Tommy Vercetti Shirt remain safely collectable after Chapter 5.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can missing an outfit or sunglasses lock me out of 100% completion in GTA 6?

No verified wardrobe item is required for the core 100% Completion Tracker categories (collectibles, stunts, weapons, vehicles, animals). However, skipping Coming Home errands like the $475 necklace can cost missable relationship heart boosts.

Can you still get Tommy Vercetti's shirt after beating the story?

Yes—the Tommy Vercetti Easter egg shirt and bike are free-roam discoveries obtainable after Chapter 5.

Where do you buy Lucia's necklace in Chapter 1?

Purchase the $475 necklace at The Siren's Chest jewelry shop in Key Lento before driving to Hamlet Prison in Coming Home.
