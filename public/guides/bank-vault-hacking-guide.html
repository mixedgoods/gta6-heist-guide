Confirmed Bank & Vault Entry Mechanics in GTA 6

  1. Wipe the CCTV security room first: In endgame Large Bank Vaults (unlocked after Chapter 4's That's Where The Money Is...), clearing the security camera room slows police escalation and maximizes your vault looting window.
  2. Choose silent thermal drilling over loud explosives: Thermal breaching takes longer on the door than C4/explosives, but it delays heavy SWAT arrival—making it far easier to clear the top-tier ~$205,000 payout cleanly.
  3. Activate the Dye Pack Jammer before bagging stacks: Unlocked during Chapter 3's Credit Check, the Dye Pack Jammer neutralizes exploding ink packs on teller and vault cash bundles.
  4. Use verified fixed 4-digit keypad codes where applicable: Gang safes and story lockups use fixed 4-digit keypads rather than randomized hacking puzzles.
Factual Safeguard — No Invented Hacking Minigames: GTA6HeistGuide does not invent unverified fingerprint-cloning minigames, circuit-board bypasses, or universal vault keypad codes. Rumored keypad sequences like Crab Outpost's 0072 / 0212 remain explicitly unverified.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a universal bank vault keypad code in GTA 6?

No. Repeatable bank vaults rely on thermal drilling or explosive breaching paired with CCTV wipes and the Dye Pack Jammer, not a single universal keypad code.

When do I unlock the Dye Pack Jammer?

You unlock the Dye Pack Jammer automatically during Chapter 3's Credit Check mission with Raul Bautista.

Which breaching method yields the highest vault payout?

Silent thermal drilling combined with a CCTV room wipe gives you the longest uncontested looting window inside Large Bank Vaults, maximizing the ~$205,000 ceiling.
