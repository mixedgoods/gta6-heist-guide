GTA 6 All 80 Challenges Guide (70 Required for 100% & 50 for Trophy)

Grand Theft Auto VI features 80 Challenges divided evenly across 8 categories (10 per category): Combat, Criminal, Exploration, Fitness, Shopping, Sports, Survivalist, and Vehicle. Completing 50 challenges unlocks the “One Last Thing” trophy, and completing 70 of 80 checks off the Challenges requirement for 100% completion (A Long Road)—letting you safely skip the 10 hardest.

All 8 Challenge Categories (80 Total — 10 Per Category)

1. Combat (10)Multi-kills, Focus headshots, drive-bys, melee streaks (e.g., Speed Kills: 3 kills in <30s; Melee Combat: 5 melee KOs without dying; Explosive Combat: 3 hits with 1 explosive; Stealth Sniping: 7 unalerted scoped kills)
2. Criminal (10)Muggings, stick-ups, carjackings & fencing (e.g., Mugging: rob 5 peds; Stick Ups: rob 3 stores in 1 day without dying/arrest; Carjacking: steal 3 cars & hit Pay ’n’ Spray in 1 day)
3. Exploration (10)WAiNK app scans, collectibles, map discovery & unique landmarks
4. Fitness (10)Gym workouts, stamina feats & Meditation Spots for Jason & Lucia
5. Shopping (10)Clothing purchases, vehicle customization & retail vendor milestones
6. Sports (10)Street races, shooting range score targets, basketball & Leonida sports
7. Survivalist (10)Bret Vogel hunting targets, fishing & studying wildlife with binoculars
8. Vehicle (10)High-speed streaks, near-miss chains, stunt ramps & crash-free distance
Trophy Thresholds50/80 = “One Last Thing” trophy  •  70/80 = 100% Completion (A Long Road)

Fastest Strategy to Hit 70/80 Challenges

  1. Check Progress → Challenges Frequently: Open the pause menu and go to Progress > Challenges. Hovering over any tile shows a “Completed” checkmark in the bottom-right corner once finished.
  2. Stack Criminal & Combat Challenges During Free Roam: Knock out Stick Ups (3 stores in one day) and Carjacking (3 cars to Pay ’n’ Spray in one day) while farming cash in Port Gellhorn or Vice City. Pair Stealth Sniping (7 unalerted scoped kills) with rural Illegal Operations.
  3. Knock Out Easy Shopping, Fitness & Exploration Tiles First: Visiting Leonida’s 9 gyms, hitting 18 Meditation Spots, buying outfits, and running WAiNK scans naturally clears 25–30 challenges with zero combat risk.
  4. Use Your 10-Challenge Skip Buffer Wisely: Because 100% only requires 70/80, skip any frustrating RNG or no-death streak challenges (such as long crash-free driving or tight melee chains) until last.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you need all 80 challenges for 100% completion in GTA 6?

No. You only need 70 out of 80 challenges for 100% completion ("A Long Road"), giving you a buffer to skip the 10 hardest.

What trophy unlocks at 50 challenges?

Completing 50 challenges unlocks the "One Last Thing" trophy/achievement.

Where do you view challenge progress in GTA 6?

Open the Pause Menu and navigate to Progress > Challenges; completed challenges show a checkmark in the bottom-right corner.
