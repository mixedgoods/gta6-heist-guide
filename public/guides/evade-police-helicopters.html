How to Evade Police Helicopters & VCPD Air Support in GTA 6

Once a Vice City or Kelly County chase climbs to 3+ stars, VCPD Air Support helicopters (VCPD Air Support Helicopter) keep your wanted timer refreshed from above even after you outrun ground cruisers. This guide focuses strictly on reproducible line-of-sight evasion tactics — excluding unverified city-wide sewer networks and unsupported numerical heat percentages.

4 Reproducible Ways to Shake VCPD Air Support

  1. Duck under multi-level overpasses & parking decks: Straight highway sprints cannot outrun a helicopter's sight cone. Cut beneath concrete highway interchanges, covered bridges, or multi-story parking structures to sever vertical line-of-sight immediately.
  2. Transition from street to dense urban alleys or covered bays: Once you break the overhead cone, kill speed and tuck cleanly out of street-level cruiser patrol lines instead of peeling out back onto open boulevards.
  3. Use terrain masking in rural Leonida: In Mount Kalaga, hug steep ridgelines and dense pine canopy with an off-road 4x4 (Vapid Riata) to block low-angle helicopter spotlight sweeps.
  4. Break pursuit on water when near piers: Along Vice Beach, Watson Bay, or Grassrivers, shifting from a hot road vehicle to a Shitzu Squalo or Swamp Airboat lets you slip under docks, bridges, and mangrove channels where patrol cruisers cannot follow.
Factual Safeguard: Ignore claims of a connected city-wide Vice City underground sewer escape network or guaranteed "cool-down" paint-shop resets — neither is independently verified. Stick to physical overhead cover, terrain masking, and clean line-of-sight breaks.

Best Overhead Cover by Leonida Region

Vice City & Downtown

Multi-story parking bays, highway underpasses, and covered port warehouses around Downtown Vice City and Watson Bay.

Port Gellhorn, Keys & Grassrivers

Container stacks at the Port Gellhorn Shipyard, causeway bridge undersides in the Leonida Keys, and mangrove/bridge cover along Grassrivers waterways.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you outrun a VCPD police helicopter in a supercar on open highways?

Rarely on flat open roads — helicopters maintain overhead line-of-sight at high speed. You must break vertical sight using overpasses, covered parking decks, bridges, or dense canopy.

Can I use a connected underground Vice City sewer network to escape helicopters?

A connected city-wide Vice City sewer escape network has NOT been independently verified. Rely on confirmed surface overpasses, parking garages, and dock cover instead.

Should I shoot down the police helicopter during a robbery getaway?

Shooting down VCPD Air Support escalates your wanted level and spawns replacements quickly; breaking line-of-sight under overhead cover is faster and far safer.
