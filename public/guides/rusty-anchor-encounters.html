The Rusty Anchor Bar Optional Encounters & Cal Hampton Texts (Ch. 1)

The Rusty Anchor in Key Lento (Mariana County) is more than a scenic Keys dive bar—it is the hub for multiple Chapter 1 story beats and optional encounters, including Cal Hampton’s optional text-triggered clothing interaction before Swamped and Burim’s ankle-monitor bypass at the start of Day Release.

How to Catch Every Rusty Anchor Encounter

  1. Monitor Cal Hampton’s Texts Before Swamped: While working through mid-Chapter 1, open your smartphone messages (D-Pad Up) regularly. Before triggering Swamped, answer Cal’s text inviting you to The Rusty Anchor for the optional bar and clothing interaction.
  2. Socialize & Grab Bar Drinks: Stopping by the counter lets you test bar drink interactions and soak in local Keys chatter—see our Food, Drinks & Bars Guide for how consumables behave.
  3. Trigger Day Release at the Back Bar: When Day Release is available, walk to the rear of The Rusty Anchor and order a drink to start the cutscene where Burim temporarily disables Lucia’s ankle monitor before you launch from the rear speedboat dock.
  4. Finish Everything Before Tourist Trap: Clear all Rusty Anchor visits, Cal texts, and Donnie fence sales prior to Tourist Trap so nothing is left behind when Chapter 1 closes.
