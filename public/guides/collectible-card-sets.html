GTA 6 Collectible Card Sets Guide: 480 Cards, 18 Sets & Pawn Shop Resale

Grand Theft Auto VI features 18 Collectible Card Sets totaling 480 unique cards hidden across Leonida. You need 3 complete sets for the Don't Touch My Deck trophy/achievement and 15 complete sets for 100% completion—and you can safely sell collected cards at Pawn Shops for $20 to $1,000+ without losing collection progress.

Where to Look for Cards & Safe Selling

Check Yellow Phone Boxes, Toolboxes & Interior Counters: Outdoor cards frequently sit inside yellow phone boxes and toolboxes, while indoor cards rest on bars, desks, and display shelves. Feel free to cash them in at any Leonida Pawn Shop—your Progress Menu remembers every card you have ever picked up.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Collectible Card Sets do you need for 100% in GTA 6?

You need 15 complete sets out of 18 total sets for 100% completion, and 3 complete sets for the 'Don't Touch My Deck' trophy/achievement.

Is it safe to sell Collectible Cards at Pawn Shops?

Yes. Selling cards at Pawn Shops earns $20 to $1,000+ per card and does not remove them from your completed collection progress.

Can you buy Collectible Cards in stores?

No. Unlike Red Dead Redemption 2, GTA 6 Collectible Cards cannot be bought—they must all be found and picked up in the open world.
