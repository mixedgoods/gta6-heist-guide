Kingpin Bowling Guide (Port Gellhorn — Safe Code 0300 & “Let’s Go Bowling”)

Kingpin Bowling in Port Gellhorn (Kelly County) is one of Chapter 2’s most rewarding optional hits. Taken over by the Kolt & Associates syndicate, clearing the alley unlocks right after Coffee Break and rewards a fixed safe (code 0300), a luxury watch collectible, and the unique Gutterball Moreland 850 weapon variant.

Step-by-Step Kingpin Bowling Walkthrough

  1. Prep Armor & Explosives First: Before hitting Kingpin Bowling after Coffee Break, refill body armor at Ammu-Nation and pack sticky bombs as backup (though you already have the exact safe code 0300).
  2. Clear the Kolt & Associates Crew: Approach the bowling alley and neutralize the syndicate guards holding the lanes and back rooms. Use lane dividers and counter booths as hard cover against shotgunners.
  3. Crack the Back-Office Safe (Code 0300): Head to the office safe and enter to empty the cash stash cleanly without wasting explosives.
  4. Grab the Luxury Watch (Southeast Room): Before leaving, sweep the southeast room to collect the luxury watch collectible (Crowex Octane Orca style) required for the trophy checklist.
  5. Claim the Gutterball Moreland 850 & Escape: Pick up the unique Gutterball Moreland 850 weapon variant, verify you have all three valuables to pop Let’s Go Bowling, and lose any Port Gellhorn police heat.

Choices, Missables & Verification Notes

Don’t Leave Without All 3 Valuables!: Corroborated trophy guides (PowerPyx) warn that Let’s Go Bowling requires grabbing all three items—the 0300 safe, the southeast-room luxury watch, and the Gutterball Moreland 850—during the hit. Double-check every room before bolting to your getaway car.
Safe Codes & Kolt Gang Ops Links: Kingpin Bowling is 1 of 3 Kolt & Associates safes in Port Gellhorn. Get the other two codes (6823 and 6969) in our Kolt & Associates Gang Ops Guide and Master Safe Codes Database.
