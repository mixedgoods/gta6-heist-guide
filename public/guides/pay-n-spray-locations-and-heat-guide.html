GTA 6 Pay ’n’ Spray Guide (All 19 Locations, Heat Clearing & Services)

The classic Pay ’n’ Spray returns across 19 locations in Leonida as a multi-purpose getaway and registration hub. Driving in for a respray wipes Vehicle Heat (license plate & car description), repairs damage, and lets you register or sell stolen cars—but you cannot enter while police have active line-of-sight.

How to Clear Heat Correctly at a Pay ’n’ Spray

  1. Break Police Line of Sight First: If your wanted stars are solid and cruisers have a visual on your bumper, the Pay ’n’ Spray shutters will not let you clear heat. Duck into an alley, parking garage, or storm drain until the search cone breaks.
  2. Use the Map Index GPS Shortcut: Open the Map Index, switch to the Vehicles tab, and select Pay ’n’ Spray to auto-route to the closest of the 19 shops.
  3. Change Clothes If Cops Saw Your Face: Respraying clears Vehicle Heat (plate & color), but GTA 6 police also remember Jason and Lucia’s personal description. Swap outfits, hats, glasses, or hairstyles if witnesses positively ID’d you.
  4. Know Pay ’n’ Spray vs. Mod Shops: Use Pay ’n’ Spray for fast repairs, heat-clearing resprays, registration, and selling; visit dedicated Mod Shops (like Rideout Customs) for exotic liveries, decals, and deep cosmetic builds.

Challenge Synergy

Criminal Challenge Shortcut: The Carjacking tile in All 80 Challenges asks you to carjack 3 vehicles and take them to a Pay ’n’ Spray in a single in-game day without dying or being arrested—easy to clear in downtown Vice City.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Pay 'n' Spray locations are in GTA 6?

There are 19 Pay 'n' Spray garages across Leonida, sortable by distance via Map Index > Vehicles > Pay 'n' Spray.

Why won't Pay 'n' Spray clear my wanted level?

You cannot use Pay 'n' Spray while cops have direct line-of-sight on you. Break visual contact first, then pull in to wipe Vehicle Heat—and change clothes if police also ID'd your character.

Can you customize liveries and decals at Pay 'n' Spray?

No. Pay 'n' Spray handles repairs, basic resprays, registration, and car sales; dedicated Mod Shops handle exotic paint palettes, custom liveries, and deep visual mods.
