GTA 6 Under the Bridge Guide: 202 Bridge Passes & Fastest 100% Stunt Strategy

Under the Bridge is the largest stunt category in Grand Theft Auto VI, spanning 202 bridges across Leonida's five counties. Because 100% completion requires 300 cumulative stunts out of 397 total (and 250 for the Cunning Stunts trophy), corroborated PowerPyx and IGN guides single out Under the Bridge as the fastest category to grind.

Best Aircraft & Clean-Pass Rules

Use a Compact Helicopter

Pick a nimble light helicopter rather than a wide-wing plane. Hover-braking lets you line up narrow canal spans in Vice City and low marshes in Grassrivers without clipping concrete pillars.

Zero Blade or Skid Contact

To register credit, pass cleanly beneath the bridge deck without grazing the underside, piers, or water surface. If the counter does not tick up, loop around and fly dead-center through the widest arch.

Smartest Region-by-Region Sweep to 300 Stunts

Work One County at a Time: Clear the long linear chains first—the Leonida Keys causeways, Vice City canals and bay bridges, and Port Gellhorn / Grassrivers highway spans. Pair ~180–200 bridges with all 25 Base Jumps and the cleanest Stunt Jumps to hit 300/397 with 97 stunts to spare!

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Under the Bridge challenges are in GTA 6?

There are 202 Under the Bridge passes across Leonida's five counties (some early guides round this to 200), contributing toward the 300 cumulative stunts needed for 100% completion.

What is the best aircraft for Under the Bridge in GTA 6?

A compact, nimble helicopter is far easier than a fixed-wing plane because you can slow down and hover-align through narrow canal spans and low clearances.
