GTA 6 Free-Roam Robbery Respawn Timers & Cooldown Guide

Farming free-roam robberies in GTA 6 is governed by clear in-game reset rules. Knocking over a retail store or business closes it for the remainder of the current in-game day (marked by a minimap hourglass icon), and it reopens automatically at the start of the next in-game day—which you can advance immediately by sleeping at a safehouse.

How to Chain Robberies Without Waiting Around

  1. Watch the Minimap Hourglass Icon: After robbing a convenience store, pawn shop, or retail register, check your map—the hourglass badge confirms the business is shut for the rest of the current in-game day.
  2. Rotate Across Districts Instead of Idling: Rather than camping one block, run a multi-stop loop across Vice City or Port Gellhorn using our Vice City Jewelry Route and Pawn Shop Robbery Guide.
  3. Sleep at a Safehouse to Force the Next Day: Once your preferred district shows hourglass icons across the board, fence your stolen goods, return to your nearest safehouse, and sleep to advance into the next in-game day so every standard register reopens.
  4. Know What Respawns at Gang Hideouts: Cleared gang operations grey out on the map. Although the gang eventually repopulates the site with fresh enemies and standard cash/loot, unique weapon variants and one-time luxury items never respawn.

No Real-Time Clock Gating

In-Game Day Only: You do not need to wait real-world hours or complete story missions to reset standard free-roam stores. Exact per-venue minute counts for large bank vaults vary by heat state and are not fabricated here—use the minimap hourglass indicator and safehouse sleep cycle as your reliable reset trigger.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do stores stay closed after you rob them in GTA 6?

Robbed stores stay closed for the remainder of the current in-game day (shown by an hourglass icon on the map) and reopen at the start of the next in-game day.

Can you speed up robbery cooldowns in GTA 6?

Yes. Sleeping at a safehouse to advance to the next in-game day resets closed stores immediately without waiting in real time.

Do gang hideouts respawn in GTA 6?

Yes, cleared gang hideouts grey out and eventually repopulate with fresh enemies and regular loot, though unique one-off weapons and special items do not respawn.
