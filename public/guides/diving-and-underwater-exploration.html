Stamina, Swimming, Scuba Diving & Underwater Exploration Guide

Roughly a third of Leonida’s scenic exploration sits below the waterline—from Key Lento’s coral reefs to sunken wreck fields. Mastering cardio stamina, scuba gear swaps, O2 monitoring, Metal Detector sweeps, and shark deterrence is mandatory if you want all 33 Underwater Discoveries for 100% completion.

Diving & Swimming Mechanics Summary

Scuba Gear UnlockComplete Chapter 1’s Island Dreams (Kenton Rees, northeast Key Lento pier shack)
How to Equip ScubaWalk to the rear deck of Kenton’s charter boat or a dive boat where tanks and fins are stacked
Reported Swim ControlsLeft Stick to swim/accelerate; hold L2 / LT to strafe or swim backward; follow on-screen dive/surface prompts
Oxygen (O2) GaugeTracked in the top-left HUD corner—always surface with margin before empty
Treasure ToolMetal Detector (aim for audio/visual pings, then press Search / Triangle / Y to dig up treasure)
Marine HazardsGreat White & Hammerhead sharks (deter with Harpoon Gun), lionfish, jellyfish, and alligators in inland marsh water

Step-by-Step Safe Underwater Exploration Guide

  1. Unlock Full Scuba Kit Early: Complete Island Dreams in Chapter 1 before attempting deep dives—free-diving without tanks limits your bottom time and prevents efficient seabed searches.
  2. Equip Gear Before Dropping Overboard: Anchor your boat above the target reef, walk to the stern tank rack to put on scuba gear, and confirm your Harpoon Gun and Metal Detector are ready.
  3. Use L2 / LT Strafe Control Around Wrecks: Holding L2 / LT lets you hover, strafe, and back up smoothly inside tight sunken hulls without snagging on beams.
  4. Follow Metal Detector Pings: Sweep the Metal Detector across sandbars and hull debris until the signal peaks, then interact to unearth Gold Doubloons and wreck collectibles.
  5. Watch Both O2 & Predator Silhouettes: Keep an eye on the top-left O2 meter and scan open blue water periodically—if a shark circles in, switch to your harpoon gun to drive it off before continuing.

Choices, Missables & Verification Notes

Cardio Stamina & 33/33 Discoveries: Regular swimming and running builds your Cardio / Stamina stat for longer sprints and surface swims. Remember that 100% completion requires all 33 of 33 Underwater Discoveries plus 5 of 7 Kenton Shipwreck Sets—see Leonida Keys Salvage Jobs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you put on scuba gear in GTA 6?

After completing Island Dreams, walk to the back deck of a dive boat where scuba tanks and fins are stacked and press the gear-swap prompt.

Where is the oxygen meter during diving?

Your O2 oxygen bar appears in the top-left corner of the HUD while submerged.

Can sharks attack you while scuba diving in GTA 6?

Yes—sharks patrol deeper Keys reefs and offshore channels. Carry a harpoon gun to deter them safely.
