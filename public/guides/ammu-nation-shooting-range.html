Leonida Shooting Ranges Guide: Rodgers Ranch & Griffin (All 42 Gold Medals)

Grand Theft Auto VI features two major shooting range hubs—Rodgers Ranch & Shooting Range northeast of Port Gellhorn and the Griffin Shooting Range in Vice City—combining for 12 challenges and 42 total Gold Medals. Sweeping all 42 Gold Medals permanently boosts your Focus stat, unlocks exclusive weapon rewards, and earns The Shootist trophy/achievement.

All 6 Rodgers Ranch Score-Attack Courses (24 Gold Medals)

Each of the 6 themed stationary courses at Rodgers Ranch has 4 difficulty tiers (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Master) for 24 Gold Medals, while Vice City's Griffin Shooting Range hosts obstacle courses accounting for the remaining 18 Gold Medals:

Standard & Tactical Ranges

1. Market Range — civilian-vs-hostile target discrimination.
2. Hunting Range — precision distance shots.
3. Cops & Robbers Range — moving pop-up targets.

Novelty Themed Ranges

4. Zombie Range — rapid headshot waves.
5. Alien Range — erratic speed targets.
6. Saloon Range — classic quick-draw western targets.

Pro Tips for Sweeping All 42 Gold Medals

Freshen Up Before Grinding Master Tiers: Corroborated PowerPyx tips note that arriving fresh (washed face, fed/hydrated at your safehouse) maximizes your baseline stamina/Focus meter. Because every attempt permanently raises your Focus stat, tackling Beginner and Intermediate tiers first makes Master tiers dramatically easier.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many shooting range Gold Medals are required for 100% in GTA 6?

All 42 Gold Medals across both Rodgers Ranch (24 Golds across 6 courses x 4 tiers) and Griffin Shooting Range in Vice City (18 Golds) are required for The Shootist trophy and 100% completion.

How do you unlock the Rodgers Ranch shooting range?

Head northeast of Port Gellhorn in Kelly County and pay the inside clerk $5 for a Waiver Pass to unlock all 6 range courses on your map.
