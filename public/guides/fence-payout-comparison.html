How to Maximize Net Cash Without Losing Margin

Avoid Dumping Marked Bills at Dunk

Post-launch reporting notes that Dunk pays very low rates on marked bills and reduced margins on jewelry. In Chapter 2, rely on Kolt & Associates safes and Moses car flips to reach $15,000 cleanly, then save major jewelry runs for Yasmani in Chapter 3.

Bank Above $20,000 via ATMs & BuckMe

Free-roam pocket cash caps at $20,000. Deposit fencing proceeds into BuckMe Family Savings or nearby ATMs immediately so Wasted/Busted penalties never eat your heist profit.

Permanent Cutoffs & Debunked Fence Rumors

Do Not Search for Ambrosia or Grassrivers Fences: Earlier pre-launch lists claimed Ambrosia and Grassrivers hosted fence contacts. Our source register found zero gameplay evidence supporting either; only the 7 contacts above exist in Story Mode, and Donnie closes permanently after Swamped.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which fence pays the best for stolen jewelry in GTA 6?

Yasmani (unlocked in Chapter 3 via Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs in Vice City) is the best liquidation fence for stolen jewelry store loot.

How much does Moses pay for stolen cars in Port Gellhorn?

Community reporting places Moses's buyouts at roughly $1,000–$2,000 per untracked everyday vehicle scanned with the WAiNK app.

Why should you avoid selling marked bills to Dunk?

Corroborated reports note that Dunk's Trailer in Port Gellhorn pays lower rates for narcotics/jewelry and very low returns on marked bills.
