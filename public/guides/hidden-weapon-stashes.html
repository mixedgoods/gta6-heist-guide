Frequently Asked Questions

Where do you get the Moreland 850 Shotgun for free?

Grab the Moreland 850 Shotgun directly off the bed during the Starlet Motel raid in Chapter 2's Checking Out mission.

How do you unlock Bret's SM-69 rifle without buying a sniper at Ammu-Nation?

Complete Chapter 2's Coffee Break, visit Bret Vogel at Killtelligence Provisions in Mount Kalaga, and progress through Veteran Hunted and his Hunting Target Decks.

Do free picked-up weapons count toward the 50 of 66 Weapons Compendium requirement?

Yes—every distinct weapon you pick up and equip logs toward the 50-weapon 100% completion threshold.
