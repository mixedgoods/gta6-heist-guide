How to Spot, Break & Track Cocaine Flamingos Efficiently

  1. Check the Beak Before Shooting: Aim down sights at any pink lawn flamingo. Real Cocaine Flamingos always have a black-and-white beak; regular yard decorations have a black-and-yellow beak and will not shatter.
  2. Use a Silenced Pistol to Avoid Police Heat: Shoot the torso of the flamingo with a suppressed handgun so suburban neighborhoods in the Leonida Keys, Port Gellhorn, and Vice City suburbs don't trigger unnecessary VCPD wanted levels.
  3. Collect & Fence the Cocaine Package: Walk over the shattered lawn ornament to pocket the hidden cocaine brick. Sell accumulated packages to unlocked stolen-goods fences (such as Dunk or Tyrone) for extra cash.
  4. Hit 50 for the Cheat, 100 for 100%: Reaching 50 destroyed unlocks the Inflatable Flamingo vehicle spawn cheat (save before testing any cheat since cheats disable saving/trophies). Reaching 100/125 checks off the category on your 100% tracker—giving you a generous 25-flamingo cushion.
  5. Track Progress in Pause › Stats / Map › Progress: Check your running Cocaine Flamingo tally in the in-game Progress menu and mark off your 100% milestones in our 100% Completion Tracker.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can you tell a real Cocaine Flamingo from a normal lawn flamingo in GTA 6?

Real Cocaine Flamingos have a black-and-white beak. Regular decorative lawn flamingos have a black-and-yellow beak and cannot be destroyed.

Do you need all 125 Cocaine Flamingos for 100% completion?

No. You only need to find and destroy 100 out of the 125 Cocaine Flamingos to satisfy the 100% Completion Checklist.

What do you unlock at 50 Cocaine Flamingos?

Destroying 50 Cocaine Flamingos unlocks the Inflatable Flamingo vehicle cheat. Remember to manual-save before using any cheat because cheats disable trophies and saving.
