What Is Permanently Missable (Cannot Wait Until Post-Game)

Story Fences & Safehouses

Donnie's Trailer Fence closes permanently after Swamped (Ch. 1), and the Starlet Motel base closes after Checking Out (Ch. 2).

Relationship & Trophy Triggers

Pre-prison errands in Coming Home and yacht choices in Day Release feed into the Hot Together (4/4) relationship trophy. Keep a Chapter 1 backup in the 15-Slot Save Planner.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you still reach 100% completion after finishing Chapter 5?

Yes. All major 100% tracker categories—Cocaine Flamingos (100/125), Card Sets (15/18), Underwater Discoveries (33), Kenton Shipwrecks (5/7), WAiNK scans (100), stunts, and the Vehicles/Weapons/Animals compendiums—are completable in post-game free roam.

Do unconfirmed reference animals in animals.json count toward the 100 Animals requirement?

No. Our 100% Completion Tracker only counts verified in-game species (gta6PresenceConfirmed: true) and excludes real-world reference entries.

What is the best order for post-game cleanup?

Start with WAiNK 100 scans + 200 Vehicles (they overlap naturally in Vice City traffic), then clear Bret Vogel's 15 Hunting Target Decks alongside the 100 Animals Compendium, and finish with underwater salvage and aerial stunts.
