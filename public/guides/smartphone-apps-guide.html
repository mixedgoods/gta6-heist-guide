  1. Check Texts Before Chapter Cutoffs: Always read new texts from Cal, Brian, and Port Gellhorn contacts before starting cutoff missions like Swamped or Tourist Trap.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you open the smartphone in GTA 6?

Community-reported Default controls open the smartphone with D-pad Up on both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

How do you unlock the WAiNK and Scooter Bros apps?

Both the WAiNK vehicle tracker scanner and the Scooter Bros Personal Vehicle recall app unlock in Chapter 2 by completing Moses at Watkins Auto Parts.

How do you open the cheat terminal on the GTA 6 phone?

Open your smartphone with D-pad Up, then hold L1/LB and tap R1/RB. Remember that activating cheats disables trophies and saving for that session.
