Vice City Nightclub & NINE1NINE Side Encounters Guide (Cards & Secret Radio)

Vice City’s club district comes alive once you enter Chapter 3. Between Boobie Ike’s Crosstown venue (The Jack of Hearts) and Downtown Vice City’s flagship mega-club NINE1NINE, nightlife venues tie directly into character hangouts, VIP activities, the NINE1NINE Dancers Card collectible set, and underground music discoveries.

Vice City Nightlife Quick Facts

Flagship NightclubNINE1NINE (Downtown Vice City / south of Crosstown — modeled on Miami’s 24-hour mega-clubs)
Story Hub VenueThe Jack of Hearts (Crosstown, Vice City — owned by Boobie Ike, introduced in Welcome to Vice City)
In-Club ActivitiesDrinks at the bar, tipping dancers, and $300 private VIP dances (PC Gamer / RadioTimes reported)
Connected Collectible SetNINE1NINE Dancers Card set (36–37 trading cards scattered across Leonida; counts toward 15 of 18 Collectible Card Sets for 100%)
Secret Radio Tie-InVice City club posters and nightlife walls connect to unlocking secret radio stations (see Poster Scans guide)
Unlock WindowFull Vice City club free roam opens in Chapter 3 after Welcome to Vice City (earlier preview during Day Release)

How to Explore Vice City Nightlife & Maximize Unlocks

  1. Reach Chapter 3 for Unrestricted Club Access: Complete Checking Out and Welcome to Vice City to move into Bayside Apartments right beside Crosstown and Downtown.
  2. Visit NINE1NINE South of Crosstown: Step inside NINE1NINE to explore the main floor, order drinks, tip performers, or pay $300 for VIP room interactions.
  3. Hunt the NINE1NINE Dancers Card Set: Keep Focus Mode ready whenever you pass Vice City club alleys, hotel lobbies, and entertainment venues—the NINE1NINE Dancers cards form one of the 18 Collectible Card Sets (you need 15 of 18 sets for 100% completion).
  4. Scan Nightlife Posters for Secret Radio: While touring Vice City’s club corridors at night, watch exterior walls and alleys for underground music posters that unlock hidden frequencies like The Vault Radio.
  5. Return to The Jack of Hearts for Story Beats: Check your map regularly for Dre’Quan Priest and Boobie Ike icons at The Jack of Hearts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the NINE1NINE nightclub in GTA 6?

NINE1NINE is located in Downtown Vice City, just south of Crosstown.

What collectibles are tied to NINE1NINE?

NINE1NINE is tied to the NINE1NINE Dancers trading card set (36–37 cards hidden around Leonida), which counts toward the Collectible Card Sets requirement for 100% completion.

How much are VIP dances at NINE1NINE?

Corroborated launch guides report that private VIP dances at NINE1NINE cost $300.
