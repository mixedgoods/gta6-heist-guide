Randy’s High-End Vehicle Wish-List Jobs Guide (Balsero, Vice City)

Randy is one of GTA 6’s two dedicated stolen-vehicle buyers (alongside Moses). Unlocked in Chapter 3, Randy focuses on targeted high-end wish-list vehicles rather than everyday untracked street cars—making his drop-off one of the cleanest mid-game vehicle-theft income streams in Vice City.

Step-by-Step Wish-List Delivery Loop

  1. Unlock Vehicle Theft Tools in Chapter 2 First: Complete Moses at Watkins Auto Parts so you already own the Slim Jim and WAiNK scanner before entering Chapter 3.
  2. Unlock Randy in Chapter 3 (Balsero, Vice City): Advance past Welcome to Vice City until Randy’s contact and wish-list requests become active.
  3. Scout High-End Districts & Scan with WAiNK: Patrol affluent Vice City neighborhoods (Vice Beach, Ocean Beach, Downtown, Star Island approaches) and scan candidate sports/super cars with WAiNK to identify requested targets and tracker status.
  4. Steal Cleanly & Avoid Heavy Body Damage: Use your Slim Jim for quiet entry, clear any pursuing heat before approaching the drop-off, and avoid wrecking the bodywork on the drive to Balsero so you preserve full payout value.
  5. Deliver & Bank Your Earnings: Drop the vehicle at Randy’s bay and route excess pocket cash into your BuckMe account or safehouse stash to protect it from Wasted penalties.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is Randy different from Moses in GTA 6?

Moses (Chapter 2, Watkins Auto Parts) buys standard untracked vehicles, whereas Randy (Chapter 3, Balsero) specifically pays for requested high-end wish-list vehicles.

Where is Randy located in GTA 6?

Corroborated post-launch reporting places Randy in Balsero, Vice City during Chapter 3.

Do you need the WAiNK app for Randy’s wish-list cars?

Yes—using WAiNK (unlocked during Moses in Chapter 2) lets you verify whether a target car is tracked before you risk a high-heat Vice City chase.
