Chapter 3 Vice City Connections & Story Role

The Jack of Hearts Hub

Boobie's strip club, The Jack of Hearts, serves as a major Vice City nightlife anchor during Chapter 3: Tits, Ass, Drug Money—tying directly into Dre'Quan Priest's club introduction mission.

Only Raw Records Backing

While Dre'Quan handles day-to-day music hustling and signing viral rap duo Real Dimez (Bae-Luxe & Roxy), Boobie provides the capital, studio infrastructure, and street-to-boardroom legitimacy in Vice City.

  1. Advance past Port Gellhorn's Checking Out finale to reach Vice City in Chapter 3, where Boobie Ike and Dre'Quan Priest's storyline opens up.
  2. Watch for missions centered on The Jack of Hearts and Only Raw Records as Jason and Lucia establish their Vice City foothold.
  3. Do not expect Boobie to act as one of the 7 free-roam fences—use our All 7 Fences & Vehicle Buyers directory (Tyrone, Yasmani, Randy) for Vice City loot liquidation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Boobie Ike in GTA 6?

Boobie Ike is a Rockstar-confirmed Vice City legend who turned his street background into a legitimate empire covering real estate, the Jack of Hearts strip club, and Only Raw Records.

How are Boobie Ike and Dre'Quan Priest connected?

Boobie Ike is partnered with young music mogul Dre'Quan Priest in Only Raw Records, backing the studio and its roster while Dre'Quan pushes acts like Real Dimez.

Can players buy Boobie Ike's strip club or studio in GTA 6 Story Mode?

No—Story Mode allows purchasing garages (8 purchasable garages plus story/bonus garages), not passive-income clubs or recording studios.
