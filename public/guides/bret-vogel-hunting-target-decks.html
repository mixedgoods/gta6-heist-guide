Bret Vogel's 15 Hunting Target Decks Guide: Unlock the Killtelligence SM-69 Rifle

To start Bret Vogel's 15 Hunting Target Decks (required for 100% completion), finish Chapter 2's Coffee Break, switch to Jason Duval, and visit Killtelligence Provisions in Mount Kalaga (Lummox County) to complete Veteran Hunted. Delivering Good or Pristine carcasses using Focus Mode (L3 + R3) earns Deck Cash Payouts and the unique Killtelligence SM-69 Hunting Rifle.

How to Unlock Bret Vogel's Hunting Target Decks

  1. Complete Coffee Break (Chapter 2): Finish Coffee Break at the Starlet Motel to unlock free character switching between Jason and Lucia.
  2. Switch to Jason Duval: Bret Vogel's hunting progression is Jason's exclusive questline.
  3. Travel to Killtelligence Provisions (Mount Kalaga): Drive north into Lummox County (Mount Kalaga National Park Guide) and start Veteran Hunted.
  4. Earn the Killtelligence SM-69 & Track All 15 Decks: Completing the intro equips you for the 15 Target Decks and links directly into your 100% Completion Tracker.

How to Guarantee Good & Pristine Carcasses

Focus Mode Vital Shots (L3 + R3): Always trigger Focus Mode before firing to highlight an animal's vital zones (skull or heart/lung cluster). A single clean rifle shot preserves Pristine quality; rapid-fire automatic sprays, shotguns, or vehicle collisions ruin carcass value.

Match Caliber to Animal Size

Use the Killtelligence SM-69 or Bolt-Action Hunting Rifle for large predators (Alligators, Black Bears, Panthers, Bobcats). Avoid explosives or heavy battle rifles that shred pelts.

Scout With Binoculars First

Use binoculars in free roam to study new species before shooting—this logs them toward both your 100-species wildlife requirement and the 60/62 Birds Compendium.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Bret Vogel's 15 Hunting Target Decks required for 100% completion?

Yes. Completing all 15 Hunting Target Decks is explicitly flagged as required for 100% completion in our animals and 100% tracker datasets.

Can Lucia complete Bret Vogel's Target Decks?

No—Veteran Hunted and Bret Vogel's Hunting Target Decks are tracked under Jason Duval after Chapter 2's Coffee Break.

What weapon do you get from Bret Vogel?

You unlock Bret's SM-69 (the Killtelligence SM-69 Hunting Rifle) alongside deck cash payouts.
