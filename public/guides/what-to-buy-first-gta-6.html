GTA 6 Early-Game Spending Guide: What to Buy First (Chapters 1–3)

Early-game money in GTA 6 is tighter than in late-game Vice City because Chapters 1 and 2 have explicit cash milestones (the $5,000 BuckMe Family Savings goal and the $15,000 Checking Out requirement). Follow this prioritized buying order so you never stall story progression or waste cash on items you can unlock for free.

Verified Priority Order: What to Spend Cash On First

  1. Chapter 1 Missable Errands ($475 Necklace + Groceries/Flowers): Before driving to Hamlet Prison in Coming Home, spend $475 at The Siren's Chest and buy Jason's errand supplies for permanent Relationship progress.
  2. Hit the $5,000 BuckMe Goal & Keep Body Armor Stocked: Bank spare Chapter 1 cash into BuckMe Family Savings and buy basic body armor/ammo at Ammu-Nation before story gunfights.
  3. Clear the $15,000 Chapter 2 Checking Out Gate: Don't blow Chapter 2 cash on cosmetics before funding Checking Out (see How to Make $15,000 Fast).
  4. Buy Suppressors & Practical Weapon Attachments: Once in Vice City (Chapter 3), prioritize a suppressed pistol/carbine for clean robberies (Attachments & Ammo Guide).
  5. Buy Strategic Vice City Garages First: After unlocking the real-estate app, prioritize Little Cuba Lockup Garage or Downtown Vice Parking Bay for Chapter 3–4 heist staging (All 13 Garages).

Three Things You Should NOT Buy Early

Skip Buying Early Hunting Rifles & Shotguns

You earn Bret's SM-69 Hunting Rifle for free in Veteran Hunted and can pick up the Moreland 850 Shotgun right off the bed during Checking Out.

Skip Upgrading Unregistered Street Cars

Wait until Moses unlocks the WAiNK scanner and Watkins Auto Parts registration so you can claim a great untracked street vehicle for free instead of sinking cash into a temporary car.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the very first thing worth buying in Chapter 1?

Buy the $475 necklace at The Siren's Chest and Jason's Key Lento errand supplies before triggering Coming Home so you don't miss permanent early Relationship boosts.

How much cash should I keep saved during Chapter 2?

Prioritize reaching $15,000 in Port Gellhorn to clear the Checking Out Chapter 2 finale requirement before spending heavily on garages or cosmetics.

Which purchasable garage should I buy first?

Little Cuba Lockup Garage or Downtown Vice Parking Bay give the strongest Chapter 3–4 Vice City heist staging value.
