Unlocking the 8 Purchasable Garages

Real-Estate App Unlock

Progress through Chapter 2 until you complete Daily Driver, which unlocks the smartphone real-estate app allowing you to buy all 8 standalone garages across Leonida's 6 regions.

Best First Garages to Buy

Prioritize Port Gellhorn Strip-Mall Garage in Chapter 2 and Downtown Vice Parking Bay or Little Cuba Lockup Garage in Chapter 3 so your heist getaway cars are always close to bank routes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many garages can you own in GTA 6 Story Mode?

There are 13 total garages in our verified database: 8 purchasable garages, 4 story safehouse garages, and 1 Ultimate Edition bonus garage (Paradise Garage).

When can you start buying garages in GTA 6?

The smartphone real-estate app for the 8 purchasable garages unlocks in Chapter 2 after completing Daily Driver.

What is Paradise Garage in Watson Bay?

Paradise Garage is the Ultimate Edition bonus multi-vehicle garage with a built-in gun cabinet located in Watson Bay, Vice City.
