Fast Cash Safe Code — Direct Answer (Randomized, No Universal PIN)

RANDOMIZED FREE-ROAM SAFE Fast Cash is a specific robbable pawn shop located in Little Cuba, Vice City (Vice-Dale County)—one of the 15 Smash 'n' Grab pawn shops unlocked in Chapter 2 after completing Coffee Break. Its back-room safe does NOT have a universal fixed code: the 4-digit combination randomizes per playthrough and changes again if you rob the shop a second time.

  • Method 1 — Intimidate the Clerk (Fastest): Aim your weapon at the Fast Cash employee behind the counter and press the threat prompt (Square on PS5 / X on Xbox) until the "Safe Code?" prompt appears—the clerk will give you your active combination.
  • Method 2 — Scan the Back Office with Focus Mode (L3+R3): Click both thumbsticks in the back room to highlight sticky notes, desk papers, clipboards, or monitors displaying the current code.
  • Method 3 — Lockpick or Sticky Bomb: Smaller safes can be lockpicked or breached with a sticky bomb (unlocked mid-game, e.g., after Credit Checked), though explosives trigger alarms and immediate police response.
  • Story Missions vs. Free-Roam Rule: Only scripted story keypads (such as Lab Fees 7700 / 0420) and the 10 Gang-Operation safes below use fixed codes. Free-roam pawn shops like Fast Cash always randomize.

Randomized Free-Roam Robbery Safes (Why No Fixed Code Works)

RANDOMIZED PER PLAYTHROUGH Standard free-roam convenience stores, pawn shops, jewelry counters, and small local bank back-office safes do not share a universal fixed code across saves. If a guide claims a single fixed code for every Pawn Shop or Gas Station safe, it will fail in your game.

How to Find the Active Code for Any Randomized Safe

  • Intimidate / Interrogate Store Employees: Aim at the store clerk or manager and use the threat prompt (Square on PS5 / X on Xbox) until the "Safe Code?" option appears to force them to reveal the active combination before VCPD arrives.
  • Use Focus Mode (L3+R3) in the Back Office: Activate Focus Mode near desks, registers, computer monitors, and wall corkboards to highlight sticky notes or documents carrying the 4-digit PIN.
  • Alternative Forced Entry: Smaller safes can be lockpicked or blown open with sticky bombs once unlocked via story progression, though loud breaches trigger immediate alarms.
