Cooling Down Hot Vehicles, WAiNK Trackers & Vehicle Swap Guide

Stealing a car in GTA 6 is no longer a simple smash-and-drive. Unlocked during Chapter 2's Moses mission, the WAiNK scanner and Slim Jim workflow determine whether a stolen vehicle is tracked, sellable, or safe to keep. Here is how to cool down hot vehicles and swap cleanly after a heist.

Reported Steps for Managing Hot & Stolen Vehicles

  1. Scan with the WAiNK app before committing: After completing Moses, scan parked cars with WAiNK to check tracker status before stealing them—untracked targets save you immediate heat and can be fenced or stored cleanly.
  2. Stage a clean swap vehicle 2 blocks from major robberies: After a Large Bank Vault or Jewelry Store hit, ditch the spotted getaway car out of police line-of-sight and transfer into a clean personal or garage-staged vehicle.
  3. Register or store keepers via Moses & Owned Garages: Corroborated guides report two complementary paths: delivering eligible untracked cars to Moses at Watkins Auto Parts, and parking vehicles inside garages unlocked after Chapter 2's Daily Driver (starting with the free Port Gellhorn Station Garage and additional bays bought via the Vice City Real Estate app). See our WAiNK & Registration Guide.
  4. Never drive a live wanted-level vehicle straight to a fence: Clear your wanted status completely before pulling into Moses, Dunk, or Randy's garage.
Factual Safeguard: Do not assume that driving into a mod shop (Rideout Customs or One-Eyed Willie's) while directly inside an active police visual cone automatically erases a high wanted level. Break line-of-sight physically first. Note also that exact bounty-payoff mechanics remain unconfirmed in Story Mode.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you keep a stolen car as a Personal Vehicle in GTA 6?

Complete Chapter 2's Moses mission to unlock the WAiNK scanner and Slim Jim, steal an untracked vehicle, and bring it to Moses at Watkins Auto Parts for clean registration.

Do I have to lose my wanted level before visiting a vehicle buyer or fence?

Yes—always break police line-of-sight and let your wanted level clear completely before delivering stolen cars to Moses or Randy.

Where can I safely swap into a clean getaway car after a heist?

Park a secondary vehicle inside or near one of your 8 purchasable garages (such as Downtown Vice Parking Bay or Port Gellhorn Strip-Mall Garage) outside the immediate crime perimeter.
