Tow Truck & Repo Side Activity Guide: Where to Find Tow Trucks & Winch Tactics

Heavy tow trucks in GTA 6 are far more than background traffic. Equipped with a working rear winch, they let players haul vehicles out of tight spots, pull freestanding ATMs straight out of storefronts, and experiment with physics-driven free-roam heists across the Keys, Port Gellhorn, and Vice City.

How to Source & Use a Tow Truck in Free Roam

  1. Grab an Early Tow Truck in the Keys or Impound: Check around Nifty Threads Island Wear in the Leonida Keys or scout local police impound yards (notably seen during Unwanted Attention) and Port Gellhorn industrial strips.
  2. Position & Hook the Winch: Reverse squarely toward your target—either a disabled vehicle you want to haul back to a safe alley or a freestanding exterior ATM—and follow the on-screen winch hook prompt.
  3. Pull Cleanly & Clear the Street: Accelerate firmly to yank a freestanding ATM free from its mount. Be ready for immediate witness calls and a fast wanted level; drag the unit into a secluded alley or industrial yard rather than trying to crack it on a main avenue.
  4. Crack the Unit Safely: In Chapters 1–2 (prior to unlocking the Dye Pack Jammer in Chapter 3), use explosives to bust open a ripped-out ATM from a safe distance, collect the cash, and ditch the slow tow truck for a faster getaway vehicle.
  5. Hauling Untracked Cars: If you are towing a damaged vehicle for cover or transport, remember that Moses and Randy still require clean delivery to their bays—see Moses Vehicle Jobs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can you find a tow truck early in GTA 6?

Corroborated launch guides report early tow trucks in the Leonida Keys near Nifty Threads Island Wear and inside police impound yards during Unwanted Attention.

Can you really rip out ATMs with a tow truck in GTA 6?

Yes. Back a tow truck up to a freestanding ATM, hook the winch, and accelerate to yank it free, then crack it open with explosives in a secluded area.

Does GTA 6 have dedicated tow-truck repo missions like GTA V?

No standalone Story Mode repo mission chain has been verified yet; tow trucks currently function as free-roam utility and ATM-heist sandbox tools.
