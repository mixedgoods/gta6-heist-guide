GTA 6 Vehicle Impound & Lost Car Recovery Guide

In GTA 6, a registered Personal Vehicle is never permanently lost—even if it is blown up, sunk, or impounded by police. Recover impounded or wrecked Personal Vehicles through the Scooter Bros app, by calling Joey, or by breaking directly into the police impound lot. Random unowned street cars, however, have no recovery protection and vanish permanently if destroyed.

Vehicle Recovery Rules — Personal vs. Unowned

Personal Vehicles (Destroyed)Never permanently lost — call Joey or use Scooter Bros to repair and deliver with all upgrades intact
Personal Vehicles (Impounded)Check Map Index → Personal or Scooter Bros; pay the retrieval fee OR sneak into the police impound lot
Unowned / Stolen CarsNo insurance — last driven car stays marked briefly, but is gone forever if destroyed or despawned
Trunk Stash WarningLoose stash bags or loot stored in a stolen car’s trunk are lost if you get busted or wasted
Verification BasisIndependently corroborated across GTABase, IGN, Space4Games, GamesRadar, and GameSpot.

3 Ways to Recover an Impounded or Wrecked Car

  1. Method 1 — Scooter Bros App (Fastest Legal Recovery): Unlocked during Moses in Chapter 2. Open Scooter Bros on your smartphone to locate any impounded Personal Vehicle and pay a retrieval fee to have it delivered straight to your street.
  2. Method 2 — Call Joey (Destroyed Vehicle Claims): If your registered car exploded or sank in the Keys, open your phone contacts and call Joey (or order via Scooter Bros) to recover the vehicle with every custom part preserved.
  3. Method 3 — Break Into the Police Impound Lot (Free, High Risk): Open the map, check the Personal index tab to pinpoint the holding lot, hop the fence, and take out security cameras or alarms with a silenced weapon before forcing the gate and driving out.
  4. Check Safehouse & Real Estate Garages: Misplaced an extra registered car? Check your safehouse bays or garages purchased via the Vice City Real Estate app—extra fleet vehicles auto-return to storage.

Protect Heist Loot Before Police Chases

Trunk Safety Rule: Never leave fenceable jewelry or contraband bags sitting in an unregistered street car. Register your dedicated getaway ride first using our How to Buy & Register Cars Guide and Best Getaway Vehicles Guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you permanently lose your Personal Vehicle in GTA 6?

No. Registered Personal Vehicles are never permanently lost when blown up or sunk; call Joey or use the Scooter Bros app to restore them with all mods intact.

How do you get a car out of impound without paying?

Locate the impound lot via Map Index > Personal, hop the fence, disable cameras/alarms with a suppressed weapon, and drive your car out.

What happens to an unregistered stolen car if it gets destroyed?

Unowned street vehicles have no insurance and are permanently lost once destroyed or despawned.
