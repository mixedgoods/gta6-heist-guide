Why the GTA 6 Birds Tab Lists 60 vs 62 Bird Species Explained

Completionists checking Pause Menu → Progress → Wildlife & Birds quickly spot a discrepancy: the in-game Birds tab displays 60 entries, even though Rockstar's developer figure cites 62 bird species across Leonida. Here is why the numbers differ and what it means for 100% completion.

The 60 vs. 62 Bird Count Discrepancy Explained

Verified Finding (from data/discoveries.json): The in-game Progress menu's dedicated Birds tab displays 60 standard bird entries, while a couple of specialized/distinct hunting-category birds are tracked outside the main 60-slot Birds sub-tab—accounting for Rockstar's 62 total bird species figure.

Equip your Binoculars along the Leonida Keys shoreline, Grassrivers marsh channels, Vice Beach piers, and Mount Kalaga cliff overlooks. Frequently Asked Questions

Is my GTA 6 Birds tab glitched if it only shows 60 birds?

No. The in-game Birds tab intentionally lists 60 standard entries while specialized birds are categorized separately under hunting/wildlife tracking.

Do I need all 60 or 62 birds for 100% completion?

No. GTA 6's 100% completion requirement asks for 100 of 255 total animal species across land, marine, domestic, and bird categories.

What is the easiest way to log flying birds?

Use Binoculars in free roam to study perched or soaring birds from a distance without frightening them away.
