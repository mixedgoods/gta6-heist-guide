Region-by-Region Survival Tactics

Grassrivers Wetlands

Stay aboard a Grassrivers Swamp Airboat when crossing deep channels. Scan muddy banks with Focus Mode (L3 + R3) before wading ashore near Sunrise RV Park or marsh camps.

Mount Kalaga Pine Ridges

Florida Panthers prefer dusk and night patrols. Keep Bret's SM-69 ready at medium range and never sprint blindly through tight creek crossings without scanning ahead.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the confirmed hostile predators in GTA 6?

American Alligators in Grassrivers and Florida Panthers in Mount Kalaga National Park are confirmed predators, alongside venomous Marsh Snakes and Red Lionfish.

Are Great White Sharks or Black Bears confirmed in GTA 6?

They are tracked as unconfirmed real-world reference entries in our compendium until independently verified in-game.

How do you survive an Alligator or Panther encounter?

Use Focus Mode (L3 + R3) early to spot them, keep distance using airboats or off-road vehicles, and place a single vital shot with a rifle or heavy revolver.
