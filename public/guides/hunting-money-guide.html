Clean-Kill Workflow for Pristine Payouts

  1. Unlock Bret's SM-69 first: Complete Veteran Hunted right after Chapter 2's Coffee Break so you get the dedicated Killtelligence SM-69 Rifle for free.
  2. Always trigger Focus Mode (L3 + R3): Focus Mode highlights animal vital organs so a single clean shot preserves carcass quality instead of ruining the pelt.
  3. Stack hunting with 100% completion: Study every new species before shooting so your hunting sessions double as progress toward the 100 of 255 Animals trophy and 100% milestone.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do Bret Vogel's Hunting Target Decks unlock?

They unlock in Chapter 2 right after Coffee Break once Jason completes the Veteran Hunted side mission at Killtelligence Provisions in Mount Kalaga.

Are Bret Vogel's 15 Hunting Target Decks infinitely repeatable?

No. The 15 Target Decks are one-time structured progression checklists with Deck payouts and the SM-69 rifle unlock; general wildlife hunting in free roam is repeatable.

What is the best rifle for Pristine animal kills?

Bret's Killtelligence SM-69 Hunting Rifle paired with Focus Mode (L3 + R3) vital-organ targeting gives the cleanest single-shot takedowns.
