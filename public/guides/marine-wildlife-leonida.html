Which marine animals are confirmed in GTA 6?

Do marine animals count toward the 100% Animals Compendium?

Yes. Confirmed marine species like Bottlenose Dolphins, Green Sea Turtles, and Red Lionfish count toward the 100 of 255 Animals compendium milestone.
