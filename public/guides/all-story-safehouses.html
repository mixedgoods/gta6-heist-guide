Critical Safehouse Closures & Save Planner Tips

Chapter 1: Jason's House Errands

Before picking up Lucia in Coming Home, complete optional repairs around Jason's Key Lento stilt house for missable relationship boosts.

Chapter 2: Starlet Motel Raid Cutoff

Grab the collectible card on the Starlet Motel fridge and clear Port Gellhorn prep before launching Checking Out, which triggers the raid that closes the motel base.

Can You Buy Houses in Story Mode? No—our Property Ownership Guide confirms 0 purchasable residential houses in Story Mode; real estate purchases focus on the 8 purchasable garages unlocked after Daily Driver.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many safehouses are in GTA 6 Story Mode?

There are 4 confirmed Story Safehouses unlocked automatically across Chapters 1 through 4.

Can you buy houses or apartments in GTA 6 Story Mode?

No, Story Mode has 0 purchasable houses; players unlock 4 story safehouses plus 8 purchasable garages.

Which safehouse closes permanently during the story?

The Chapter 2 Starlet Motel base in Port Gellhorn is raided and lost during the Chapter 2 finale mission, Checking Out.
