GTA 6 Character Switching Guide: When It Unlocks & Duo Strategy

Free open-world switching between Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos does not start unlocked at the beginning of Chapter 1. You permanently unlock free character switching during Chapter 2's Coffee Break mission at the Starlet Motel.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do you unlock free character switching in GTA 6?

Free character switching between Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos unlocks permanently in Chapter 2 upon completing Coffee Break at the Starlet Motel.

Why can't I switch characters in Chapter 1?

Chapter 1 locks protagonist control to story progression while Jason prepares for Lucia's release from Hamlet Prison and the duo works through the Keys finale.

What else unlocks alongside character switching in Coffee Break?

Completing Coffee Break also unlocks Jason's Veteran Hunted side mission, which introduces Bret Vogel's 15 Hunting Target Decks at Killtelligence Provisions.
