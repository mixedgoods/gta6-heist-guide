GTA 6 Jewelry & Pawn Shop Farming Loop: Verified Targets & Fencing

Combining pawn shops and jewelry stores gives you a reliable, patch-proof free-roam cash engine from Chapter 2 through the endgame. Because GTA 6 tracks 14 mapped jewelry stores and multiple regional pawn outlets, the real profit edge comes from matching each haul to the right fence—Dunk in Port Gellhorn or Yasmani in Vice City.

4-Step Clean Jewelry & Pawn Execution

  1. Stage Your Getaway First: Park a 4-door or trunk-equipped car facing the nearest alley exit—never park nose-in against a storefront curb.
  2. Equip Mask & Blunt Melee: Put on a mask before entering and use a Baseball Bat or Heavy Crowbar to shatter display cases without firing loud indoor shots.
  3. Beat the Dispatch Window: Grab high-value trays immediately and exit before VCPD units box in the block; drop your mask and swap vehicles out of sight once clear.
  4. Match Loot to Fence: Liquidate Port Gellhorn goods at Dunk only as needed for Chapter 2's $15,000 gate, and route Vice City jewelry hauls to Yasmani for stronger returns.
