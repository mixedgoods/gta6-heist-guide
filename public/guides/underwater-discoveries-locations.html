How to Complete All 33 Underwater Discoveries

  1. Unlock Captain Kenton in Chapter 1: Finish Island Dreams in the Leonida Keys so Captain Kenton's shop opens and gives you access to scuba charters and the Metal Detector.
  2. Take a Boat to Purple Chest Dive Markers: Pilot a boat to the marked coastal dive zones (such as the Urca De Santa Sierra shipwreck east of Key Lento), drop anchor/stop, and equip your scuba gear before diving.
  3. Follow the Metal Detector Light & Beep Cadence: Equip the Metal Detector underwater. A green light and faster beeping confirm you are pointing directly toward buried artifacts or sunken chests along the seabed.
  4. Clear Every Site Completely (33/33): Because 100% completion demands all 33 Underwater Discoveries—with zero skip allowance—sweep the entire wreck perimeter until the local discovery registers before heading back to the boat.
  5. Turn In Artifacts to Kenton for Cash: Bring recovered shipwreck items back to Kenton's Key Lento shop for $200 per item plus full-set completion bonuses (detailed in our Underwater Treasure Profit Guide).

Zero-Buffer 100% Category

All 33 Sites Count: While you only need 5 of 7 Kenton shipwreck sets and 100 of 125 Cocaine Flamingos, corroborated 100% checklists (PowerPyx / IGN) confirm you need 33 of 33 Underwater Discoveries.
