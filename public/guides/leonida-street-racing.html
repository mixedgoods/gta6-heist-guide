Leonida Street Racing & Time Trials Guide: All 40 Races & 100% Requirements

Street racing is one of the biggest open-world activity categories in Grand Theft Auto VI, spanning 40 unique street, cross-country, and off-road races across Leonida. You need to complete 30 of the 40 races for 100% completion—plus dedicated time trials at Gellhorn Raceway and the Thrillbilly Mud Club.

Vehicle Ratings, Rentals & Dedicated Venues

Own vs. Rent at the Grid

Every Leonida race checks whether your current ride meets its asphalt or off-road class rating. If you roll up in the wrong vehicle class, you do not have to drive back to a garage—use the on-site vehicle rental option to enter immediately.

Gellhorn Raceway & Thrillbilly Mud Club

Outside the 40 street and cross-country events, head to Gellhorn Raceway for clean circuit time trials and to the Thrillbilly Mud Club in Frogswater for lifted-truck off-road runs.

Fastest Route to 30/40 Races for 100%

Because you only need 30 of the 40 races for 100% completion, prioritize your strongest vehicle class first (either an upgraded sports/super coupe for Vice City asphalt loops or a 4x4/off-road build for Kelly County dirt sprints) and skip any frustrating rain-slicked mountain tracks until last. Frequently Asked Questions

How many street races are in GTA 6?

Corroborated guides report 40 unique street, cross-country, and off-road races across Leonida, with 30 required for 100% completion.

Do you need to own a sports car to enter GTA 6 street races?

No. If your vehicle does not match the required asphalt or off-road rating, you can rent an eligible vehicle at the race start.
