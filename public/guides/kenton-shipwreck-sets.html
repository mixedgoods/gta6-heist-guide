Captain Kenton's 7 Shipwreck & Dive Site Sets: Complete Salvage & Artifact Checklist

Captain Kenton Rees's shipwreck salvage loop unlocks in Chapter 1 right after completing Island Dreams at Island Dreams Boat Charters in Key Lento. Across 7 named dive sites containing 41 underwater artifacts, completing 5 of the 7 sets is required for 100% completion—and turning in full sets to Kenton pays out substantial cash.

How to Unlock & Gear Up for Kenton's Salvage Hunts

  1. Complete Island Dreams (Ch. 1): After finishing Island Dreams for Kenton Rees, wait for his follow-up call/text. A green KR icon appears at Island Dreams Boat Charters in Key Lento (Mariana County), and purple chest markers denote shipwreck dive sites on your map.
  2. Equip Scuba Gear on Your Boat: Sail to a purple dive marker and use the boat's interaction prompt to equip Scuba Gear (granting unlimited underwater oxygen).
  3. Ready Your Metal Detector & Speargun: Pull out your Metal Detector to sweep the seabed and keep the Arras Bullhead speargun slotted in your weapon wheel for shark defense.
  4. Sell Complete Sets to Kenton: Bring full artifact sets back to Kenton's Key Lento shop for maximum cash payout rather than leaving sets unfinished.

Reading the Metal Detector & Handling Sharks

Red-to-Green Signal Cue: Slowly pan your Metal Detector across the ocean floor. Its indicator light shifts from red (no signal) to green as you face a buried relic, with audio pings accelerating until a small yellow chest icon pops up on the seabed for excavation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Captain Kenton shipwreck sets are needed for 100% completion?

There are 7 dive sites / sets (41 total artifacts), and completing 5 of the 7 sets is required for GTA 6's 100% completion checklist.

How do you use the Metal Detector underwater in GTA 6?

Equip Scuba Gear from your boat, select the Metal Detector from the weapon wheel, and sweep the seabed until the indicator shifts from red to green and pings rapidly—then dig up the yellow chest marker.
