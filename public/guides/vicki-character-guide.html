Vicki Character Guide (Port Gellhorn & Girls' Trip)

Vicki is a Chapter 2 supporting character in Port Gellhorn who drags Lucia Caminos into the local street-hustle network during Girls' Trip. Her attempted Zombix pill switch at Delights Strip Club directly introduces Lucia to Port Gellhorn fence Dunk and leads into the Moses vehicle-theft arc.

Why Vicki's Chapter 2 Arc Matters for Progression

Catch the Pill Switch in Girls' Trip

Pay close attention to Vicki during Girls' Trip at Delights. Resolving her scheme cleanly keeps Chapter 2 moving smoothly toward Slam Dunk ($1,700 BuckMe payout and Dunk's Trailer fence).

Gateway to Moses & WAiNK

Completing the early Port Gellhorn chain unlocks Moses, which permanently gives you the Slim Jim, WAiNK vehicle tracker scanner, and Scooter Bros app — essential tools for the entire rest of the game.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which mission introduces Vicki in GTA 6?

Vicki is featured in Chapter 2's Girls' Trip mission in Port Gellhorn alongside Lucia Caminos.

What happens with Vicki at Delights Strip Club?

During Girls' Trip, Lucia has to catch Vicki's Zombix pill switch at Delights, which also brings you face-to-face with Port Gellhorn fence Dunk.

Is Vicki a fence or shopkeeper you can visit in free roam?

No. Vicki is a Chapter 2 story character; the actual Port Gellhorn fence contacts are Dunk (stolen goods) and Moses (untracked vehicles).
