Marquee Story, Combat & Relationship Trophies

Break the Bank & Hi-Jacked (Story)

Break the Bank (Bronze) unlocks automatically upon completing Chapter 3's The Cartel Heist (Mission #71). Hi-Jacked (Bronze) unlocks automatically after Chapter 4's VCIA Takedown ($250,000 airport heist).

Hot Together (Free Roam & Bond)

The Hot Together trophy unlocks by losing a 4-star Wanted Level together with your partner and escaping the heat (pairs naturally with Large Bank Vaults). Separately, maxing your 4/4 bond is required for the Two Years Later best ending.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many trophies are in GTA 6 on PS5?

GTA 6 has 55 PS5 trophies (1 Platinum 'Vice Got a Price!', 2 Gold, 6 Silver, and 46 Bronze) and 54 Xbox achievements.

How do you unlock the Hot Together trophy in GTA 6?

Escape and lose a 4-star Wanted Level together with your partner (Jason and Lucia) in free roam or after a large bank robbery.

Yes—because the launch build has no mission replay menu, keeping manual saves at key Chapter 1–4 cutoffs via our 15-Slot Save Planner protects your Platinum run.
