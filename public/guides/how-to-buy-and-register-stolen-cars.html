How to Buy, Claim & Register Personal Vehicles in GTA 6

Unlike GTA V’s online dealership catalogs, nearly every Personal Vehicle in GTA 6 starts as a car you steal off the street and legally register. Scan targets with the WAiNK app to check tracker tiers and fees, register untracked cars at Moses (Watkins Auto Parts) in Chapter 2, and unlock full Pay ’n’ Spray registration & selling in Chapter 3.

Step-by-Step: Turning a Street Car Into Your Personal Ride

  1. Scan Before You Steal (WAiNK App): Point your smartphone’s WAiNK app at any parked vehicle to read its security/tracker tier, resale payout, and exact registration fee before breaking in.
  2. Use the Slim Jim for Quiet Entry: After completing Moses in Chapter 2, use the Slim Jim on parked vehicles to avoid smashing windows and alerting nearby witnesses.
  3. Register at Moses (Ch. 2) or Pay ’n’ Spray (Ch. 3+): Drive untracked vehicles to Moses’s Port Gellhorn shop in Chapter 2 (your very first registration is free!). Starting in Chapter 3, you can register standard-tracker rides at any of the 19 Pay ’n’ Spray locations.
  4. Summon & Store via Scooter Bros: Once registered, your vehicle is immune to permanent loss and can be summoned anywhere via Scooter Bros or parked in garages bought through Vice City Real Estate.

Don’t Waste Your Free First Registration

Pro Tip: Save your first-time free registration bonus at Moses for a high-value standout vehicle like the untracked LCC Monarch Avarus ($2,100 registration fee normally) or a fast 4-door getaway sedan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there car dealerships where you buy cars in GTA 6?

Traditional catalog car buying is not the main acquisition loop in GTA 6 Story Mode; instead, you steal vehicles off the street, scan them with WAiNK, and register them at Moses (Ch. 2) or Pay 'n' Spray (Ch. 3+).

When can you register tracked vehicles in GTA 6?

Chapter 2 (Moses) handles untracked vehicles; early in Chapter 3, Pay 'n' Spray unlocks registration and selling for vehicles with standard trackers.

How do you expand your garage space?

Purchase additional garages using the Vice City Real Estate app on your in-game smartphone.
