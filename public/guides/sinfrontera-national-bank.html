Vault Security, Silent Entry & Extraction Discipline

Protect the ~11-Minute Window

Going loud in the lobby burns your cell-tower suppression window before you crack the main vault stacks. Use Guard Disguises and suppressed weapons to reach the vault quietly.

Freeway Escape & Immediate Banking

Once bags are full, transition directly to your staged getaway car (Getaway Staging), break pursuit via Vice City freeway interchanges, and deposit your share at a physical ATM or BuckMe terminal.

All 5 Required Raul Bautista Setup Walkthroughs

  1. Raul Setup 1: Cell Tower Recon — Survey comms relay nodes to establish the blackout window.
  2. Raul Setup 2: Vault Blueprints — Acquire Sinfrontera structural plans for thermal placement.
  3. Raul Setup 3: Getaway Staging — Park a fast 4-seat or armored getaway car near the extraction corridor.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the payout for Sinfrontera National Bank in GTA 6?

The Cartel Heist at Sinfrontera National Bank pays a verified $120,000 baseline, with up to $150,000 reported when maximizing vault bag sweeps within the timer.

Can you rob Sinfrontera National Bank in free roam outside the story?

Sinfrontera National Bank is the centerpiece of Chapter 3's scripted Story Heist (The Cartel Heist). For repeatable free-roam bank hits, see our Small Banks & Payday Loans and Large Bank Vaults guides.

How long is the timer inside Sinfrontera National Bank?

Completing Cell Tower Recon buys roughly an 11-minute operational window before heavy police and cartel reinforcements close in.
