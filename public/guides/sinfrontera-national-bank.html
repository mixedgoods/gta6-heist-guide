Interior Zones & Vault Floorplan

1. Main Banking Lobby

Wide street-facing entry with civilian tellers, hostages, and armed security guards. Front steps form the main choke point once VCPD cruisers arrive outside.

2. Upper Mezzanine & Roof Access

Elevated interior overlook and roof access points where security spotters and snipers monitor the street perimeter and call in aerial support if left unchecked.

3. Basement Vault Corridor

Reached via the interior stairwell below the lobby. Features tight corner sightlines, an armored vault guard, and the main thermal-breach vault door housing center-table stacks and side bundles.

4. Exterior Street & Freeway Exit

Open asphalt frontage vulnerable to cruiser crossfire and sniper sightlines; smoke cover and direct routing to Vice City freeway interchanges are the primary exit vectors.

Vault Security & Communications Blackout Window

~11-Minute Cell-Tower Disruption Window: Sinfrontera National Bank's emergency dispatch relies on nearby cellular relay towers. Disrupting those relays delays full VCPD and cartel perimeter encirclement for roughly 11 minutes, giving operatives a bounded window to breach the basement vault before the lobby becomes a siege zone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you enter or rob Sinfrontera National Bank in free roam?

No. Sinfrontera National Bank's vault interior is a scripted Story Mode location tied to Chapter 3's The Cartel Heist (Mission #71) and is not a repeatable free-roam bank robbery.

What are the main levels and security zones inside Sinfrontera National Bank?

The building consists of a street-facing main lobby guarded by armed security, an upper mezzanine and roof access used by spotters, and a lower-level basement vault corridor accessed via the interior stairwell.

How does the 11-minute cell-tower security window work at the bank?

Disabling nearby cell towers creates roughly an 11-minute communications blackout that delays full VCPD and cartel perimeter lockdown while operating inside the vault floor.
