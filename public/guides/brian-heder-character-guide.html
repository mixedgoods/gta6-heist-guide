Brian Heder Character Guide: Keys Landlord & Brian's Lockup (7700)

Brian Heder is Jason Duval's veteran smuggling landlord in the Leonida Keys. He provides Jason's Chapter 1 coastal safehouse, runs Brian's Lockup (unlocked with code during Lab Fees), and anchors one of the game's best early money loops.

Brian's Lockup & The ~$55K Red Barn Money Loop

After you punch 7700 into Brian's Lockup during Lab Fees, the lockup becomes a critical economic hub. Players running the confirmed Apollo Aggregates Red Barn 4-Bag Trunk Loop haul high-value bags from South Vice-Dale County straight back to Brian's Lockup for roughly $55,000 per run.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the keypad code for Brian's Lockup?

The verified keypad code for Brian's Lockup is 7700, introduced during Chapter 1's Lab Fees.

Who is Brian Heder to Jason Duval?

Brian Heder is Jason's landlord and a longtime Leonida Keys smuggler who lets Jason stay at the Key Lento safehouse while running local jobs.

Why is Brian's Lockup important for making money?

Brian's Lockup (7700) is the drop-off point for the Apollo Aggregates Red Barn 4-bag trunk restock loop (~$55K/run).
