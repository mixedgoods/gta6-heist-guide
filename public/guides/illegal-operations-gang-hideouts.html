GTA 6 Illegal Operations Guide (All 39 Gang Hideouts & “Pest Control” Trophy)

Illegal Operations (Gang Hideouts) are repeatable faction raids scattered across Leonida: 39 base-game operations plus 1 Ultimate Edition exclusive (40–41 total map points). Clearing all base operations is required for 100% completion (tracked under Progress > Gangs), and robbing at least one operation from every faction unlocks the “Pest Control” trophy.

How to Clear Illegal Operations Cleanly

  1. Approach “Robbery Opportunity” Markers: Unvisited hideouts display generic money-bag icons until you get close enough to reveal the gang name and compound title.
  2. Loot Every Key Valuable Before Leaving: Killing every guard is not enough—open the safe, grab luxury watches, narcotics, and any yellow-text unique weapon variant so the hideout officially checks off under Progress > Gangs.
  3. Fence Contraband Immediately: Haul narcotics and luxury watches straight to one of Leonida’s 7 Fences before risking a police chase.
  4. Track Respawns Honestly: Cleared hideouts grey out and later refill with guards and standard cash, making them strong repeatable farms alongside our Robbery Cooldown Timers Guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Illegal Operations are in GTA 6?

There are 39 base-game Illegal Operations across Leonida, plus 1 Ultimate Edition exclusive operation.

How do you unlock the "Pest Control" trophy?

Complete at least one Illegal Operation belonging to every criminal gang/faction in Leonida.

Why didn't a gang hideout count as completed?

You must loot all Key Valuables (safes, contraband, and unique items) inside the hideout—not just eliminate the guards.
