GTA 6 Legendary Hunter Challenge & Wildlife Compendium Guide

Under Progress › Challenges › Survival, the Legendary Hunter Challenge tasks players with hunting 8 unique animals across Leonida. Rockstar keeps the 8 exact unique identities concealed in the launch Progress log, while 100% completion requires studying/hunting 100 of 255 total wildlife species.

How the 8 Legendary Hunter Challenge Works

Verification Disclosure: In the launch build, Rockstar conceals the 8 unique target names inside Progress › Challenges › Survival. We do not invent unverified spawn coordinates—use the verified predator habitats below and our interactive tracker on /animals to annotate each unique hunt as you discover it.

Survival Challenge Goal

Kill all 8 unique Legendary Hunter animals across Leonida's biomes (wetlands, highlands, coastal waters, and interior plains).

100% Compendium Requirement

Separate from Legendary hunts, 100% completion requires logging 100 of 255 wildlife species—use binoculars and Focus Mode (L3 + R3) across all 6 regions.

Why the Birds Tab Lists 60 Entries vs. 62 Bird Species

Players completing the wildlife compendium noticed that Pause Menu › Progress › Wildlife & Birds displays 60 bird slots even though developer figures cite 62 bird species across Leonida. Two specialized birds are tracked under separate hunting/challenge buckets rather than the standard Birds tab, so hitting 60/60 on the Birds tab does not mean your save is bugged.

You need to log 100 of the 255 wildlife species across Leonida for 100% completion.

Where do I track the Legendary Hunter Challenge in-game?

Open Pause Menu > Progress > Challenges > Survival to view your 8-animal Legendary Hunter Challenge counter.

Why does the Birds tab only show 60 species instead of 62?

Two specialized bird species are logged under separate hunting categories rather than the standard 60-slot Birds tab.
