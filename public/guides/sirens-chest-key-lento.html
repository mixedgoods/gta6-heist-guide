The Siren's Chest Jewelry Store Guide (Key Lento & $475 Necklace)

The Siren's Chest is a Key Lento jewelry store in the Leonida Keys that plays a pivotal role during Chapter 1's Coming Home preparation errands. Stopping here to purchase the $475 necklace for Lucia Caminos before driving to Hamlet Prison triggers one of GTA 6's earliest permanent Relationship boosts.

Missable Window Warning: If you drive straight to Hamlet Prison without stopping at The Siren's Chest, you miss the Coming Home necklace gift interaction. Make sure Jason has at least $475 on hand before starting the errand sequence.

All Pre-Hamlet Prison Errands to Pair With The Siren's Chest

  1. Repair Jason's Key Lento Safehouse: Fix the roof, steps, and bedroom wall (Key Lento Safehouse Errands Guide).
  2. Pick up groceries, flowers & a haircut: Knock out all personal prep stops around Key Lento.
  3. Buy the $475 necklace at The Siren's Chest: Complete the gift purchase before heading to Hamlet Prison to pick up Lucia.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Lucia's necklace cost at The Siren's Chest?

The necklace costs $475 at The Siren's Chest in Key Lento during Chapter 1's Coming Home errands.

Where is The Siren's Chest located?

The Siren's Chest is located in Key Lento in the Leonida Keys (Mariana County).

Is buying the $475 necklace required for 100% completion?

It is an optional missable errand that permanently boosts Jason and Lucia's Relationship meter toward Hot Together (4/4), though relationship tiers are not mandatory for the Platinum trophy.
