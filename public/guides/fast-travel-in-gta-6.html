Fast Travel in GTA 6: Taxis, RydeMe App & Fastest Ways Across Leonida

Crossing the full State of Leonida—from Key Lento’s southern bridges to Mount Kalaga’s northern trails—takes serious driving time. Corroborated launch guides report that GTA 6 includes reliable taxi and RydeMe app fast travel, allowing you to skip long cross-county drives for a modest fare.

How to Fast Travel Efficiently Across Leonida

  1. Open Your Phone & Launch RydeMe: Tap D-Pad Up to pull up your smartphone and summon a ride through the RydeMe app (or hail a passing street taxi on foot).
  2. Pick a Discovered Destination & Pay the Fare: Select your waypoint or discovered landmark—paying the fare (typically around $100 depending on distance) lets you trigger the skip-journey prompt for instant arrival.
  3. Call Your Car After Arriving: If you fast-traveled across counties without your vehicle, open the Scooter Bros app (unlocked in Moses) to recall your registered personal vehicle.
  4. Use Sleep or Meditation to Shift Time: Need nightfall between 19:00 and 06:00? Sleep at your nearest safehouse instead of idling in free roam.
  5. Never Fast Travel Mid-Easter Egg: During multi-step world sequences like the Vice City Portal Easter Egg (12 LPL substations), drive manually between every stop—fast traveling resets the chain.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does GTA 6 have fast travel?

Yes. Corroborated launch guides report that you can fast travel to discovered locations by hailing taxis or booking a ride through the in-game RydeMe phone app.

How much does RydeMe fast travel cost?

Fares scale with distance and are reported around $100 to skip straight to your destination.

Can I fast travel during missions or with a wanted level?

No—just like previous GTA titles, fast travel is for free roam when you are out of active police chases and mission locks.
