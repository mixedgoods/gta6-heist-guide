GTA 5 & GTA 4 Easter Eggs in GTA 6: Every Corroborated Character Callback & Cameo

Grand Theft Auto VI packs genuine, independently corroborated callbacks to both GTA V and GTA IV—ranging from a radio true-crime podcast dissecting Michael, Franklin, and Trevor's Los Santos heist spree to a physical Jimmy De Santa encounter outside the Jack of Hearts strip club (plus a major spoiler-shielded Chapter 5 cameo).

4 Corroborated GTA 5 Callbacks Across Leonida

1. "Vice City True Stories" Radio Podcast

While driving around Leonida, tune into the in-game true-crime conspiracy podcast. The hosts directly dissect Los Santos's infamous heist trio—naming Michael, Franklin, and "Wade's BFF" (Trevor), questioning Wade's line about "robbing all them banks," and speculating about Floyd and Debra's deaths (GamesRadar / Gfinity corroborated).

2. Jimmy De Santa Outside Jack of Hearts

Michael's son Jimmy De Santa physically appears in Vice City styled as a crypto-bro influencer waiting in line outside The Jack of Hearts strip club. During a bouncer/security sequence, you can choose to let him inside or turn him away—letting him in triggers dialogue referencing his dad.

3. Michael's Missing Boat in the Leonida Keys

Outside a safehouse in the Leonida Keys, players can inspect a moored boat matching the visual profile of Michael De Santa's missing yacht/watercraft from GTA V (Mobalytics corroborated).

4. Michael's Classic Phone Ringtone

Open your in-game smartphone settings and you can equip the classic chime ringtone Michael De Santa used throughout GTA V.

How Not to Miss Jimmy De Santa's Dialogue

Let Him Into the Club: When working the velvet rope outside The Jack of Hearts, choose to admit Jimmy De Santa rather than turning him away if you want to trigger his follow-up Los Santos / Michael dialogue inside.

Major GTA IV Chapter 5 Cameo (Spoiler-Shielded)

Reveal Major Chapter 5 GTA IV Cameo (Contains Late-Game Story Spoilers)

Corroborated across Kotaku, Beebom, GTABase, and G2A: during Chapter 5 (Taking Liberties, tied to Giorgio Gallo's shipping/Coast Guard arc), a visibly older Niko Bellic makes a physical cutscene appearance in Vice City—accompanied by subtle musical nods to GTA IV's iconic Soviet Connection theme—operating as a senior enforcer for a Balkan crime family collecting smuggled cargo.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Michael, Franklin, or Trevor physically appear in GTA 6?

No physical appearances for Michael, Franklin, or Trevor have been corroborated—instead, they are directly referenced on the 'Vice City True Stories' radio podcast, via Michael's Keys boat and ringtone, and through Jimmy De Santa's cameo.

Where do you find Jimmy De Santa in GTA 6?

Jimmy De Santa appears waiting in line outside The Jack of Hearts strip club in Vice City during a bouncer/security sequence.
