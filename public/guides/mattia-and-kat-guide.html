Mattia Tauro & Katerina Rosselli Character Guide (Day Release)

Mattia Tauro and Katerina Rosselli (Kat) are the wealthy Vice City superyacht hosts featured in Chapter 1's Day Release (Mission #17). Their yacht party is one of Chapter 1's highest-value relationship and missable-item stops, awarding free unique sunglasses and a permanent Relationship heart boost if played right.

How to Get Every Reward During Mattia & Kat's Sequence

  1. Talk to the deck railing guests first: After boarding Mattia and Kat's superyacht, speak with the guests near the deck railing before heading below deck so you don't miss the free unique sunglasses.
  2. Pick the tandem jet ski option: When the jet ski race kicks off, have Lucia ride tandem with Jason rather than splitting up to trigger a permanent Relationship heart boost toward Hot Together (4/4).
  3. Follow Mattia ashore cleanly: Wrap up the mission by trailing Mattia into Vice City's nightlife stops (Huxley Motel and Nine1Nine).

Frequently Asked Questions

Who are Mattia and Kat in GTA 6?

Mattia Tauro and Katerina Rosselli (Kat) are the hosts of the Vice City offshore superyacht visited by Jason and Lucia during Chapter 1's Day Release mission.

What is missable on Mattia and Kat's superyacht?

Two things: talking to the deck railing guests before going below deck for free unique sunglasses, and choosing the tandem jet ski ride with Jason and Lucia for a permanent Relationship heart boost.

Can you replay Day Release if you miss the sunglasses?

GTA 6 has no built-in mission replay menu, so keep a manual save in Slot #02/#03 via our 15-Slot Save Planner before starting Day Release.
