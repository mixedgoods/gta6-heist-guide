Before you can launch Chapter 2's finale—Checking Out—you need to clear the $15,000 cash milestone in Port Gellhorn. Here is the fastest verified, zero-glitch Chapter 2 money route using fixed-code safes, story payouts, and untracked vehicle fencing.

Fastest Verified 5-Step Chapter 2 Cash Route

  1. Complete Moses First: Unlocks the WAiNK GPS scanner, Slim Jim, and untracked car fencing at Watkins Auto Parts—turning clean street cars into fast, low-heat cash.
  2. Complete Slam Dunk: Nets a reported $1,700 BuckMe payout and permanently unlocks Dunk's Trailer fence for selling stolen robbery valuables.
  3. Sweep All 3 Port Gellhorn Fixed Safes: Crack Kingpin Bowling (0300), Brooks Boatworks (6823), and Kolt Depot (6969) for guaranteed non-RNG safe cash.
  4. Grab the Grassrivers Kingfishers Safe: Make a quick detour to Sunrise RV Park in Pelican Valley and enter 6769 for a fourth verified fixed-code safe.
  5. Fill the Gap with Pawn Shops & Moses Cars: Hit Port Gellhorn Pawn Shops, fence valuables at Dunk's Trailer, and bank every dollar via physical ATMs or BuckMe so you never bump into the $20,000 pocket cap.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do you need $15,000 in Chapter 2 of GTA 6?

Building $15,000 is the reported Chapter 2 cash progression threshold before launching the Chapter 2 finale, Checking Out.

What is the fastest legitimate way to earn $15,000 in Port Gellhorn?

Combine all 3 fixed-code Kolt & Associates safes (0300, 6823, 6969), the Sunrise RV Park safe (6769), Slam Dunk's payout, Moses untracked car deliveries, and Port Gellhorn pawn shops fenced at Dunk's Trailer.

Should you bank your Chapter 2 cash at ATMs?

Yes—free-roam pocket cash is capped at $20,000, so deposit earnings at Port Gellhorn ATMs or BuckMe safehouse terminals regularly.
