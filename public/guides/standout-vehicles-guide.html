GTA 6 Standout Vehicles Guide (280 Unique Variants & 25 Categories)

Standout Vehicles are rare, pre-customized static vehicles marked with a yellow icon and parked across Leonida—featuring unique paint jobs, special liveries, and boosted performance over base traffic models. The in-game Progress Database (Map > Progress) tracks 280 Standout Vehicles across 25 sub-categories.

How to Find, Scan & Keep Standout Vehicles

  1. Always Scan With WAiNK First: Whenever you spot a custom-painted parked ride with unique trim or a yellow icon, scan it with the WAiNK app immediately so it registers in your Progress Database toward the 200 Vehicle Models 100% requirement.
  2. Check Gang Hideouts & Side Strands: Several of the best Standout Vehicles are parked behind Illegal Operations—for example, the LCC Monarch Avarus sits untracked in the wooden shed behind The Thirsty Wolf.
  3. Register Untracked Standouts Immediately: Many Standout Variants have better acceleration and top speed than standard traffic cars. Drive untracked finds straight to Moses (Ch. 2) or Pay ’n’ Spray (Ch. 3+) to lock them into your Scooter Bros fleet.
  4. Track Progress by Sub-Category: Open Map > Progress to see which of the 25 sub-categories (Ultra-Luxe, Lowriders, Street Racer, Leonida Man, etc.) you have already discovered.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Standout Vehicles are in GTA 6?

There are 280 Standout Vehicles split across 25 sub-categories in the Map > Progress database.

Do you need all 280 Standout Vehicles for 100% completion?

No. 100% completion requires scanning 200 out of 557 total vehicle models, not all 280 Standout Variants.

Are Standout Vehicles faster than regular cars?

Many Standout Variants—like the LCC Monarch Avarus—come with pre-installed upgrades, higher top speed, and faster acceleration than base models.
