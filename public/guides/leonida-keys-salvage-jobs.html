Leonida Keys Diving & Salvage Side Jobs (Kenton Rees & 33 Discoveries)

Beneath the turquoise reefs and shipwrecks of the Leonida Keys lies one of GTA 6’s richest exploration activities: Kenton Rees’ diving and salvage jobs. Unlocked via Island Dreams, salvage runs equip you with scuba gear, a charter boat, and a Metal Detector to hunt Gold Doubloons, 7 Kenton shipwreck sets, and all 33 Underwater Discoveries.

Salvage Activity Quick Facts

Character / ContactKenton Rees (first met near Brian’s pool at the end of Hey Arnold!, then at the yellow pier shack in northeast Key Lento)
Unlock MissionIsland Dreams (Chapter 1 — unlocks Kenton’s charter boat, scuba gear, and Metal Detector)
Primary LocationsLeonida Keys coral reefs, coastal wrecks, and deeper offshore channels
100% Completion Targets33 of 33 Underwater Discoveries AND 5 of 7 Kenton Shipwreck Sets (tracked under Progress > Collections > Kenton’s Treasures)
Essential GearScuba tanks/fins (equipped at the rear of a dive boat), Metal Detector, and Harpoon Gun for shark defense
Key HazardsO2 oxygen meter management (top-left HUD) + marine predators (sharks, lionfish, jellyfish)

Step-by-Step Salvage & Shipwreck Hunting Loop

  1. Unlock Scuba Gear via Island Dreams: After meeting Kenton Rees around Hey Arnold!, visit his yellow shack on the northeast Key Lento pier to complete Island Dreams.
  2. Equip Scuba Gear at the Rear of a Dive Boat: Walk to the stern of Kenton’s charter boat (or any equipped dive boat) where tanks and fins are stacked to swap into full scuba gear.
  3. Sweep Wrecks with the Metal Detector: Dive down to shipwreck ribs and sandy seabed troughs. Equip and aim the Metal Detector until you lock onto an audio/visual ping, swim directly over the source, and press the Search prompt (Triangle / Y) to dig up Gold Doubloons and Kenton treasures.
  4. Watch Your O2 Bar & Carry a Harpoon Gun: Monitor the oxygen gauge in the top-left corner and keep your harpoon gun ready when diving deeper reefs where Great White and Hammerhead sharks patrol.
  5. Track Sets Under Kenton’s Treasures: Open Progress > Collections > Kenton’s Treasures to verify your progress toward 5 of 7 Kenton Sets and 33 of 33 Underwater Discoveries.

Choices, Missables & Verification Notes

100% Completion Requirement Breakdown: Unlike Kenton Sets (which allow 5 of 7 for 100%), Underwater Discoveries require all 33 of 33. Pair every Keys dive with our Stamina, Swimming & Scuba Diving Mechanics Guide and log your checks in the 100% Completion Tracker.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you unlock Kenton Rees’ salvage jobs in GTA 6?

Meet Kenton at the end of Hey Arnold!, then visit his yellow shack on the northeast Key Lento pier to complete Island Dreams, unlocking scuba gear and the Metal Detector.

How many Kenton shipwreck sets are needed for 100% completion?

You need 5 of 7 Kenton’s Treasures sets, plus all 33 of 33 Underwater Discoveries.

How do you defend against sharks while salvaging?

Keep a harpoon gun equipped when diving deeper offshore reefs to deter aggressive sharks.
