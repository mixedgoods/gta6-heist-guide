Ambrosia Banks & Vault Targets in GTA 6: Verified Routes & Access

Located in central Leonida around the industrial refinery and freight yards, Ambrosia hosts regional small-bank targets, nearby commercial shops, and high-security depository routes that tie directly into Chapter 3–4 robbery progression. This guide separates verified Story Mode bank mechanics from unverified pre-launch payout claims.

Access Requirements & Story Unlock Timing

Regional Small Banks (Chapter 3+)

Free-roam small bank robberies unlock after completing Raul Bautista's Credit Check mission in Chapter 3, which awards the Dye Pack Jammer. Regional branches pay roughly $25,000–$45,000 cleanly when jammed.

Large Bank Vaults (End of Chapter 4)

The 8 repeatable Large Bank Vaults unlock at the end of Chapter 4 after That's Where The Money Is..., paying up to ~$205,000 per clean run when paired with a CCTV wipe and silent thermal drilling.

Verification Disclosure: Ignore pre-launch rumors claiming a "$6.5M–$9.8M Ambrosia Federal Bullion Vault" free-roam payout. In verified Story Mode, repeatable free-roam Small Banks pay $25K–$45K and endgame Large Bank Vaults cap near ~$205K per run.

Recommended Ambrosia Staging, Garages & Map Pins

  1. Stage at Ambrosia Freight Yard Garage: Purchase the Ambrosia Freight Yard Garage (unlocked via the real-estate app after Daily Driver) so you have a clean trunk-equipped getaway vehicle minutes from Ambrosia's commercial strip.
  2. Equip the Dye Pack Jammer before entry: Never bag teller or rear-cage cash stacks in regional banks without activating your Dye Pack Jammer; ruined bills slash your take.
  3. Break pursuit via freight yards & industrial backroads: Avoid prolonged straight-line highway chases where VCPD / county air support holds overhead sight. Duck under industrial loading bays near the Ambrosia Freight Yard to break line of sight.
  4. Deposit immediately into BuckMe: Once clear of heat, transfer cash above the $20,000 pocket threshold into BuckMe Family Savings to eliminate Wasted/Busted loss risk.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there fixed keypad safe codes in Ambrosia banks?

No verified fixed 4-digit keypad codes are documented for Ambrosia bank vaults. All 7 verified fixed keypad codes (0420, 7700, 0300, 6823, 6969, 6769, 2727) belong to Leonida Keys, Port Gellhorn, Grassrivers, and Moses locations.

Is there a stolen-goods fence located inside Ambrosia?

No. Verified Story Mode has 7 fence contacts total (5 stolen-goods fences and 2 vehicle buyers) located in Leonida Keys, Port Gellhorn, and Vice City. Ambrosia is not classified as a fence hub.

When should I rob Ambrosia regional banks?

Wait until Chapter 3 after completing Credit Check so you have the Dye Pack Jammer, and purchase the Ambrosia Freight Yard Garage for reliable getaway staging.
