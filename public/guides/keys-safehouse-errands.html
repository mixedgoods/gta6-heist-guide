CHAPTER 1 · MISSABLE SIDE ERRANDS · KEY LENTO

Jason’s Key Lento Safehouse Repairs & Pre-Coming Home Errands (Missable)

Before driving north to Hamlet Prison to pick up Lucia in Coming Home, Chapter 1 gives Jason a quiet window of optional Key Lento safehouse errands. Completing these repairs and gifts—including the $475 necklace—triggers unique dialogue and early relationship boosts that are permanently missed once you start Coming Home.

Complete Pre-Coming Home Errands Walkthrough

  1. Make Save Slot #02 First: Before tackling any final Chapter 1 prep, create a manual backup in Save Slot #02 so you never accidentally trigger Coming Home early.
  2. Complete Jason’s Safehouse Repairs (Roof & Steps): Inspect the interactive maintenance prompts around Jason’s Key Lento house—fixing the weathered roof and porch steps cleans up the home before Lucia arrives.
  3. Stock Up on Groceries: Stop by the local Keys market to grab household provisions so the kitchen is stocked when Lucia walks in.
  4. Clean Up Jason’s Look & Ride: Visit a barber for a fresh haircut (see Character Customization Guide) and finish Just a Scratch at Coastal Auto Care so Jason’s Vapid ’70 Ganado is repaired and resprayed.
  5. Buy the $475 Necklace Gift: Save at least $475 from early Keys pickups or Donnie’s jobs to purchase the welcome-home necklace before driving to Hamlet Prison for Coming Home.

Choices, Missables & Verification Notes

Hard Missable Warning: Once you trigger Coming Home at Hamlet Prison, these pre-release errands cannot be retroactively completed—and because GTA 6 has no mission replay menu, you would need to reload an earlier save to see Lucia’s reactions. Cross-check all chapter cutoffs in Missable Missions & Cutoffs.
Relationship & Story Impact: Every pre-Coming Home errand feeds directly into Jason and Lucia’s trust dynamic. Read our spoiler-shielded GTA 6 Relationship Guide to see how relationship choices carry into later chapters.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the necklace before Coming Home cost?

The welcome-home necklace costs $475 in Chapter 1.

Can you do Jason’s Key Lento house repairs after Coming Home?

No. These optional pre-release errands are missable once you trigger Coming Home to pick up Lucia from Hamlet Prison.

Do the Key Lento errands count toward 100% completion?

They are not listed among the 35 mandatory 100% completion criteria, but they provide missable relationship boosts and unique story dialogue.
