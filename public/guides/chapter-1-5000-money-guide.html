Step-by-Step Chapter 1 Cash & Budget Plan

  1. Complete Buck Me and Lab Fees first so your Family Savings account and early Keys lockups (0420 / 7700) are active.
  2. Set aside at least $475+ before starting Coming Home so Jason can buy the necklace at The Siren's Chest, groceries, flowers, and a haircut for early relationship boosts.
  3. Run clean convenience store stickups and collect marked valuables across the Leonida Keys, fencing everything at Donnie's Trailer before triggering Swamped.
  4. Avoid blowing cash on unessential cosmetics or heavy Ammu-Nation rifles in Chapter 1—free world pickups like the Baseball Bat and mission weapons carry you cleanly into Chapter 2.
  5. Save manually in Slot #03 (Save Planner) before Tourist Trap, then use our Chapter 2 $15,000 Money Guide once you arrive in Port Gellhorn.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fastest legitimate way to make $5,000 in GTA 6 Chapter 1?

Combine early story payouts (Buck Me, Lab Fees), clean convenience store stickups, and fencing marked Keys valuables at Donnie's Trailer before completing Swamped.

How much cash should I keep before Coming Home?

Keep at least $475 for the necklace at The Siren's Chest plus pocket cash for groceries, flowers, and a haircut so you don't miss Jason's early relationship boosts.

Does the Apollo Aggregates Red Barn loop work in Chapter 1?

Players report it is reachable early in South Vice-Dale County (~$55,000 per 4-bag run), but because reload-based loops may be patched, always isolate testing on Save Slot #15.
