Fast Cash Safe Code & Robbery Guide (Little Cuba, Vice City)

Direct Answer: Fast Cash in Little Cuba, Vice City does NOT have a universal fixed safe code. Like all 15 Smash 'n' Grab pawn shops unlocked after Chapter 2's Coffee Break, its 4-digit back-room safe code randomizes every playthrough—and re-randomizes if you rob the shop again.

3 Verified Ways to Crack the Fast Cash Back-Room Safe

  1. Method 1 — Intimidate the Clerk (Fastest & Quietest): Aim your weapon at the Fast Cash employee behind the counter and press the threat prompt (Square on PS5 / X on Xbox) until the "Safe Code?" prompt appears. The clerk will hand over your active 4-digit combination.
  2. Method 2 — Scan the Back Office with Focus Mode (L3 + R3): Click both thumbsticks in the back office to highlight sticky notes, desk clipboards, papers, or monitors displaying your current randomized PIN.
  3. Method 3 — Lockpick or Sticky Bomb (Loud Fallback): Smaller shop safes can be lockpicked or breached with a sticky bomb (unlocked mid-game after Credit Check), though explosives trigger immediate alarms and police response.

Fixed Codes vs. Randomized Free-Roam Safes

When Codes Are Fixed

Only scripted story keypads (Lab Fees 7700 / 0420) and the 10 Gang-Operation safes (such as 0300, 6823, 6969, 6769) use universal fixed codes. See all 17 in our Safe Codes Database.

When Codes Always Randomize

Every free-roam pawn shop (including Fast Cash in Little Cuba), convenience store, and small bank/payday loan counter generates a fresh 4-digit code per run—so never trust clickbait sites claiming a "universal Fast Cash PIN."

Fast Cash Robbery & Escape Tips

Stage Your Getaway in Little Cuba: Park facing the nearest side alley or store a clean swap vehicle at Little Cuba Lockup Garage. Grab counter cash, intimidate the clerk for the safe code, empty the back room, and cut into neighborhood side streets before VCPD Cruisers box you in.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Fast Cash safe code in GTA 6?

There is no universal fixed code—Fast Cash is a free-roam Smash 'n' Grab pawn shop whose 4-digit safe combination randomizes every playthrough and every robbery.

Where is Fast Cash located in GTA 6?

Fast Cash is located in Little Cuba, Vice City (Vice-Dale County) and becomes robbable after Chapter 2's Coffee Break.

How do you find your Fast Cash safe code quickly?

Intimidate the clerk behind the counter (Square on PS5 / X on Xbox) until the 'Safe Code?' prompt appears, or scan the back office with Focus Mode (L3 + R3).
