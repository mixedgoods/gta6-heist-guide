Choosing the Right Watercraft by Region

Grassrivers Swamps: Swamp Airboat

Deep-hull speedboats beach immediately on marsh flats. Always grab a Grassrivers Swamp Airboat to skim over reeds and stay above Alligator ambush zones.

Vice Bay & Leonida Keys: Squalo & Seashark

Use the Shitzu Squalo for high-speed open-water escapes and the nimble Speedophile Seashark to weave under Keys causeways and piers.

Stealth & River Exploration: The Crest Kayak spawns along calm Mount Kalaga rivers and Keys coves—ideal for quiet wildlife spotting without engine noise.
