How to Spot Each Reference While Exploring

1. Brian's Marina Novel (Chapter 1+)

Inside Brian Heder's office at Brian's Marina in Key Lento, inspect the fictional Western novel The Cowboy's Prayer Is to Die Young. Its passage explicitly references Flaco Hernández's frozen body at Cairn Lake—directly echoing Arthur Morgan's RDR2 mission The Noblest of Men, and a Woman.

2. Frontier Mini Golf on Valentine Drive

Visit the Western-themed Frontier Mini Golf attraction located on Valentine Drive (nodding to RDR2's Chapter 2 town of Valentine). Watch the grounds for a wandering chihuahua wearing a tiny toy Arthur Morgan figure styled after his classic trailer outfit.

3. $35 Cattleman Hat in Kelly County

While exploring residential neighborhoods in Kelly County around Port Gellhorn, stop at the residential yard sale event to buy a $35 hat modeled closely after Arthur Morgan's iconic gambler/cattleman hat.

4. New Austin Plates & Frontier Lore

Check parked and cruising tourist vehicles across Vice City and the Leonida Keys—you can spot out-of-state plates labeled New Austin, linking the Red Dead frontier geography as a playful in-universe nod.

Editorial Standard: We only list RDR2 references reported by identifiable launch outlets (IGN, Vice, BroBible). Rumored RDR2 grave sites or uncorroborated social-media claims remain excluded until independently confirmed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you get Arthur Morgan's hat in GTA 6?

Vice reports that a residential yard-sale vendor in Kelly County sells a weathered cattleman hat heavily resembling Arthur Morgan's signature hat for $35.

Where is the Flaco Hernández book in GTA 6?

Inside Brian Heder's office at Brian's Marina in Key Lento. The readable Western novel The Cowboy's Prayer Is to Die Young references Flaco Hernández at Cairn Lake along with New Austin, Blackwater, and Armadillo.

Where is the tiny Arthur Morgan chihuahua Easter egg?

IGN reports finding a chihuahua with a miniature toy Arthur Morgan strapped to its back roaming the Western-themed Frontier Mini Golf course on Valentine Drive.
