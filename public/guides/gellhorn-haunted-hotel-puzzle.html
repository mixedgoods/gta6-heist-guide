Gellhorn Foggy Haunted Hotel & Dynamic Neon Billboard Puzzle Guide

On the outskirts of Port Gellhorn, an abandoned art-deco resort transforms between 00:00 and 05:00 in-game time into one of GTA 6's creepiest confirmed Easter eggs—complete with dense rolling fog, shifting mannequins, rooftop clues, and a warped neon horror billboard.

Verified Trigger Conditions

LocationAbandoned art-deco resort on the Port Gellhorn outskirts (Kelly County)
Required Time Window00:00 to 05:00 in-game time
Visual CuesSudden dense fog bank, shifting mannequin poses, warped neon billboard
Discovery StatusConfirmed in data/discoveries.json (disc-silent-hill-hotel)

How to Experience the Haunted Hotel Sequence

  1. Advance In-Game Time to Midnight: Sleep at a safehouse or wait in free roam until your phone clock passes 00:00 (before 05:00).
  2. Drive to the Port Gellhorn Outskirts Resort: Approach the abandoned art-deco hotel structure outside Port Gellhorn. Watch for a localized fog bank rolling in around the lot.
  3. Inspect the Neon Billboard & Courtyard: Look up at the dynamic neon billboard as its lettering distorts, then walk through the exterior corridors where mannequins subtly reposition when out of direct camera view.
  4. Check the Rooftop Clues: Climb to the upper walkway and roof access to inspect the environmental clues before dawn clears the fog at 05:00.

Confirmed vs. Unverified Theories

No Fabricated Secret Unlock: While the 00:00–05:00 fog trigger, shifting mannequins, rooftop clues, and dynamic neon billboard are confirmed, no permanent secret weapon or hidden interior vault has been verified as a reward for this puzzle yet.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time do you need to visit the Gellhorn Haunted Hotel?

Visit between 00:00 (midnight) and 05:00 in-game time to trigger the dense fog and warped neon billboard.

Where is the foggy haunted hotel located?

It is an abandoned art-deco resort situated on the outskirts of Port Gellhorn in Kelly County.

Does the Gellhorn Haunted Hotel unlock a secret weapon?

Not currently verified. The fog, shifting mannequins, rooftop clues, and neon billboard shift are confirmed, but no hidden weapon reward is verified.
