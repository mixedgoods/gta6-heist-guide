Optimal Duo Loadout & Loop Discipline

Entry & Crowd Control

Equip masks before stepping through the door. Use a Flashbang or suppressed Tactical 9mm SMG to neutralize armed boutique security without triggering prolonged street firefights.

Getaway Vehicle Choice

Stage a fast narrow escape car (Bravado Banshee, Coquette D10) or Sportbike facing the nearest alley or bridge exit. Always clear your wanted level completely before hitting the next boutique.

4-Step Vice City Jewelry Loop & Yasmani Fencing

  1. Start on the Coast: Hit Vangelico Vice Beach first while heat is zero, smash the highest-density glass cases, and cut west across the causeway.
  2. Break Sightlines Completely: Never chain two Vice City stores back-to-back with active stars—duck into parking garages or highway underpasses until the search radar clears.
  3. Swap or Cool Hot Plates: If VCPD scanned your vehicle plates during the first hit, swap cars at your Chapter 3 Safehouse Garage before hitting Downtown or Biscayne Harbor.
  4. Fence with Yasmani: Deliver stolen jewelry to Yasmani's Jewelry Fence (Chapter 3 specialist) rather than low-tier pawn buyers for better payout returns, then bank cash at an ATM.
