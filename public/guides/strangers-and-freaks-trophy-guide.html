GTA 6 Strangers & Freaks Complete Guide (Silver Trophy & Cooldowns)

GTA 6’s Strangers & Freaks side missions appear as purple map markers across Leonida, but not every stranger counts toward the same trophy. You need 8 qualifying stranger strands (including Kirk, Kyle, Yago, Pilot Pete, and other main arcs) for the Strangers & Freaks Silver trophy, while Tyrone and Andy grant separate standalone trophies and Chapter 1’s Shirley is missable but not required.

How to Track & Unlock the Strangers & Freaks Silver Trophy

  1. Prioritize Hourglass Icons Immediately: Most stranger strands remain open in post-story free roam, but any purple marker with an hourglass icon (like Yago’s timed race or Chapter 1’s Shirley) is time-sensitive.
  2. Check Your Console Achievement Tracker: Because GTA 6’s map mixes standalone-trophy strangers with 8-strand qualifying strangers, open your PlayStation/Xbox trophy tracker before and after finishing a chain’s final mission to confirm the +1/8 increment.
  3. Trigger Next Stages via Story & Sleep Cycles: Finished Part 1 of Kirk, Kyle, or Papa Audacity and see nothing on the map? Complete a main mission, run a quick Top Fun Drone Race, or sleep at a safehouse until the contact calls or texts.
  4. Don’t Skip Tyrone & Andy for Platinum: Even though Tyrone and Andy do not increment the 8-strand Silver trophy, both are mandatory for the Platinum because they award Spin On This! and Road to Rehabilitation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Strangers & Freaks strands are needed for the trophy?

You need to complete 8 qualifying Strangers & Freaks strands (such as Kirk, Kyle, Yago, Pilot Pete, and other main arcs) to unlock the Strangers & Freaks Silver trophy.

Why didn't Tyrone or Andy increase my Strangers & Freaks trophy count?

Tyrone ("Spin On This!") and Andy ("Road to Rehabilitation") each unlock their own separate trophies rather than counting toward the 8-strand cumulative trophy.

Can Strangers & Freaks be finished after beating the main story?

Yes, all trophy-required strands can be cleaned up in post-story free roam, though you should always complete any marker showing an hourglass icon promptly.
