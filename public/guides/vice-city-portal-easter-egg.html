GTA 6 Vice City Portal Easter Egg — All 12 LPL Substations, Reactor 2 and Tommy Vercetti's Shirt

How to trigger the hidden 2002 Vice City time-travel portal in Grand Theft Auto VI: starting at the Badger R&D Radio Tower in Key Lento, activating all 12 Leonida Power & Light (LPL) substations in exact order, entering Reactor 2 between 7:00 PM and 6:00 AM, and unlocking Tommy Vercetti's Hawaiian shirt and classic motorcycle.

Verification & Source Transparency: Last updated November 23, 2026. This guide synthesizes the November 23, 2026 community discovery originally solved by YouTuber Memeio and independently corroborated by IGN, Kotaku, Vice, Dexerto, Insider Gaming, Beebom, KeenGamer, and Sportskeeda. GTA6HeistGuide.com has not independently completed this puzzle in-game; every step, coordinate note, and reward reflects corroborated community reporting.

Step 1: Play as Jason & Start at the Badger R&D Radio Tower (Key Lento)

Required Player Character: Switch to Jason before starting. Community reports (Dot Esports, Beebom, Dexerto, Insider Gaming) confirm the Reactor 2 text message, portal sequence, and Tommy Vercetti shirt reward are tied to Jason — and switching characters mid-run resets the 12-substation chain.

Before visiting any substation, initialize the Easter egg sequence at the Badger R&D Radio Tower in Key Lento (Leonida Keys):

  1. Travel to the Badger R&D Radio Tower in Key Lento (view pin on Interactive Leonida Map →).
  2. Climb the ladders up into the elevated control office. Inside you will find a television, a Leonida Power & Light (LPL) poster, a retro JoySystem/PlayStation-style poster reading "Lose Track of Time", a power lever, and an activation button.
  3. Flip the power lever ON, then press the button directly beside it.
  4. A classic 2002 GTA: Vice City audio chime plays and the television flashes the Club Malibu logo, signaling that the 12-substation sequence is live.

Recommended preparation: make a manual backup save via our 15-Slot Save Planner and bring a fast helicopter so you can fly cleanly between Key Lento, Vice City, Hamlet, and Port Gellhorn.

The Original 2002 Vice City Cheat-Code Connection

The control-room clues connect the 12 LPL substations directly to the classic 2002 Grand Theft Auto: Vice City time-acceleration cheat code: LIFEISPASSINGMEBY.

  • Wall Markings: Each of the 12 LPL substations carries painted letters on its exterior wall that spell out L · iF · Ei · S · P · A · Ss · iN · G · Me · B · Y in order.
  • Controller-to-Switch Mapping: On PlayStation 2, the Speed Up Time cheat input was Circle, Circle, L1, Square, L1, Square, Square, Square, L1, Triangle, Circle, Triangle. At each GTA VI substation, the switches and buttons are labeled with directional letters and a dot that map 1-to-1 to that PS2 layout:

Complete all 12 substations in one continuous session (a helicopter makes the run much faster and avoids road traffic hazards).

Step 3: The Text Message, Reactor 2 Location & Time Window

Immediately after pulling Lever W at Substation #12 near Breakwater Quarry, your phone receives an anonymous text message reading:

U-235 REACTOR2 19-06

  • Location: Reactor 2 inside the LPL Turtle Head (T.H.) Power Station, located east of Hamlet in southern Vice-Dale County (view Reactor 2 on Interactive Leonida Map →).
  • In-Game Time Requirement: 7:00 PM to 6:00 AM (19:00–06:00). If you arrive during daytime, wait until 19:00 for the purple portal anomaly to materialize inside Reactor 2.
Spoiler Toggle: What Happens Inside the Purple Portal (2002 Malibu Club Cameo)

Stepping through the purple portal inside Reactor 2 transports Jason into a retro, low-poly 2002-era recreation of the original Malibu Club from Grand Theft Auto: Vice City, complete with a surprise appearance from classic character Kent Paul (voiced by Danny Dyer). Completing the brief throwback sequence and stepping back through the portal returns you to modern-day Leonida with your rewards unlocked.
