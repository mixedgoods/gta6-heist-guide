Thrillbilly Mud Club Guide: Frogswater Location, Rentals & Off-Road Events

Tucked into the mudflats of Frogswater outside Port Gellhorn, the Thrillbilly Mud Club is Kelly County’s rowdiest off-road playground. First mentioned by Wyman during Coffee Break, the venue combines dirt-bike and mud-truck racing, Roostere’s Rentals, exclusive club apparel, and Billy’s five-part side mission chain.

Unlock RequirementSpeak with Wyman in the Starlet Motel kitchen during Chapter 2’s Coffee Break
On-Site FacilitiesEntrance Merch Stall (Jason & Lucia Mud Club outfits) + Roostere’s Rentals (off-road vehicles)
Best Rental ChoiceSanchez dirt bike (higher rental fee, top acceleration for 1st-place finishes)
Reported Event PayoutsUp to $2,500 on the introductory Thrillbillies race (PowerPyx reported); opens Billy’s 5-quest chain

How to Unlock & Win at the Thrillbilly Mud Club

  1. Get the Lead from Wyman: In Chapter 2’s Coffee Break, talk to Wyman in the Starlet Motel kitchen (and grab the collectible card pinned to the fridge while you are there).
  2. Drive Out to Frogswater: Follow the icon northwest out of Port Gellhorn into Kelly County’s muddy backroads until you spot the Thrillbilly Mud Club banners and grandstands.
  3. Gear Up at the Merch Stall & Roostere’s Rentals: Pick up your Mud Club outfit at the entrance stall, then rent the Sanchez from Roostere’s Rentals if you do not already have a tuned off-roader.
  4. Master Mud Traction: Deep mud ruts sap top speed on heavy RWD muscle cars. Hug the drier outer berm on sweeping corners, tap the brake before muddy hairpins, and keep momentum steady through puddles.
  5. Chain Into Billy’s Full 5-Mission Line: Placing well unlocks Billy’s follow-up texts starting with Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab and continuing through Mall Rats, The Impaler, and Hometown Heroes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the Thrillbilly Mud Club on the GTA 6 map?

It is located in Frogswater in Kelly County, outside Port Gellhorn, and appears after speaking with Wyman during Coffee Break.

How much money can you win at the Thrillbilly Mud Club?

Corroborated guides report up to $2,500 for placing well in the opening Thrillbillies race, plus $2,000 in Billy’s follow-up job Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab.

Are there collectible outfits at the Thrillbilly Mud Club?

Yes—the entrance merch stall lets you purchase Mud Club outfits for Jason and Lucia during your visit.
