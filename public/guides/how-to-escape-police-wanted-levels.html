How to Escape Police & High Wanted Levels in GTA 6 (Routes, Sewers & Evasion)

To escape high wanted levels in GTA 6, immediately break visual line of sight (cut through Vice City alleys, parking garages, or underground storm drains), swap out of your spotted getaway car while unseen, and avoid open highway bridges where VCPD Air Support helicopters and roadblocks trap you. Always bank spare cash in BuckMe Family Savings so a Busted or Wasted run never wipes out your bankroll.

5 Proven Tactics to Drop Police Heat Fast

  1. Break Sightlines Before Accelerating Straight: Outrunning VCPD Pursuit Interceptors on open boulevards invites helicopters and spike/roadblock traps. Take sharp 90-degree turns into narrow Vice City alleys or multi-level parking structures first.
  2. Duck Into Underground Storm Drains & Overpasses: Once VCPD Air Support helicopters join the chase, overhead cover is mandatory. Use Vice City's storm-drain tunnels and highway underpasses mapped on our Escape Routes Guide.
  3. Swap Vehicles Out of Line of Sight: Changing cars while no cruiser or witness has eyes on you makes patrol units far less likely to re-acquire you during the cooldown search phase.
  4. Use Terrain Cops Cannot Traverse: In Grassrivers, jump into a Swamp Airboat; in Mount Kalaga, climb steep dirt fire roads in a 4x4 or Dinka Enduro; in the Leonida Keys, ditch the causeway for a speedboat.
  5. Protect Cash Before Every Robbery: Pocket cash caps at $20,000 and is vulnerable on Busted/Wasted—hit an ATM or BuckMe terminal before chaining robberies.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fastest way to lose a police helicopter in GTA 6?

Drive under solid overhead cover—such as Vice City's underground storm drains, highway overpasses, or multi-level parking garages—so VCPD Air Support loses visual lock.

Do masks and car swaps help evade police?

Yes—breaking visual line of sight and swapping out of a spotted vehicle reduces re-detection during the cooldown search phase.

How do I protect my cash from Busted or Wasted penalties?

Deposit spare cash at physical ATMs or transfer funds into BuckMe Family Savings (unlocked in Chapter 1's Buck Me mission).
