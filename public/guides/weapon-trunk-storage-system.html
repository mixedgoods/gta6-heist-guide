Weapon Trunk Storage & Carry Limit System Explained

Unlike GTA V's bottomless weapon wheel, Grand Theft Auto VI pairs realistic on-person long-gun limits with physical Personal Vehicle trunk storage. Mastering trunk staging before every heist or hunting trip keeps you from getting caught under-armed.

On-Person Carry vs. Vehicle Trunk Armory

On-Person Carry

Jason and Lucia carry concealable sidearms and a limited slung long-gun loadout on foot. Walking openly through Vice City with heavy rifles draws immediate attention from civilians and VCPD patrols.

Personal Vehicle Trunk

Your Personal Vehicle acts as a mobile armory (similar to a saddlebag in Red Dead Redemption 2). Open the trunk before entering a bank, motel raid, or hunting zone to swap rifles, shotguns, and tactical gear.

Slot Count Transparency: Exact per-vehicle numeric trunk slot counts are still marked Not yet confirmed in our vehicles.json telemetry ledger. We never invent fixed slot numbers where Rockstar has not published exact caps.

3 Rules for Smart Trunk Staging

  1. Register Your Primary Getaway Car: Use WAiNK + Moses registration so your armory vehicle persists as a true Personal Vehicle and never despawns with your stowed rifles.
  2. Swap Before Crossing the Threshold: Pull up one block away from a Fleeca branch or gang safe, open the trunk out of witness sightlines, and equip your assault rifle or Moreland 850 Shotgun.
  3. Use Trunk Space for Loot Loops: Physical trunks also power high-profit cargo runs like the Apollo Aggregates Red Barn 4-Bag Trunk Loop (~$55K/run).

Best Vehicle Classes for Trunk Utility

Full-size sedans, muscle coupes (Vapid Ganado), and SUVs/pickups (Albany Cavalcade XL, Vapid Caracara 4x4) offer the most practical heist staging profiles. Compare every confirmed model in our Best Getaway Vehicles & Trunk Storage Guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you store weapons in your car trunk in GTA 6?

Approach the rear trunk of your Personal Vehicle to stow extra long guns, rifles, and shotguns and swap them into your active on-person loadout.

Do you lose weapons if your Personal Vehicle is destroyed?

Keep your primary loadout registered to a true Personal Vehicle and stored in an unlocked garage; avoid stowing key rifles in unregistered stolen street cars.

Which vehicles are best for trunk storage?

4-door sedans, muscle cars, and full-size SUVs/pickups provide the most reliable trunk staging for heist gear and loot bags.
