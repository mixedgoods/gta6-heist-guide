GTA 6 Gangs & Criminal Factions of Leonida Guide

Every region of Leonida is carved up by distinct criminal syndicates that control local smuggling routes, drug labs, and the 39 Illegal Operations required for 100% completion and the “Pest Control” trophy. Knowing who runs each county helps you anticipate enemy loadouts, unique weapon drops, and safe locations.

How Faction Territories Affect Free-Roam Gameplay

  1. Faction Retaliation & Alertness: Hitting multiple hideouts belonging to the same syndicate back-to-back (such as Final Chapter MC sites in Ambrosia) can make nearby gang hangouts immediately hostile when you walk in.
  2. Unique Faction Weapon Drops: Several gangs guard signature yellow-text weapons—like the Kingfishers’ W-22 Small Game Rifle at Wetman Bay Fishing Shacks and Final Chapter’s Ride Free H&L .38 Revolver at The Thirsty Wolf.
  3. Regional Safe Codes Follow Lore Clues: Always scan gang flags, back-office walls, and computers inside faction fronts—Final Chapter’s 1982 founding year unlocks their VIP safe.
  4. Plan a Pest Control Sweep: Hit one Kingfishers site in Grassrivers, one Kolts site in Port Gellhorn, The Thirsty Wolf in Ambrosia, and one site each from the Vice City syndicates to pop Pest Control efficiently.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which gang controls Ambrosia in GTA 6?

Final Chapter MC, an outlaw motorcycle club operating out of Ambrosia County and The Thirsty Wolf bar on SR-27.

Who controls Port Gellhorn's illegal operations?

Kolt & Associates (Kolts) run 5 operations in Port Gellhorn / Kelly County, including Kingpin Bowling, Brooks Boatworks, and La Cascara Motel.

How many gangs do you need to raid for Pest Control?

You must complete at least one Illegal Operation from every criminal gang tracked under Progress > Gangs.
