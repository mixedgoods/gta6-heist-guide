GTA 6 SIDE ACTIVITIES Q&A · PARADISE TAXI · RYDEME APP

Are There Taxi, Rideshare or Delivery Side Jobs in GTA 6?

Short answer: No. Unlike older Grand Theft Auto entries, GTA 6 Story Mode does not feature playable radiant career side jobs where you drive a taxi, deliver packages, or run paramedic/vigilante shifts. Services like Paradise Taxi and the RydeMe app are strictly passenger fast-travel tools—while repeatable side income comes from narrative contact gigs.

Best Real Side-Hustle Alternatives in GTA 6 Story Mode

  1. Use Paradise Taxi & RydeMe for Fast Travel Only: Don't waste time trying to steal a cab to trigger fare prompts. Instead, call Paradise Taxi or open the RydeMe app on your phone whenever you want to skip a long cross-Leonida drive for an extra $100.
  2. Run Untracked Car Thefts for Moses: After Chapter 2's Moses mission, use the Slim Jim and WAiNK tracker scan to steal and flip clean vehicles—detailed in our Moses Vehicle Jobs Guide.
  3. Deliver High-End Wish-List Cars to Randy: In Chapter 3, unlock Randy's High-End Vehicle Wish-List in Balsero for larger luxury-car payouts.
  4. Fly Pilot Pete's Airdrop Deliveries (Ch. 4): If you specifically want delivery-style gameplay, complete Pilot Pete's 5 Sundown Airfield Airdrops—which also unlocks hangar safe code 3718.
  5. Dive for Captain Kenton or Hunt for Bret Vogel: For combat-free cash, run Kenton's Underwater Treasure ($200/item + set bonuses) or complete Bret Vogel's 15 Hunting Target Decks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you drive a taxi or work for RydeMe in GTA 6 Story Mode?

No. Both Paradise Taxi and the RydeMe app are passenger-only fast-travel services where you can pay an extra $100 to instantly skip to your waypoint.

What are the closest delivery-style side jobs in GTA 6?

Vehicle theft deliveries for Moses and Randy, plus Pilot Pete's 5 Sundown Airfield airdrop runs in Chapter 4, are the closest narrative delivery gigs in Story Mode.
