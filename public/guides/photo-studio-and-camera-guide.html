GTA 6 Photo Studio (Photo Mode) & Camera Guide

GTA 6’s built-in Photo Mode—called Photo Studio—can be launched almost anytime (including during cutscenes) via the Pause Menu or by pressing Square (PS5) / X (Xbox Series X|S). Unlike smartphone photos that stay in Jason and Lucia’s phone gallery, Photo Studio captures save directly to your console’s native Media Gallery.

How to Take Clean Shots in Photo Studio

  1. Trigger Photo Studio Mid-Action: Press Pause and select Photo Studio (or use the Square / X shortcut) to freeze Leonida mid-drift, mid-jump, or during story cutscenes.
  2. Switch to Free Drone Camera: Detach the camera to orbit Jason, Lucia, or your Standout Vehicle, then fine-tune focal length, tilt, and depth-of-field blur.
  3. Place Custom Lighting & Filters: Drop lighting presets to illuminate Vice City neon reflections at night, or experiment with stylized filters like Cartoon Universe, Found Footage, and Pixel Art.
  4. Hide UI & Capture to Console: Tap Triangle / Y to strip the interface, then snap the shot—find your full-resolution files inside your PS5 Media Gallery or Xbox Captures.

Photo Studio vs. In-Game Smartphone Camera

Mission Photo Objectives: When a story mission (like Dirty Work or Photo/Shoot) or a poster scan asks you to take a picture, always use Jason or Lucia’s smartphone camera—Photo Studio is for player screenshots and does not trigger mission photo checks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do GTA 6 Photo Studio pictures save?

They save directly to your PS5 Media Gallery or Xbox Captures folder rather than an in-game album.

Can you use Photo Mode during GTA 6 cutscenes?

Yes, Photo Studio works during cutscenes, though certain free-camera or lighting options may be restricted.

How do you hide the HUD in Photo Studio?

Press Triangle on PlayStation 5 or Y on Xbox Series X|S before capturing.
