How to Unlock Secret Radio Stations in GTA 6 (Nightclub Poster Camera Scans)

To unlock hidden radio frequencies in GTA 6, walk inside underground Vice City nightclubs such as The Vault in Downtown Vice City, open your smartphone camera (D-Pad Up → Camera), and aim directly at the illuminated DJ booth posters. Once scanned, the new station permanently joins your vehicle radio wheel.

Step-by-Step: How to Scan Nightclub Posters

  1. Enter an Underground Vice City Club: Head to Downtown Vice City venues such as The Vault (available once you explore Vice City freely in Chapter 3+).
  2. Locate the Illuminated DJ Booth Poster: Walk near the DJ booth or hallway promo walls and look for glowing flyer posters featuring underground station branding.
  3. Open Your Smartphone Camera: Press D-Pad Up on PS5 or Xbox Series X|S, select the Camera app, and frame the illuminated poster cleanly.
  4. Confirm Frequency Unlock: When the scan registers, the hidden radio station is added to your in-car radio dial across all vehicles in Story Mode.
