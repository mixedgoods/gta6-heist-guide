Super Mario Bros. Easter Egg Location: Unlock Jason's 'J' & Lucia's 'L' Hats

One of GTA 6's most playful hidden discoveries is a Super Mario Bros. Easter egg tucked inside the ruined Lawton Co. Paper Mill in southern Moreau, Lummox County. Triggering the hallucinatory floating block above the brick ruins permanently unlocks two wardrobe caps: a Mario-inspired "J" cap for Jason and a Luigi-inspired "L" cap for Lucia.

Where to Find the Mario & Luigi Easter Egg

LandmarkLawton Co. Paper Mill (abandoned brick ruins with graffiti-covered walls)
County & AreaSouthern Moreau, Lummox County — just north of Mount Kalaga National Park
Nearby Visual CueSmall riverbank brick ruins near a Water Wheel meditation spot
RewardsTwo collectible baseball caps: Jason's "J" Cap (red/pink with white diamond J badge) & Lucia's "L" Cap (green with white diamond L badge)
Verification BasisIndependently corroborated across Rock Paper Shotgun, Sportskeeda, Mobalytics, and Games.gg launch guides.

Step-by-Step: How to Reveal & Hit the Floating Block

  1. Travel to the Lawton Co. Paper Mill Ruins: Head north of Mount Kalaga National Park into southern Moreau (Lummox County) and locate the brick riverfront ruins beside the Water Wheel meditation spot.
  2. Find the Bong on the Tarp-Covered Crate: Walk into the center of the graffiti-covered brick ruins and look for a crate/brick stack draped with a purple/blue tarp holding a bong.
  3. Interact With the Bong to Trigger the Hallucination: Use the bong so your protagonist begins tripping. While under the influence, a translucent floating Question-Mark style block materializes in the air above the ruins.
  4. Climb & Hit the Block From Underneath (or Shoot It): Parkour up the broken brick walls beneath the floating block and jump straight up into it from below—or shoot it—to trigger a classic retro jingle and drop the two hats.
  5. Collect Both Wardrobe Caps: Pick up both dropped caps so Jason's "J" cap and Lucia's "L" cap are permanently stored in your respective wardrobes.
Character Note: Rock Paper Shotgun notes that switching between Jason and Lucia ensures both hats register cleanly in each protagonist's wardrobe.

Mario & Luigi Wardrobe Rewards Explained

Jason's "J" Cap (Mario Homage)

A Mario-inspired cap (reported as red/pink across lighting conditions) stamped with a white diamond badge carrying a bold "J" for Jason Duval.

Lucia's "L" Cap (Luigi Homage)

A green Luigi-inspired cap stamped with a matching white diamond badge carrying an "L" for Lucia Caminos.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the Mario & Luigi Easter egg in GTA 6?

It is located at the ruined Lawton Co. Paper Mill in southern Moreau, Lummox County—just north of Mount Kalaga National Park near the river's Water Wheel meditation spot.

Why can't I see the floating block at the Lawton Co. Paper Mill?

The block only appears while your character is tripping—interact with the bong sitting on the tarp-covered crate inside the brick ruins first.

Do the Mario & Luigi hats stay in your wardrobe permanently?

Yes—once you hit or shoot the floating block and collect the dropped caps, Jason's 'J' cap and Lucia's 'L' cap are added to your wardrobe.
