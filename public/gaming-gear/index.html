Section 1 · Grip & Comfort

Best Value Controller Accessories

Optional Upgrades

Standard PS5 and Xbox controllers already play GTA VI great out of the box. If your thumbs slip during long highway chases or you prefer slightly taller analog sticks for smoother camera control, slip-on thumbstick grips are a much cheaper experiment than buying a $200 pro controller. Always double-check controller fit: PlayStation 5/PS4 grips do not fit Xbox Series X|S sticks.

Section 3 · Budget Wired Audio

Affordable Gaming Headsets

3.5mm Wired

You do not need a $180 wireless headset to hear directional audio cues, dialogue, and Leonida radio stations clearly. A lightweight 3.5mm wired headset plugs straight into your DualSense or Xbox controller, never needs charging, and avoids wireless dongle compatibility headaches. Note that console-specific features like PS5 Tempest 3D Audio or Xbox Windows Sonic are handled by the console itself, not built into a 3.5mm headset.

Section 4 · Verified Heatsink Drives

PS5 Internal M.2 Storage Upgrades

PS5 Only · Optional

An internal SSD upgrade is completely optional if you only keep a few games installed at a time. If you want to keep GTA VI installed alongside several other large titles on PS5, Sony requires an M.2 2280 PCIe Gen4 x4 NVMe SSD with sequential read speeds of at least 5,500 MB/s and an integrated cooling heatsink. We never recommend bare drives without a heatsink for PS5 installs.

1TB vs. 2TB Practical Comparison: Choose a 1TB heatsink SSD (like the Lexar NM790 1TB) if you want the most budget-friendly way to double your usable PS5 space. Choose a 2TB officially licensed heatsink SSD (like the WD_BLACK SN850P 2TB) only if you maintain a large library of 100GB+ games and never want to uninstall them. Neither drive increases GTA VI frame rate — they expand console storage capacity.
