Koss Porta Pro Classic On-Ear Wired Headphones

PS5 Xbox PC

Designed in 1984 and virtually unchanged since, the Koss Porta Pro looks like a cassette-era prop — yet audio reviewers still recommend it as one of the most enjoyable budget wired headphones you can plug straight into a DualSense or Xbox controller.

Why We Selected It
An authentic 1984 industrial design still in continuous production four decades later — lightweight, collapsible, and zero batteries to charge.

Open-back featherweight folding steel headband with ComfortZone pressure switches that ease ear fatigue during multi-hour story sessions.

Solo story-mode players, retro aesthetic fans, and anyone tired of heavy clamping wireless headsets.

Section 3 · Wired Console Audio, Open-Back Hi-Fi & Clean Comms

Headsets, Studio Headphones & Microphones

3.5mm & USB Audio

Section 4 · NVMe M.2 Expansion (PS5 & PC)

Storage Upgrades (Heatsink Rules Explained)

PS5 & PC Only

Internal storage expansion is optional if you only keep a few games installed. For PlayStation 5 internal expansion, Sony specifies an M.2 2280 PCIe Gen4 x4 NVMe SSD rated at 5,500 MB/s+ with a cooling heatsink. Choose a pre-heatsinked drive like the WD_BLACK SN850P or Lexar NM790 w/ Heatsink for drop-in PS5 installs; buy the bare Samsung 990 PRO 4TB only for PC/laptop builds or if you plan to fit a separate PS5-sized heatsink yourself. Xbox Series X|S notice: standard M.2 NVMe drives do not fit Xbox Series X|S consoles, which require proprietary Storage Expansion Cards.
