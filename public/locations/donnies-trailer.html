Donnie's Trailer Fence Location Guide (Leonida Keys)

Donnie's Trailer is your earliest stolen-goods fence in the Leonida Keys (mapped as fence-donnie). It is also one of GTA 6's most important permanent missable cutoffs: once you trigger and complete Swamped in Chapter 1, Donnie's Trailer fence closes permanently.

Permanent Cutoff Warning: Do not start Swamped while holding unfenced Chapter 1 loot or before locking a manual backup in Slot #02/#03 of the 15-Slot Save Planner. After Swamped, you must wait until Port Gellhorn's Dunk's Trailer unlocks in Chapter 2 for your next dedicated stolen-goods fence.

How to Maximize Donnie's Trailer Before Swamped

  1. Liquidate early Keys loot: Fence all Chapter 1 stolen goods at Donnie's Trailer to accelerate your $5,000 BuckMe Family Savings goal.
  2. Stash cash in BuckMe immediately: After Donnie pays out, deposit your cash into BuckMe Family Savings so Wasted/Busted penalties can't drain it.
  3. Finish Brian's Lockup (7700) & Cal's texts: Use Brian's Lockup (7700) for Lab Fees stashing and check Cal Hampton's texts at The Rusty Anchor before triggering Swamped.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does Donnie's Trailer fence close permanently?

Donnie's Trailer fence closes permanently once you complete the Chapter 1 mission Swamped.

Can you sell stolen cars at Donnie's Trailer?

No. Donnie is a stolen-goods fence, not a vehicle buyer. Stolen vehicles are sold to Moses (Port Gellhorn) and Randy (Balsero, Vice City).

What fence replaces Donnie's Trailer after Chapter 1?

In Chapter 2 (Port Gellhorn), completing Slam Dunk unlocks Dunk's Trailer on Tanglewood Lane as your next stolen-goods fence.
