Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Apollo Aggregates Red Barn on the GTA 6 map?

It is located in South Vice-Dale County on the southern industrial edge of Vice City, plotted at map pin glitch-apollo-red-barn near LPL Turtle Head Power Station.

What vehicle should I drive to Apollo Aggregates Red Barn?

Bring a pickup truck or full-size SUV with 4+ trunk/bed stowage slots so you can load all 4 contraband duffel bags in a single trip.

Is the Apollo Aggregates Red Barn money loop guaranteed to stay unpatched?

No—while the landmark and duffel bags are documented in launch discoveries, reload-restock behavior may be adjusted in future patches, so always isolate tests on Save Slot #15.
