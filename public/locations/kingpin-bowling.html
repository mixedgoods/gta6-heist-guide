How to Clear Kingpin Bowling & Open Code 0300

  1. Approach Southern Port Gellhorn in Chapter 2: Park a getaway car facing the main exit road before entering the bowling alley perimeter.
  2. Neutralize Kolt & Associates Guards: Clear the interior and rear service area around the lanes and pinsetter machinery.
  3. Punch In Code 0300: Enter 0300 on the safe to claim your one-time gang-operation cash and contraband stash, then deposit at a nearby Port Gellhorn ATM.
