Brooks Boatworks Guide: Safe Code 6823 & Upstairs Wall Map

Brooks Boatworks is a fortified Kolt & Associates marine yard near Port Gellhorn (Draper Island). Clearing the compound lets you crack its upstairs safe using verified code and inspect the gang's upstairs wall map revealing the remaining Port Gellhorn safes.

Step-by-Step Compound Raid

  1. Scout the Dock Perimeter: Approach from the landward road or quietly by boat; thin out outer lookouts with a suppressed pistol or carbine.
  2. Push to the Upstairs Office: Clear the boat-bay floor and climb the interior stairs to the office overlooking the slips.
  3. Enter Code 6823 & Check the Wall Map: Punch 6823 into the office safe, collect the cash, and check the wall map for leads on Kingpin Bowling and La Cascara Motel.
  4. Exfiltrate by Water or Highway: Either peel out toward the Port Gellhorn highway or launch a boat off the slip to dodge Kelly County patrol cars.

What is the safe code for Brooks Boatworks in GTA 6?

The verified safe code for Brooks Boatworks is 6823.

The safe is inside the upstairs office alongside the Kolt & Associates wall map.
