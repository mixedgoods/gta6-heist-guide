Hamlet Prison Location Guide (Lucia Caminos Release & Chapter 1)

Hamlet Prison (Leonida Department of Corrections) is the correctional facility where Lucia Caminos begins her GTA 6 story arc and where Jason Duval picks her up during Chapter 1's Coming Home mission. Before driving to the prison gates, make sure you finish every missable Key Lento preparation errand.

Frequently Asked Questions

What mission takes place at Hamlet Prison in GTA 6?

Hamlet Prison is the focal point of Chapter 1's Coming Home mission, where Jason picks up Lucia upon her parole release.

What should I do before going to Hamlet Prison in Coming Home?

Complete Jason's optional Key Lento preparation errands—fixing the safehouse roof, steps, and bedroom wall, buying groceries and flowers, getting a haircut, and buying the $475 necklace at The Siren's Chest.

Can you break back into Hamlet Prison in free roam?

Full free-roam interior access inside Hamlet Prison after Coming Home is not confirmed; avoid provoking heavy perimeter security unless prepared for high wanted stars.
