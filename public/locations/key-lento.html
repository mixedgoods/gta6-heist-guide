Key Lento Island Town Guide: Shops, Safe Codes & Safehouse

Key Lento is the heart of Chapter 1 in Mariana County (Leonida Keys). It packs Jason's House, the Key Lento Fleeca branch, Moonstone Boutique (0420), Brian's Lockup (7700), The Rusty Anchor bar, and The Siren's Chest jewelry shop into one compact coastal town.

What safe codes are in Key Lento?

Moonstone Boutique uses code 0420 and Brian's Lockup uses code 7700 during Chapter 1's Lab Fees.

Where do you buy Lucia's $475 necklace in Key Lento?
