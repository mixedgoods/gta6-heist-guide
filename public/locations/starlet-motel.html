Starlet Motel Guide: Safehouse, Fridge Card & Checking Out Raid

The Starlet Motel in Port Gellhorn serves as Jason and Lucia's primary Chapter 2 story safehouse. It holds an easily missed collectible card inside the motel refrigerator and hosts the explosive Checking Out raid where you grab the Moreland 850 Shotgun.

Starlet Motel Quick Facts

RegionPort Gellhorn, Kelly County
Story RoleChapter 2 Story Safehouse (#2 of 4) + Motel Parking Lot Garage
Key MissionsCoffee Break (Wyman intro) & Checking Out (Ch. 2 finale)
Collectible InsideCollectible card located on/inside the motel room refrigerator
Weapon PickupMoreland 850 Shotgun spawns on the bed during Checking Out

Do Not Leave Without These 2 Items

1. Starlet Motel Fridge Card

Inspect the motel room fridge right after Coffee Break to claim the collectible card toward your 15-of-18 Card Sets 100% requirement.

2. Moreland 850 Shotgun

When the Checking Out raid erupts, immediately grab the Moreland 850 Shotgun off the bed to shred breaching enemies in the tight hallway.

Permanent Safehouse Closure

Chapter 2 Cutoff: Once you survive Checking Out ($15,000 prerequisite) and relocate to Vice City for Chapter 3, the Starlet Motel closes as an active story safehouse. Keep a manual backup on Save Slot #06 before launching the raid.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the collectible card in the Starlet Motel?

Check the motel room refrigerator in Chapter 2 before triggering Checking Out.

How do you get the Moreland 850 Shotgun at Starlet Motel?

It appears on the bed inside the motel room during the Chapter 2 finale mission Checking Out.

Can you still sleep at Starlet Motel in Chapter 3?

No. The Starlet Motel closes as a story safehouse after the Checking Out raid forces Jason and Lucia to Vice City.
