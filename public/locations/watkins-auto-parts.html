Watkins Auto Parts Location Guide (Port Gellhorn — Moses's Garage)

Watkins Auto Parts is the Port Gellhorn automotive yard and workshop run by Moses (marked by the MW icon on the in-game map). Visiting this Kelly County landmark in Chapter 2 unlocks GTA 6's untracked vehicle fencing loop, the WAiNK scanner, the Slim Jim, and the Scooter Bros recall app.

Arrival Rules & Free-Roam Utility

  1. First Visit (Mission Trigger): Drive up as Lucia in a modest, everyday street car—avoid rolling up in an exotic supercar or grabbing a car parked right outside the gate.
  2. Back-Lot Tutorial: Follow Moses into the back yard to scan test cars with WAiNK (R1/RB) and pick up the Slim Jim for quiet vehicle entry.
  3. Ongoing Drop-Off Hub: After completing Moses, return here whenever you steal a WAiNK-verified untracked everyday car around Port Gellhorn or Kelly County highways.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Watkins Auto Parts in GTA 6?

Watkins Auto Parts is in Port Gellhorn (Kelly County), marked by the MW icon in-game and mapped at pin fence-moses on our Interactive Leonida Map.

Why won't the Moses mission start at Watkins Auto Parts?

Arrive as Lucia in a modest, everyday street vehicle rather than a supercar or a car taken directly from outside the lot.

Can you sell stolen jewelry at Watkins Auto Parts?

No—Watkins Auto Parts is a stolen-vehicle drop-off for untracked everyday cars only. Sell stolen goods at Dunk's Trailer in Port Gellhorn instead.
