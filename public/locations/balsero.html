Balsero District Guide (Vice City)

Balsero is a Vice City district reported as the Chapter 3 drop-off zone for Randy—GTA 6 Story Mode's high-end wish-list vehicle buyer. Unlike Moses in Port Gellhorn (who buys everyday untracked vehicles), Randy targets curated luxury and exotic requests in Vice City.

Randy vs. Moses: Know Before You Steal

Do not drive random everyday sedans to Randy expecting standard buyouts, and do not bring stolen jewelry to Balsero. Use Moses (Port Gellhorn) for untracked street cars scanned via the WAiNK app, and use Randy in Balsero for high-end wish-list deliveries.

Coordinate Verification Note

Earlier pre-launch notes placed Randy in Port Gellhorn; corroborated post-launch reporting places Randy's Chapter 3 wish-list garage in Balsero, Vice City. Our legacy map pin remains labeled pending final in-game GPS confirmation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Randy's vehicle buyer located in GTA 6?

Post-launch reporting places Randy's Chapter 3 high-end wish-list vehicle buyer in Balsero, Vice City.

What does Randy buy in GTA 6?

Randy buys high-end wish-list vehicles—he is one of the 2 vehicle buyers (alongside Moses), not a stolen-goods pawn fence.

What is the difference between Moses and Randy?

Moses (Watkins Auto Parts, Port Gellhorn) buys everyday untracked cars and registers personal vehicles; Randy (Balsero, Vice City) handles high-end wish-list cars in Chapter 3+.
