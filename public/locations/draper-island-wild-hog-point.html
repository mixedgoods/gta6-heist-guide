Draper Island & Wild Hog Point Sub-Region Guide (Port Gellhorn)

Draper Island and Wild Hog Point form the northern waterfront and airstrip sub-region of Port Gellhorn in Kelly County. Together they house two of Chapter 2's most valuable landmarks: Brooks Boatworks (Kolt & Associates safe code 6823 and upstairs wall map) and Wild Hog Airfield (the climax zone of Slam Dunk).

Two Must-Visit Northern Port Gellhorn Stops

Draper Island: Brooks Boatworks (6823)

Cross onto Draper Island to infiltrate Brooks Boatworks. Clear the Kolt & Associates guards, inspect the upstairs wall map that links their Port Gellhorn network, and open the safe with 6823.

Wild Hog Point: Airfield & Slam Dunk

During Slam Dunk ($1,700 BuckMe payout), Jason and Lucia track Dr. Bill past Kolts Docks to Wild Hog Point / Wild Hog Airfield, unlocking Dunk's Trailer stolen-goods fence on Tanglewood Lane.

Efficient Chapter 2 Northern Port Gellhorn Circuit

  1. Start with Slam Dunk: Complete Slam Dunk at Wild Hog Point first so Dunk's Trailer fence is open for business.
  2. Hit Brooks Boatworks (6823): Sweep Draper Island for the Kolt & Associates safe cash and loot.
  3. Check Shipyard SCOOP Containers: Swing down to the Port Gellhorn Shipyard before fencing stolen valuables at Dunk's Trailer and banking cash into BuckMe.
