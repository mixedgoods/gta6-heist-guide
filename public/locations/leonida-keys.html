Geographic Location & How to Reach Leonida Keys

Geography & Districts

Stretching southwest off Leonida's southern tip in Mariana County, the Leonida Keys connect Key Lento, outer cays, boatyards, coral reefs, and narrow over-water causeway bridges. Waterways surround every island, making speedboats, jet skis, and kayaks essential for bypassing bridge police checkpoints.

How to Reach It

You begin Story Mode in the Leonida Keys during Chapter 1. Later in the campaign, drive south from Grassrivers or Vice City along the main island causeway highway, or approach by boat or seaplane from the southern coast.

Nearby Missions, Activities & Highlights

Story Missions & Heists

Major Chapter 1 Keys missions include Buck Me (Key Lento Fleeca & BuckMe app), Lab Fees (codes 0420 & 7700), Coming Home (safehouse errands & Hamlet Prison), Swamped (Donnie's fence cutoff), Day Release (The Rusty Anchor), and Tourist Trap (Chapter 1 point of no return).

Free-Roam Activities

Building your $5,000 BuckMe Family Savings goal, diving for underwater discoveries and Kenton shipwreck sets, shopping at The Siren's Chest ($475 necklace), visiting The Rusty Anchor bar, and spotting marine species (Manatees, Sea Turtles, Dolphins, Hammerheads, Great White Sharks).

What are the verified keypad codes in the Leonida Keys?

Lab Fees uses two verified codes in the Leonida Keys: 0420 for the Moonstone Boutique back-room lab in Key Lento, and 7700 for Brian's Lockup further south.

Which fence closes permanently in the Leonida Keys?

Donnie's Trailer fence closes permanently once you complete the Chapter 1 mission Swamped. Sell any marked loot beforehand.
