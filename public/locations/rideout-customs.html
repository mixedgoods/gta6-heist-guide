Frequently Asked Questions

What is Rideout Customs in GTA 6?

Rideout Customs is a named vehicle customization shop reported in GTA 6 guides for paint, rims, spoilers, and performance upgrades, similar to Los Santos Customs in GTA V.

Are Rideout Customs upgrade prices confirmed?

No. Exact upgrade part lists, prices, and numeric performance deltas are not independently confirmed here, so none are fabricated.

Should I customize a stolen car before registering it?

No. Always use the WAiNK app and Watkins Auto Parts (unlocked in Moses) to register an untracked car as a Personal Vehicle before spending money on upgrades.
