Grassrivers Region Guide: Wetlands, Safe Code 6769, Airboats & Wildlife

Grassrivers is Leonida's sprawling subtropical wetland between the Leonida Keys, Port Gellhorn, and Vice City. Our database tracks 20 verified map pins—including 3 gun stores, the Kingfishers gang safe at Sunrise RV Park (code in Pelican Valley near Gullet Marsh and Cottonmouth Lake), swamp airboat spawns, and high-danger wildlife led by American Alligators and Florida Panthers.

Geographic Location & How to Reach Grassrivers

Geography & Districts

Occupying southern-central Leonida (including Mariana County's Pelican Valley, Gullet Marsh, and Cottonmouth Lake), Grassrivers is a network of shallow sawgrass marshes, raised dirt levees, wooden docks, and winding airboat canals. Standard low-clearance sports cars bog down off-pavement—use lifted 4x4 pickups or the Grassrivers Swamp Airboat.

How to Reach It

Enter Grassrivers heading north from the Leonida Keys causeway, southeast from Port Gellhorn, or west from Vice City's outer suburbs. Airboats and lifted trucks let you cut straight across flooded marsh flats where patrol cruisers cannot follow.

Nearby Missions, Activities & Highlights

Story Missions & Heists

Chapter 1's Swamped takes place on the wetlands/Grassrivers border (triggering Donnie's permanent fence cutoff). Grassrivers is also a prime free-roam corridor for Kingfishers gang safe runs and hunting.

Free-Roam Activities

Cracking the Sunrise RV Park dock building safe (), skimming waterways in the Grassrivers Swamp Airboat, hunting Alligators, Herons, Pelicans, and Panthers using Focus Mode (L3 + R3), and shaking wanted levels across shallow canals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is safe code 6769 used in Grassrivers?

Safe code 6769 opens the Kingfishers gang safe inside the Sunrise RV Park dock building in Pelican Valley (near Gullet Marsh and Cottonmouth Lake)—it is in Grassrivers/Mariana County, not Port Gellhorn.

Is there a confirmed stolen-goods fence in Grassrivers?

No. Our source register explicitly rejected earlier pre-launch claims of a Grassrivers fence; the Grassrivers Wetlands Dock is classified strictly as an Unverified Regional Lead.

What is the best vehicle for exploring Grassrivers?

Use the Grassrivers Swamp Airboat for water channels or a lifted 4x4 (such as the Vapid Riata or Canis Lifted 4x4 Pickup) for muddy levees.
