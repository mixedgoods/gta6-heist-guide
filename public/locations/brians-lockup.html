Brian's Lockup (Keypad Code 7700 Fence Location)

Brian's Lockup is a Chapter 1 storage lockup and stolen-goods fence in Key Lento (Leonida Keys) accessed with verified fixed keypad code . Introduced during Lab Fees, it lets Jason and Lucia stash and fence contraband—including acid, jewelry, and marked bills.

How to Use Brian's Lockup (Code 7700)

  1. Complete Moonstone Boutique First (0420): During Chapter 1's Lab Fees, enter code 0420 at Moonstone Boutique to recover the acid lab stash.
  2. Head to Brian's Lockup in Key Lento: Follow the marker to Brian Heder's storage unit in the Leonida Keys.
  3. Enter Fixed Code 7700: Punch 7700 into the door keypad to unlock the storage unit, stash mission goods, and establish Brian as your early-game fence contact.

Important Clarification: No Free Cash or Weapon Spawns

Verified Fact: Code 7700 opens Brian's Lockup strictly for stashing and fencing stolen goods (including acid in Lab Fees, marked bills, and jewelry). Unlike Gang-Operation safes, it does not spawn free cash piles or hidden weapons. Also note that Brian's Lockup remains available after Chapter 1, whereas Donnie's Trailer Fence closes permanently after Swamped.
