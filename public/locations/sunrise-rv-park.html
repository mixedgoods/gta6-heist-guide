How to Find & Open the Sunrise RV Park Dock Building Safe

  1. Navigate to the Waterfront Dock Building: Inside Sunrise RV Park, head past the parked trailers toward the waterside dock building where the Kingfishers stage contraband.
  2. Clear Guard Resistance: Neutralize Kingfishers guards around the dock shed before interacting with the keypad.

Code Distinction & Wetlands Tips

6769 (Sunrise RV Park) vs. 6969 (La Cascara Motel)

Double-check the second digit: 6769 is the Kingfishers dock building safe at Sunrise RV Park; 6969 belongs to Kolt & Associates at La Cascara Motel in western Port Gellhorn.
