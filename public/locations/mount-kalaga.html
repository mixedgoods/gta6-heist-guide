Geographic Location & How to Reach Mount Kalaga National Park

Geography & Districts

Rising north of Lake Leonida and Ambrosia in Lummox County, Mount Kalaga features steep pine ridges, dirt fire roads, river canyons, trailhead outposts, and dense forest canopies that break police helicopter sightlines during high-heat escapes.

How to Reach It

Drive north along the interior highways past Ambrosia and Lake Leonida, or fly in via Weazel News Maverick / Dodo Seaplane. Bring a high-clearance off-road vehicle (Vapid Riata, Caracara 4x4, or Dinka Enduro dirt bike) for steep switchbacks.

Nearby Missions, Activities & Highlights

Story Missions & Heists

After finishing Coffee Break in Chapter 2, switch to Jason Duval and visit Killtelligence Provisions in Mount Kalaga to launch Veteran Hunted—opening all 15 Bret Vogel Hunting Target Decks required for 100% completion.

Free-Roam Activities

Completing Bret Vogel's 15 Hunting Target Decks with Good/Pristine carcasses (Focus Mode L3 + R3), tracking Legendary Hunter targets, unlocking the Killtelligence SM-69 Hunting Rifle, purchasing the Mount Kalaga Trailhead Garage, and off-road dirt biking.
