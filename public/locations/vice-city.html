Geographic Location & How to Reach Vice City

Geography & Districts

Located along Leonida's southeastern Atlantic coastline, Vice City spans Vice Beach, Ocean Beach, Washington Beach, Downtown Vice City, Little Cuba, Watson Bay, La Perle, Balsero, and Vice City International Airport (VCIA). Major bridges connect the barrier-island beach strips to the mainland financial core and western highways leading toward Ambrosia and Port Gellhorn.

How to Reach It

You first visit Vice City's offshore waters and nightlife briefly during Chapter 1's Day Release, then relocate permanently after surviving Chapter 2's Checking Out finale in Port Gellhorn. In free roam, take the eastern coastal highway north from the Leonida Keys or cross east from Port Gellhorn/Ambrosia.

Nearby Missions, Activities & Highlights

Story Missions & Heists

Key Vice City story beats include Day Release (superyacht & Nine1Nine), Welcome to Vice City (meeting Dre'Quan at The Jack of Hearts), Credit Check (3 Fleeca branches + Dye Pack Jammer), The Cartel Heist ($120,000 at Sinfrontera National Bank), and VCIA Takedown ($250,000 airport finale).

Free-Roam Activities

High-value robbery loops (jewelry stores, large vaults, Fleeca branches), nightclub poster camera scans for secret radio stations, the Vice City Portal Easter egg, underground storm-drain police escapes, and Randy's high-end stolen vehicle wish-list deliveries in Balsero.
