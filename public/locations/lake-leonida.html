Lake Leonida is the massive inland freshwater basin in central Leonida bordering Ambrosia and the foothills of Mount Kalaga. Our interactive map tracks 7 verified pins around the lake—including 2 gun stores, shoreline points of interest, boat/seaplane staging spots, and inland aquatic wildlife.

Geographic Location & How to Reach Lake Leonida

Geography & Districts

Located in central Leonida just west of Ambrosia and south of Mount Kalaga National Park, Lake Leonida's ring road connects rural docks, bait shops, and lakeside campgrounds while offering unobstructed water takeoff lanes for the Mammoth Dodo Seaplane.

How to Reach It

Drive northwest from Ambrosia, northeast from Port Gellhorn, or descend south from Mount Kalaga. Because the lake sits at the geographic center of the mainland, staging a Dodo Seaplane or speedboat here gives you rapid access to every northern and central region.

Nearby Missions, Activities & Highlights

Story Missions & Heists

Lake Leonida acts primarily as a high-visibility central navigation hub and water escape corridor between Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, and Mount Kalaga hunting zones.

Free-Roam Activities

Resupplying at the 2 lakeside gun stores, launching seaplanes and watercraft across open freshwater, tracking inland waterfowl and aquatic species for the 100% Animals Compendium, and cutting across the lake to evade highway roadblocks.

Where is Lake Leonida on the GTA 6 map?
