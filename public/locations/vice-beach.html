Vice Beach & Ocean Beach District Guide: Banks, Garages & Secrets

Vice Beach and Ocean Beach form Vice City's iconic neon-lit Atlantic barrier strip. Packed with high-value jewelry stores, beachfront Fleeca branches, the purchasable Vice Beach Strip Garage, and retro arcade cabinets, it is the premier Chapter 3–5 playground.

Key Vice Beach & Ocean Beach Locations

Vice Beach Strip Garage

One of GTA 6's 8 purchasable garages (unlockable via the real-estate app after Chapter 2). Perfect for staging fast Sports cars like the Grotti Cheetah Classic right off the strip.

Banks & Luxury Jewelers

Home to the Ocean Beach Fleeca hit during Credit Check, Vangelico Vice Beach jewelry displays, and the Vice Beach Commercial vault.

Arcades & Easter Eggs

Check the Vice Beach Arcade for playable retro mini-games, plus Ocean Beach callbacks like the Tommy Vercetti Easter egg.

Escape Routes

Bridge chokepoints get roadblocked fast at 3+ stars—use beachfront alleys, storm-drain channels, or personal watercraft along the coast to shake VCPD.

High-Value Robbery Loop

Chain Ocean Beach payday/Fleeca targets with your Dye Pack Jammer (unlocked in Credit Check), then drop luxury jewelry off with Yasmani's Jewelry Fence before cooling down heat inside the Vice Beach Strip Garage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you buy a garage in Vice Beach?

Yes. Vice Beach Strip Garage is one of the 8 purchasable garages in GTA 6 Story Mode.

Which story missions take place in Vice Beach / Ocean Beach?

Chapter 1's Day Release visits Vice City's beachfront nightlife, and Chapter 3's Credit Check hits Fleeca branches in Washington Beach and Ocean Beach.

What is the best escape route off Vice Beach?

Avoid straight bridge dashes into roadblocks—duck into coastal alleys, storm drains, or launch a boat/Seashark off the shoreline.
