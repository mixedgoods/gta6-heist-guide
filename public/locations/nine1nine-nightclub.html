Nine1Nine Nightclub Location Guide (Vice City)

Nine1Nine is an upscale Vice City nightclub featured during the ashore nightlife sequence of Chapter 1's Day Release, when Jason and Lucia follow Mattia Tauro from his offshore superyacht into Vice City's club scene alongside Huxley Motel.

What Not to Miss Before Reaching Nine1Nine

Day Release Missables Check: Nine1Nine appears at the end of Day Release, after the superyacht sequence. Before leaving Mattia's yacht, be sure you talked to the deck guests near the railing for the free unique sunglasses and chose the tandem jet ski ride with Jason and Lucia for the permanent Relationship heart boost.

Early Preview of Vice City

Because Chapter 1 is centered on the Leonida Keys, Nine1Nine and Huxley Motel give players one of their first up-close glimpses of Vice City's neon nightlife before the duo formally relocates there in Chapter 3.

Club Poster Camera Scans

When you return to Vice City clubs in free roam (including underground venues like The Vault in Downtown Vice City), open your smartphone camera around illuminated DJ booths to unlock hidden radio stations.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do you visit Nine1Nine Nightclub in GTA 6?

You visit Nine1Nine near the end of Chapter 1's Day Release mission after following Mattia Tauro ashore from his Vice City superyacht.

Does Nine1Nine unlock a secret radio station?

Secret radio stations are unlocked by scanning illuminated DJ booth posters inside underground Vice City clubs (such as The Vault in Downtown Vice City) with your smartphone camera.

Can you buy Nine1Nine Nightclub in Story Mode?

No. Nightclubs cannot be purchased as passive-income businesses in GTA 6 Story Mode.
