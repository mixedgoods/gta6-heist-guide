Papa Audacity Side Missions Walkthrough (AudaCity Stunts)

Papa Audacity is Vice City’s chaotic crypto-shilling stuntman whose 5-mission Strangers & Freaks chain begins in Chapter 3 at Ocean Beach. Either Jason or Lucia can play the strand, which awards an AudaCity-branded Sandking truck along the way and culminates in Catching a Whale in Leonard County.

Papa Audacity Strand — All 5 Missions in Order

1. The Unknown StuntmanOcean Beach (southern park) — Clear the stunt path, repair the damaged gas canister for pyrotechnics, and witness the jump.
2. Trial by TireLa Perle parking garage — Stunt-driving trial inside Vice City’s multi-story garage.
3. Quit Your Job, Sell Your HousePeacock Bay — Crypto promo escalation; grants access to the AudaCity-branded Sandking truck.
4. AudaCity StuntsOcean Beach / Vice City — Drive the AudaCity vehicle off the stunt ramp, land cleanly in-bounds, and debrief with the engineer.
5. Catching a WhaleLeonard County / Waning Sands — Final chapter of Papa Audacity’s crypto stunt arc.
Verification BasisIndependently corroborated across PowerPyx, LeonidaHub, KeenGamer, and Sportskeeda walkthroughs.

Step-by-Step Guide to Papa Audacity’s 5 Missions

  1. Start at Southern Ocean Beach Park (The Unknown Stuntman): Available starting in Chapter 3 as either Jason or Lucia. Approach Papa Audacity near the park, clear obstacles from his landing lane, fix the damaged gas canister so the flame rigs fire, and watch the launch.
  2. Complete Trial by Tire (La Perle): Wait for the follow-up phone prompt, then head to the La Perle parking garage to run his tire-and-ramp stunt sequence.
  3. Finish Quit Your Job, Sell Your House (Peacock Bay): Meet Papa Audacity in Peacock Bay for his next hype stunt, which introduces the AudaCity-branded Sandking truck.
  4. Nail the Ramp in AudaCity Stunts (Ocean Beach): Drive the branded stunt vehicle at full approach speed off the designated ramp. Keep the chassis level in midair so you land upright inside the target zone, then talk to the crew engineer to finish.
  5. Wrap Up Catching a Whale (Waning Sands / Leonard County): Travel north to Leonard County / Waning Sands when the final purple icon appears to close out Papa Audacity’s entire strand.

Stunt Landing Tip

Clean Landings: In AudaCity Stunts, hitting the ramp slightly off-center can clip a guardrail and fail the jump. Line up straight two blocks back, hold steady throttle, and tap mid-air pitch control only if the nose dips. Pair this with our 130 Stunt Jumps Guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many missions are in Papa Audacity's questline?

There are 5 missions: The Unknown Stuntman, Trial by Tire, Quit Your Job Sell Your House, AudaCity Stunts, and Catching a Whale.

Can both Jason and Lucia do Papa Audacity's missions?

Yes, corroborated PowerPyx walkthroughs confirm either Jason or Lucia can start and play Papa Audacity's missions.

What reward do you get from Papa Audacity?

While AudaCity Stunts itself has no direct cash payout, the chain grants the AudaCity-branded Sandking truck earlier in the strand and advances your Strangers & Freaks completion.
