South Leonida Real Estate Mission Walkthrough (Ch. 3): Rescuing Mia from M7

South Leonida Real Estate continues Lucia's family storyline immediately after Mami Knows Best. When Lucia visits her mother Mia Caminos's office, she discovers Mia has been targeted by the rival M7 gang—launching a tense chase into a trap.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Check Mia's Shopping Center Office: Drive to Mia's real estate office after her call. You will find the office empty with only a note left on the door.
  2. Investigate the Cul-de-Sac House: Head to the marked house on the cul-de-sac. As you search the property, you hear Mia yelling outside just in time to see M7 gang members shove her into a getaway vehicle.
  3. Chase the M7 Vehicle: Sprint back to your car and pursue the M7 crew through Vice City streets. Stay close without crashing into cross-traffic.
  4. Approach the Graffiti-Tagged M7 Building: Track the kidnappers to their marked building covered in graffiti. Approaching the entry doors triggers a scripted cutscene where Lucia is ambushed and locked in a cage alongside Mia.

Don't Panic If You Lose Visual on the M7 Car

Built-In Fail-Safe Call: Corroborated IGN walkthroughs note that if traffic separates you from the M7 getaway vehicle during the pursuit, you do not automatically fail—Mia calls Lucia with an approximate mid-town location so you can still locate the graffiti hideout.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if you lose the M7 car in South Leonida Real Estate?

You will not fail the mission; Mia calls Lucia with a rough mid-town location so you can continue to the graffiti-tagged M7 building.

What mission unlocks South Leonida Real Estate?

Completing Mami Knows Best in Peacock Bay unlocks South Leonida Real Estate in Chapter 3.
