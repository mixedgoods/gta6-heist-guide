Thrillbillies & Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab Optional Mission Guide (Ch. 2)

Thrillbillies and its follow-up Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab kick off Billy’s 5-mission optional side arc in Chapter 2: Paradise Lost. Introduced via Wyman during Coffee Break, these high-energy Port Gellhorn jobs blend dirt-bike mud racing with an armored security van ambush.

Complete Walkthrough (Parts 1 & 2)

  1. Unlock via Coffee Break: Talk to Wyman in the Starlet Motel communal kitchen during Coffee Break to put the Thrillbilly Mud Club on your radar.
  2. Part 1 — Thrillbillies (Check Out the Mud Club): Travel to the Thrillbilly Mud Club in Frogswater. Visit the entrance merch stall to pick up a Mud Club outfit for Jason and Lucia, then head to Roostere’s Rentals and rent the Sanchez dirt bike.
  3. Win the Mud Race: Race Billy’s crew through the slick off-road circuit. Feather the throttle through deep mud troughs rather than pinning it full open—taking 1st place pays up to $2,500 and unlocks Billy’s text chain.
  4. Part 2 — Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab: Wait for Billy’s follow-up text, meet him at the Port Gellhorn marker, and interact with the bench to start the heist. Ride the provided dirt bike off-road with Billy’s crew to track down an armored security van.
  5. Breach the Van & Riverbed Escape: Tail the van until it crashes or is forced to stop, plant the provided explosive charge on the rear doors, detonate it, grab the cash bag, and follow Billy down the riverbed dirt-bike escape route to shake the cops for $2,000.

Choices, Missables & Verification Notes

Full 5-Quest Billy Chain & 100% Tracking: Completing both Thrillbillies and Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab opens the remaining three Billy quests tracked in our 100% Completion Tracker: Mall Rats, The Impaler, and Hometown Heroes. Finish these early in Chapter 2 to stack cash toward your $15,000 Checking Out goal.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you start Thrillbillies in GTA 6?

Complete Coffee Break in Chapter 2 after speaking with Wyman in the Starlet Motel kitchen, then drive to the Thrillbilly Mud Club in Frogswater.

Which vehicle should you rent at Roostere’s Rentals?

Rent the Sanchez dirt bike—even though it costs more upfront, its agility and acceleration make winning 1st place (up to $2,500) much easier.

How do you trigger Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab?

Finish the initial Thrillbillies Mud Club mission and wait for a text message from Billy, then meet him at the bench marker in Port Gellhorn.
