Raul Setup 1: Cell Tower Recon (Hidden Charges) Walkthrough

Hidden Charges (Cell Tower Recon) is one of the 5 mandatory Chapter 3 setup missions given by Raul Bautista before launching The Cartel Heist. Rigging the surrounding cell towers creates the crucial ~11-minute police comms blackout window during the Sinfrontera National Bank vault run.

Step-by-Step Setup Execution

  1. Review Your Save & Gear: Lock a manual save in your 15-Slot Save Planner before starting the Cartel Heist setup chain and pack a suppressed sidearm in case you need quiet perimeter clearance.
  2. Scout & Reach the Surrounding Cell Towers: Follow Raul Bautista's coordinates to the designated cell towers covering Sinfrontera National Bank's local dispatch corridor.
  3. Plant the Remote Charges: Place the remote explosives cleanly on each marked relay so they remain armed and hidden until finale kickoff.
  4. Exfiltrate Without Drawing Sustained Heat: Clear the tower perimeter without triggering a prolonged pursuit so the charges stay undetected ahead of Mission #71.

How This Setup Impacts the Finale

Why the ~11-Minute Timer Matters

Triggering the rigged cell towers is Step 1 of The Cartel Heist. Knocking out local comms delays VCPD perimeter coordination, giving Jason and Lucia time to disarm lobby guards cleanly and grab the high-value big stacks of bills first.

Pairing with Other Raul Setups

Combine this blackout window with Security Expert (alarm bypass timings) and Safe Word (vault override credentials) to keep your run eligible for the Professional rating and reported up to $150,000 max payout.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Cartel Heist setup rigs the cell towers?

Hidden Charges is the mandatory Raul Bautista setup where you plant remote explosives on the surrounding cell towers.

How long does the cell tower blackout last in The Cartel Heist?

Blowing the rigged cell towers at the start of The Cartel Heist gives you an approximately 11-minute blackout window before VCPD dispatch coordinates a full perimeter.

Is Hidden Charges mandatory to start The Cartel Heist?

Yes—all 5 Raul Bautista setups (Security Expert, Heist Planning, Ride or Die, Safe Word, and Hidden Charges) must be completed before Mission #71 launches.
