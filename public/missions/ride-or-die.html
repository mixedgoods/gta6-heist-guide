Ride or Die Mission Walkthrough (Ch. 3): Stealing DWNPLY's Car for Dre'Quan

Ride or Die (also listed as Meet Dre'Quan) unlocks in Chapter 3 after Credit Check. Helping music promoter Dre'Quan settle a score with rival artist DWNPLY, Jason infiltrates the Eris Flagship store to boost DWNPLY's prized ride while dodging personal bodyguards and the Cash City gang.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Meet Dre'Quan: Link up with Dre'Quan after finishing Credit Check to get the payback plan targeting rival star DWNPLY.
  2. Approach the Eris Flagship Store: Travel to the Eris Flagship retail store in Vice City where DWNPLY's car is parked under security watch.
  3. Bypass Bodyguards & Boost the Car: Slip past or neutralize DWNPLY's personal bodyguards guarding the perimeter and take control of the target vehicle.
  4. Outrun the Cash City Gang: Once the alarm goes up, Cash City gang members give chase through Vice City traffic. Use alleys and highway ramps to break line-of-sight and deliver the car back to Dre'Quan.

Escaping Cash City Pursuers

Avoid Narrow Dead-End Streets: Cash City shooters box you in fast on tight Vice City side streets. Stick to multi-lane avenues or expressway interchanges where you can out-accelerate their sedans cleanly.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does Ride or Die unlock in GTA 6?

Ride or Die unlocks in Chapter 3 after you complete Raul Bautista's Credit Check mission.

Where is DWNPLY's car located in Ride or Die?

DWNPLY's car is parked at the Eris Flagship store in Vice City, guarded by personal bodyguards and backed up by the Cash City gang.
