Power Play Mission Walkthrough (Ch. 4): Help Kordell's Crew ($40,000)

Power Play (objective: Help Kordell's Crew) is a major Chapter 4 turning point paying $40,000. While Lucia assists Kordell Grant's crew on an armored car robbery, Jason sneaks across the rooftop of Kordell's Salton base to uncover their airport vault plans—leading Raul to plan a double-cross.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Check In at Kordell's Salton Base: Arrive at Kordell's headquarters in Salton and speak to the front receptionist as Lucia.
  2. Roof Infiltration as Jason: Switch to Jason outside, climb the ladder on the right side of the building to reach the roof, sneak inside unnoticed, and photograph/locate Kordell's airport vault heist plans.
  3. Set Up the Roadblock: Back with Kordell's crew, place the road cones as directed to funnel the armored target into the ambush zone.
  4. Cover the Gang During the Heist: Follow Kordell, pick up a heavy weapon, and lay down covering fire as the crew cracks the target.
  5. Steal a Getaway Car & Lose the Cops: Command a fast getaway vehicle once sirens close in, break line-of-sight through downtown alleys, and bank your $40,000 cut.

Right-Side Roof Ladder Shortcut

Don't Waste Time at the Front Doors as Jason: Head directly to the right exterior wall of Kordell's Salton base to spot the roof-access ladder immediately. Completing Power Play opens both Delivery Method and Team Building.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much money do you earn from Power Play in GTA 6?

Completing Power Play for Kordell's crew pays $40,000.

How does Jason get inside Kordell's Salton base?

Use the exterior ladder on the right side of the Salton building to climb onto the roof and slip inside to locate the airport heist plans.
