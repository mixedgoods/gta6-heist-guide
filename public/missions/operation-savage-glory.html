Operation Savage Glory Walkthrough (Ch. 5): Bridge Explosives & Coast Guard Raid

Operation Savage Glory is Chapter 5's blockbuster multi-stage set-piece for Vinny Capsik. Combining bridge demolitions, unlimited-ammo helicopter sniping, Rocco's container breach, an underwater tunnel infiltration, and aerial winch extraction, it pays $50,000, returns your gear, grants Pegassi Residences penthouse access, and unlocks the Snow Storm trophy/achievement.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough (All 6 Phases)

  1. Rig the Bridge Piers (Lucia): Meet Vinny upstairs, take the boat down to the bridge pier as Lucia, climb onto the concrete supports, and slide the explosive charge along the wall until the LED turns solid green before planting.
  2. Clear the Cargo Ship Deck (Jason — Heli Sniper): Switch to Jason aboard the support chopper with an unlimited-ammo sniper rifle. Track enemy weapon flashlights across the dark cargo deck, pick off every guard, and land the helicopter.
  3. Container Breach with Rocco: Follow Rocco across the ship to the marked cargo container, plant and detonate the breach charges, and hold for pickup.
  4. Bridge Detonation & Dive: Board the chopper, detonate the bridge explosives from the air, activate the terminal controls to raise the bridge span, and dive into the water below.
  5. Underwater Tunnel & Coast Guard Offices: Plant charges on the tactical boat, swim through the underwater tunnel entrance, breach the National Coast Guard Offices, and open the shutter doors for your team.
  6. Winch Extraction & RPG Defense: Fly back in the helicopter, prioritize hostile RPG operators threatening Kevin, attach the cargo winch, and defend the extraction zone until you return victorious to the penthouse.

Combat & Demolitions Tips

Watch the Green LED & RPGs: On the bridge pier, slide the charge slowly until the LED locks solid green—do not spam placement. Later, during the aerial winch extraction, immediately focus fire on RPG launchers first so Kevin's position stays intact.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the rewards for Operation Savage Glory in GTA 6?

You earn $50,000, full Pegassi Residences penthouse access, your confiscated weapons and gear back, and the Snow Storm trophy/achievement.

How do you plant the bridge explosives in Operation Savage Glory?

Climb onto the bridge pier as Lucia and slide the explosive gadget across the wall until its LED turns solid green, then lock it in place.
