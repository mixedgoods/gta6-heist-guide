Clean Up After Donnie Mission Walkthrough (Chapter 1 — Donnie Fence Unlock)

Clean Up After Donnie is a key Chapter 1 mission in Just Like Paradise where Jason tracks down a stolen contraband drop for Donnie near Hamlet. Completing it is reported to unlock Donnie as an active stolen-goods fence—making it one of the most important early money milestones before the Chapter 1 cutoff.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Answer Donnie’s Call & Travel to Hamlet: After completing Coming Home, answer Donnie’s call and set a waypoint to the small wooden fishing deck in southwest Hamlet (Vice Dale County).
  2. Inspect the Drop Under the Roof Structure: Walk onto the pier past the local anglers and check beneath the small shelter where Donnie stashed the package. You will discover the contents have already been lifted.
  3. Launch a Boat & Track the Thieves: Your radar updates with a search zone roughly 0.14 miles across the water. Board a nearby boat and cruise down the waterway—use Focus Mode (L3 + R3) or listen for activity near the shoreline to pinpoint the hideout.
  4. Clear the Kingfishers Shack: Approach the waterborne shack where three Kingfishers members are holding the stash. They turn hostile on sight, so take hard cover around the dock pilings, draw your sidearm (L1 / LB), and eliminate all three targets.
  5. Recover the Stash from the Blue Cooler: Step inside the shack, interact with the blue cooler to secure Donnie’s missing contraband, and finish the retrieval to open Donnie’s Chapter 1 fence access.

Choices, Missables & Verification Notes

Missable Cutoff & Optional Objectives Warning: While no collectibles inside Clean Up After Donnie itself are confirmed missable, Donnie’s Trailer Fence closes permanently after Swamped. Use Donnie’s newly unlocked fence to offload early Chapter 1 stolen goods and fund your $5,000 BuckMe Family Savings target before starting Swamped. No alternate story branching choice is confirmed inside this mission.
Evidence Note & Map Reference: Mission title and Chapter 1 placement are tracked in existing site data; the Hamlet fishing dock, 0.14-mile boat search, three Kingfishers gunmen, blue cooler, and Donnie fence unlock are corroborated across third-party launch walkthroughs (GamesRadar, PowerPyx, PC Gamer). Frequently Asked Questions

How do you trigger Clean Up After Donnie in GTA 6?

Corroborated launch guides report that Donnie calls Jason the morning after Coming Home in Chapter 1, marking a fishing dock in southwest Hamlet.

What does Clean Up After Donnie unlock?

Completing the retrieval unlocks Donnie as a stolen-goods fence in Chapter 1, giving you a better liquidation option until his fence closes permanently after Swamped.

Are there any choices or missables in Clean Up After Donnie?

No branching dialogue choices or missable collectibles are reported inside the mission itself, but you should use Donnie’s fence before triggering Swamped.
