Amateur Hour Mission Walkthrough (Ch. 4): Cisco's Collection Job

Amateur Hour is a solo-Lucia Chapter 4 mission for Francisco (Cisco). Looking to build trust inside Kordell Grant's crew, Lucia helps Cisco track down a local adult-film director in Washington Beach who owes him money, earning $1,000 and unlocking Cash Injections.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Accept Cisco's Debt Job: Speak with Francisco (Cisco) as Lucia and agree to help him collect an overdue debt from a local director to earn trust with the crew.
  2. Travel to Washington Beach: Head to the designated production venue in Washington Beach.
  3. Change Clothes at the Locker: Follow the objective marker to the locker area and swap into the required outfit to blend into the location.
  4. Track Down & Confront the Director: Navigate through the venue, locate the director, and enforce Cisco's collection to wrap up the job and bank $1,000.

Kordell Crew Infiltration Arc

Step 2 of Lucia's Gym Cover: Amateur Hour bridges Making Gainz and Cash Injections. Finishing it keeps Lucia's undercover standing intact ahead of Power Play and Team Building.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the reward for Amateur Hour in GTA 6?

Completing Amateur Hour for Cisco pays $1,000 and advances Lucia's standing in Kordell's crew, unlocking Cash Injections.

Where does Amateur Hour take place?

The mission takes place in Washington Beach, where Lucia changes clothes at a locker before confronting the director.
