Frequently Asked Questions

Are any 100% completion collectibles permanently missable?

Most 100% collectibles remain reachable in free roam, but chapter-specific safehouse items (like the Starlet Motel fridge card), Donnie's fence, and early relationship boosts have strict story cutoffs.

When should I make manual saves in GTA 6?

Make dedicated manual saves before Coming Home (Ch. 1), before Tourist Trap (Slot #03), before Checking Out (Slot #06), and before the Chapter 4 finale (Slot #10).

Can you reopen Donnie's Trailer Fence after Swamped?

No. Donnie's Trailer Fence closes permanently after Swamped; later chapters unlock Dunk (Port Gellhorn) and Tyrone/Yasmani/Randy (Vice City).
