GTA 6 CHAPTER 2 WALKTHROUGH · LUCIA CAMINOS · MOSES

Daily Driver Mission Walkthrough (Ch. 2): Free Port Gellhorn Garage & Vice City Real Estate App

Daily Driver is a mandatory Chapter 2 main story mission for Lucia Caminos given by Port Gellhorn car-broker Moses. Beyond its cash payout ($1,250 base plus up to a $500 condition bonus), Daily Driver is a major progression milestone because finishing it gifts you the free Port Gellhorn Station Garage and installs the Vice City Real Estate app on your phone.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough: Stealing the '82 Canis Crawler

  1. Check Moses's Text & Reference Photo: Shortly after completing earlier Port Gellhorn work for Moses, he texts Lucia a photo of the target vehicle—a white '82 Canis Crawler. You can re-open the photo at any time in your phone's Gallery app if you need to confirm the target.
  2. Scout the Traders' Union Compound: Drive to the marked Port Gellhorn search area near the old Traders' Union building. The Crawler sits inside a fenced yard watched by a small group of hostile guards.
  3. Choose Stealth or Loud Entry: You can crouch behind crates and cover to slip past or quietly neutralize the gate guards, or clear the yard aggressively. Either way, keep gunfire away from the Crawler itself—vehicle damage directly reduces your condition bonus.
  4. Pop the Door with the Slim Jim & Hotwire: Approach the '82 Canis Crawler, use your Slim Jim (unlocked in Moses) to open the door, and hotwire the ignition.
  5. Smash the Gate & Outrun the Pursuit Truck: Starting the hotwire alerts the remaining guards. Ram straight through the compound's metal gates and head back toward Moses's garage at Watkins Auto Parts. A hostile pickup will pursue and fire at you—weave through side streets to minimize bodywork damage.
  6. Deliver the Crawler to Moses: Pull into Moses's drop-off to collect your base $1,250 plus condition bonus and trigger the post-mission unlock texts.
Tip for Maximum Payout: Avoid ramming oncoming traffic on the return leg. Even moderate gunfire or bumper collisions chip away at the $500 pristine-condition bonus.

What Unlocks After Daily Driver: Free Garage & Real Estate App

Free Port Gellhorn Station Garage

Shortly after the delivery wraps up, Moses texts Lucia granting ownership of the Port Gellhorn Station Garage at no cost. Driving a vehicle into an owned garage bay stores it safely so it will not despawn.

Vice City Real Estate App

The same follow-up installs the Vice City Real Estate app on your phone, letting you browse and buy additional multi-slot garages across Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, and Vice City. Reported prices start around $4,000 for local lots (such as Mitchell Auto Center) and scale to $20,000–$40,000+ for larger spaces. See our All 13 Garages Guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you unlock the Vice City Real Estate app in GTA 6?

Complete Chapter 2's mandatory Daily Driver mission for Moses as Lucia Caminos. After delivering the '82 Canis Crawler, Moses texts you both the free Port Gellhorn Station Garage and the Vice City Real Estate app.

How much money does Daily Driver pay?

Corroborated launch guides report a $1,250 base payout plus up to a $500 vehicle-condition bonus (roughly $1,375 to $1,750 total depending on damage).

Can you buy houses with the Vice City Real Estate app?

No—Story Mode safehouses are story-unlocked. The Vice City Real Estate app is used to purchase additional vehicle garages across Leonida.
