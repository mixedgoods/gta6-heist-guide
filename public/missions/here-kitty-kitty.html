Here, Kitty Kitty Mission Walkthrough (Ch. 3): Unlock Tyrone's Fence in La Perle

Here, Kitty Kitty is a Chapter 3 main story mission for Jason Duval that triggers right after meeting Tyrone in Vice City to investigate a synthetic street drug called "Kitty Litter." Completing the mission permanently unlocks Tyrone as a Stolen Goods Fence (Drop Off – Goods) in the La Perle area.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Read Dook's Text Message: After wrapping up the opening conversation at Tyrone's apartment, check Jason's phone. Dook sends the target address—209 Warren Thacker Manor—along with a reference photo of a junkie named Charles.
  2. Question the Local Outside the Building: Drive to 209 Warren Thacker Manor in the La Perle / Rockridge area. Outside the apartment, you meet a local hanging around and can choose to Bribe ($20) or Intimidate him. Choosing Intimidate causes him to swear he knows nothing and run off.
  3. Force Entry & Search Charles's Apartment: Knock on Charles's door; when nobody answers, break the door open. Search the interior carefully—inspect the empty baggie in the kitchen and pick up the Aline's Thrift Store receipt on the bedroom bedside table.
  4. Head to Aline's Thrift Store & Chase Charles: Follow the receipt lead to Aline's Thrift Store. Spotting Charles triggers an on-foot pursuit through the surrounding structures and up onto the elevated levels/rooftops.
  5. Retrieve the Bag of Kitty Litter: Keep pursuing Charles until he reaches a top-level barrier and attempts to jump off. Follow the stairs/path back down to street level and search Charles's body to recover the bag of Kitty Litter.
  6. Complete & Unlock Tyrone's Fence: Once you secure the bag, Jason texts Lucia confirming that Tyrone is locked in as an active Vice City contact and permanent stolen-goods fence.

Why Unlocking Tyrone Early in Chapter 3 Matters

Convenient Vice City Drop-Off

Before Chapter 3, selling stolen goods meant driving back toward Port Gellhorn (Dunk) or the Keys (Brian). Unlocking Tyrone gives you a dedicated Vice City goods fence right inside La Perle.

Pairs With Yasmani & Randy

Combine Tyrone (general stolen goods) with Yasmani (high-rate jewelry buyer in Little Cuba) and Randy (Balsero high-end vehicle wish-list buyer) to cover every Chapter 3 robbery payout tier. See All 7 Fences & Vehicle Buyers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you unlock Tyrone's fence in GTA 6?

Complete the Chapter 3 Jason Duval story mission Here, Kitty Kitty. Recovering the bag of Kitty Litter from Charles permanently unlocks Tyrone as a stolen-goods fence in La Perle.

Should you bribe or intimidate the local outside 209 Warren Thacker Manor?

Either choice lets you proceed into Charles's apartment by forcing the door open—intimidating him saves the $20 bribe and sends him running off.

What clues do you need inside Charles's apartment?

Inspect the empty baggie in the kitchen and the Aline's Thrift Store receipt on the bedroom bedside table to advance the mission to the thrift store chase.
