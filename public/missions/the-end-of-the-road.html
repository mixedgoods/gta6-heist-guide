The End of the Road Walkthrough (Ch. 5 Finale): GTA 6 Final Story Mission

The End of the Road (Mission #117) is the final main story mission of Grand Theft Auto VI, starting in Mariana County (Key Lento) right after Protective Custody. Completing it rolls the end credits and unlocks the full post-story free-roam and 100% completion sweep.

Critical Pre-Finale Check: Abe's $150K Fee & The "$2M" Myth

Do NOT Grind $2 Million Unnecessarily: During Unfinished Business / Honest Abe before the finale, texts from Abe Gonzalez's Lucretia Law Firm imply you need $2,000,000 in Family Savings. Corroborated PowerPyx and IGN guides confirm this is optional bait—you only need to pay $150,000 via the BuckMe app (Pay Abe's Fee) to advance!

Deposit All Loose Cash Into Family Savings: Before starting the final stretch, transfer every spare dollar on Jason and Lucia into Family Savings. Loose pocket cash can be temporarily locked/lost during the ending sequence, while Family Savings carries cleanly into post-story free roam.

Spoiler-Shielded Finale Walkthrough

  1. Gear Up at Brian's Hangar: Select your full endgame arsenal—high-capacity assault rifles, heavy armor, and explosives—before lifting off.
  2. Chopper Turret Assault on Giorgio's Penthouse: Fly to Giorgio Gallo's penthouse and man the helicopter turret to wipe out balcony guards and rooftop resistance.
  3. Breach the Penthouse & Retrieve the Hard Drive: Push through heavy interior waves to reach Giorgio's office, access his computer terminal, and secure the hard drive.
  4. Evade Enemy Helicopter Pursuit & Regroup: Break away from hostile chase choppers, reunite with Lucia, and drive to the final safe rendezvous (Cal's house / Fleming Key) to conclude the GTA 6 campaign.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you really need $2 million in Family Savings to beat GTA 6?

No. The $2 million text from Abe's law firm is optional bait; you only need $150,000 via BuckMe to pay Abe's fee and unlock the finale.

What happens to your money at the end of GTA 6?

Deposit all loose cash into Family Savings before the final stretch. Loose pocket cash on Jason or Lucia can be lost or locked during the finale, whereas Family Savings carries safely into post-game free roam.

Can you keep playing after The End of the Road?

Yes. Completing The End of the Road unlocks full open-world free roam so you can finish side missions, collectibles, stunts, and 100% completion.
