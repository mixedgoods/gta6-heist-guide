Checking Out Mission Walkthrough: Starlet Motel Raid & Chapter 2 Finale

Checking Out is Chapter 2's Point of No Return finale—it permanently closes the Starlet Motel safehouse. Requires completing prior Chapter 2 story missions, holding $15,000, and receiving Cal's warning text. Grab the Moreland 850 Shotgun off the bed when the raid starts, regroup with Lucia by the cafeteria, and escape Port Gellhorn to unlock Chapter 3 in Vice City.

Step-by-Step Objectives & Walkthrough

  1. Finish Port Gellhorn Errands & Lock Save Slot #06: Before interacting with the motel bathroom door, make sure you have $15,000 saved, grabbed the fridge collectible card, and created a manual backup in Slot #06.
  2. Interact With the Bathroom Door to Begin Packing: After receiving Cal's text/news alert about a murdered couple near Port Gellhorn, return to your Starlet Motel room and use the bathroom door to trigger the packing sequence.
  3. Grab the Moreland 850 Shotgun Off the Bed: When hostile forces and corrupt cops raid the motel mid-pack, immediately grab the Moreland 850 Shotgun from the bed.
  4. Push Through the Motel Exterior to the Cafeteria: Fight out of the room and clear the courtyard path toward the cafeteria area to regroup with Lucia.
  5. Hold Off Waves & Break Out to the Main Road: Cover Lucia while holding off incoming waves, then follow Jason's breakout push toward the main highway.
  6. Drop the Charging Biker & Escape in Tandem: Eliminate the hostile biker charging toward Lucia so she can take his motorcycle while Jason commandeers a street car. Break line-of-sight and clear the pursuit zone to trigger the transition cutscene toward Vice City.

Optional Objectives, Missables & Decisions

Missables & Cutoffs: Permanent safehouse closure: The Starlet Motel closes forever once Checking Out starts. Grab the Moreland 850 Shotgun off the bed during the raid.
Important Decisions: No ending-branching dialogue occurs in Checking Out, but saving in Slot #06 beforehand preserves your Port Gellhorn Chapter 2 sandbox.

Rewards, Unlocks & Map References

Concludes Chapter 2 (Paradise Lost), awards the Moreland 850 Shotgun during combat, and unlocks Chapter 3's opener (Welcome to Vice City), where Jason and Lucia meet Dre'Quan at The Jack of Hearts club (corroborated by PowerPyx, IGN, GTA Base, Eurogamer, and Insider Gaming).

Frequently Asked Questions

How much money do you need to start Checking Out in GTA 6?

You need $15,000 accumulated alongside completing the preceding Chapter 2 main story missions and receiving Cal's warning alert.

Can you return to the Starlet Motel after Checking Out?

No. Checking Out is Chapter 2's point of no return and permanently closes the Starlet Motel safehouse.

Where do you get the Moreland 850 Shotgun?

Grab the Moreland 850 Shotgun off the motel bed immediately when the raid begins during Checking Out.
