Buck Me Mission Walkthrough (Chapter 1 — BuckMe Family Savings)

Buck Me is an early Chapter 1 story mission at the Key Lento Fleeca branch in the Leonida Keys. It introduces the BuckMe fintech app and unlocks the Buck Yourself transfer from Checking into Family Savings—protecting your cash from Wasted/Busted penalties as you build toward Chapter 1's $5,000 savings milestone.

Step-by-Step Objectives & Walkthrough

  1. Drive to the Key Lento Fleeca Branch: Follow the main mission marker to the local Fleeca branch in Key Lento (Mariana County) to trigger the opening scene with Jason and Lucia.
  2. Register the BuckMe Account on Your Phone: Follow the on-screen smartphone prompts to open the BuckMe fintech app and link your active Checking balance to a shared Family Savings ledger.
  3. Execute Your First 'Buck Yourself' Transfer: Transfer cash from Checking into Family Savings to complete the tutorial objective. Once locked in Family Savings, that balance is shielded from Wasted or Busted deductions.
  4. Plan Your Chapter 1 $5,000 Target: After the mission clears, use BuckMe regularly whenever you finish Keys robberies or fence runs so you never carry large sums of unprotected cash.

Optional Objectives, Missables & Decisions

Missables & Cutoffs: There are no permanently missable collectibles inside Buck Me, but skipping BuckMe deposits later in Chapter 1 leaves your pocket cash exposed if you get Wasted or Busted during Leonida Keys robberies.
Important Decisions: No branching story choices occur in Buck Me. Your only practical decision is how much spare Checking cash to move into Family Savings right away—moving everything above a small ammo/supplies buffer is the safest habit.

Does BuckMe Family Savings protect my money if I get Wasted or Busted?

Yes. Cash transferred from Checking into Family Savings via the BuckMe app is shielded from Wasted and Busted deductions during Chapter 1.

Existing site property records track a $5,000 Chapter 1 Family Savings goal linked to BuckMe in the Leonida Keys.
