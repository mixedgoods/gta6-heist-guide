The Jack of Hearts Guide (Chapter 3 — Dre’Quan, Boobie Ike & Club Operations)

The Jack of Hearts is Boobie Ike’s flagship Crosstown strip club and the narrative nerve center of early Chapter 3: Tits, Ass, Drug Money. Introduced during Welcome to Vice City and recurring across club-centric jobs like Boobie Time, it is where Jason and Lucia link up with Dre’Quan Priest, launch Vice City record-label errands, and transition toward major Chapter 3 heists.

How The Jack of Hearts Sequence Plays Out in Chapter 3

  1. Unlock Access in Welcome to Vice City: Reach Crosstown at the start of Chapter 3 and enter the rear staff door to meet Dre’Quan Priest and Boobie Ike.
  2. Navigate the Club Floor & Upstairs Office: Decide whether to join the ground-floor play-money interaction (R1 / RB) or head straight upstairs with Dre’Quan for Boobie’s briefing and your Bayside Apartments keys.
  3. Return for Boobie & Dre’Quan Follow-Ups: Throughout early Chapter 3, check your Vice City map and phone texts for recurring markers at The Jack of Hearts—these missions expand Boobie Ike’s club business and Dre’Quan’s record-label subplot.
  4. Pair Club Visits with Vice City Nightlife & Collectibles: While operating around Crosstown and Downtown Vice City, stop by nearby NINE1NINE Nightclub and scout Vice City’s Jewelry District to fund your Chapter 3 gear before tackling Credit Check.

Choices, Missables & Verification Notes

Naming Clarification (Mission vs. Venue): Some early Chapter 3 checklists list The Jack of Hearts as a standalone mission title, while corroborated launch walkthroughs (IGN, PowerPyx, Push Square) clarify that The Jack of Hearts is primarily the club venue and recurring hub introduced in Welcome to Vice City and revisited during subsequent Boobie/Dre’Quan missions (such as Boobie Time). We document both cleanly here so you never get stuck looking for a missing map icon.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is The Jack of Hearts located in GTA 6?

The Jack of Hearts is located in Crosstown, Vice City, and is first visited at the very start of Chapter 3 during Welcome to Vice City.

Is The Jack of Hearts a standalone mission or a club hub?

Corroborated launch guides clarify that it is the club venue owned by Boobie Ike—featured prominently in Welcome to Vice City and revisited across early Chapter 3 club missions.

Who runs The Jack of Hearts?

Vice City mogul Boobie Ike owns The Jack of Hearts, with Dre’Quan Priest working closely out of the venue.
