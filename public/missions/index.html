How many missions are in GTA 6? 91 vs 93 explained

INDEPENDENTLY REPORTED PC Gamer's published mission list reports 91 story missions across five chapters, and Press Start separately reports 93 entries from the in-game Story Recap. Rockstar has not published an official mission count that this site could cite, so neither number is labelled Rockstar-confirmed.

Why the numbers can differ (as attributed by the reporting, not reproduced in-game by this site): a recap that lists every intermediate story entry will count more than a guide that only counts missions required to advance the story, and some Chapter 2 jobs are treated as optional by one count and as story entries by another. The reported chapter split adds up to 91 (22 + 11 + 26 + 25 + 7).

Chapter 1: Just Like Paradise (7 Completed · 15 Pending)

Gone Fishing — Early Chapter 1 Smuggling Run & Pelican Valley Rescue RELIABLE REPORTING
  • Starting Location: Leonida Keys docks (first full gameplay mission following the Prologue).
  • Core Objectives: Meet Donnie to skim contraband bricks from a waterborne shipment, survive the Kingfishers ambush in Pelican Valley, rescue Donnie, and escape back to the Keys.
  • Step-by-Step Walkthrough: (1) Board the boat and practice shallow-water marsh handling through the Keys channels. (2) When the drop goes south in Pelican Valley, take hard cover behind the wooden dock pilings and clear flanking Kingfishers gunmen using your radar. (3) Pull Donnie clear of the crossfire and pilot back to safety.
  • Rewards & Map Connections: Establishes Donnie's early Chapter 1 smuggling network — see Donnie's Fence Record and Pelican Valley / Grassrivers on the Leonida Map.
Buck Me — BuckMe App & Family Savings Setup EXISTING SITE DATA RELIABLE REPORTING
  • Starting Location: Fleeca branch, Key Lento (Leonida Keys).
  • Unlock Requirements: Early Chapter 1 main story progression.
  • Core Objectives: Set up the BuckMe fintech app with Jason and Lucia, then use Buck Yourself to transfer cash from Checking into Family Savings.
  • Step-by-Step Walkthrough: (1) Arrive at the Key Lento Fleeca branch and trigger the mission cutscene. (2) Follow the phone prompts to register the BuckMe account. (3) Complete your first Checking → Family Savings transfer—funds moved into Family Savings are locked and protected from Wasted/Busted loss.
  • Rewards & Unlocks: Unlocks protected Family Savings storage for Chapter 1's $5,000 goal.
  • Save Recommendation: Keep a clean Chapter 1 checkpoint in Slot #01–#02 via the 15-Slot Save Planner.
Lab Fees — Moonstone Boutique (0420) & Brian's Lockup (7700) EXISTING SITE DATA RELIABLE REPORTING
  • Starting Location: Key Lento / Leonida Keys (Moonstone Boutique → Brian's Lockup).
  • Core Objectives: Access the Moonstone Boutique back-room laboratory to retrieve the acid, then transport and stash it at Brian's Lockup further south in the Leonida Keys.
  • Step-by-Step Walkthrough: (1) Head to Moonstone Boutique in Key Lento and enter keypad code 0420 on the back-room lab door to collect the acid. (2) Drive south through the Leonida Keys to Brian's Lockup—avoid unnecessary police heat during transit. (3) Enter keypad code 7700 on Brian's Lockup door to stash the acid and unlock ongoing fence access.
  • Key Codes & Related Map Pin: 0420 (Moonstone Lab) and 7700 (Brian's Lockup) — see Brian's Lockup on the Interactive Map and Complete Safe Codes Database.
Island Dreams — Leonida Keys Cay Recon & Odd Job RELIABLE REPORTING
  • Starting Location: Leonida Keys (mid-Chapter 1 progression for local Keys bosses).
  • Core Objectives: Navigate across the outer island chain, scout an isolated cay target using stealth, and clear light opposition while watching for coastal wildlife hazards.
  • Step-by-Step Walkthrough: (1) Stage a reliable boat or off-road vehicle before launching. (2) Approach the target cay quietly to scout guard positions before engaging. (3) Stay clear of marsh banks where hostile alligators patrol (see Verified Wildlife Compendium).
  • Related Tools: Leonida Keys Map Pins · Early-Game Weapons Loadouts.
Coming Home — Picking Up Lucia from Hamlet Prison (Missable Relationship Boosts) RELIABLE REPORTING
  • Starting Location: Jason's safehouse → Hamlet Prison.
  • Missable Pre-Mission Choices (Recommended Before Triggering): Complete Jason's safehouse repairs (roof, steps, bedroom wall), pick up groceries and flowers, get a haircut, and buy the $475 necklace at The Siren's Chest. Completing these optional errands maximizes your early relationship rating with Lucia.
  • Step-by-Step Walkthrough: (1) Wait for Lucia's phone call confirming her early release clearance. (2) Drive to Hamlet Prison to pick her up. (3) Escort Lucia back to the Keys safehouse to unlock joint Chapter 1 operations.
  • Related Guides: Jason & Lucia Relationship Guide ("Hot Together" 4/4) · Save Planner.
Swamped — Wetlands Ambush & Donnie's Fence Cutoff Warning EXISTING SITE DATA RELIABLE REPORTING
  • Starting Location: Leonida wetlands / Grassrivers border.
  • Pre-Mission Cutoff Warning: Donnie's fence closes permanently after Swamped. Sell any marked loot beforehand, and check texts from Cal (e.g., Rusty Anchor bar optional clothing interaction) before starting.
  • Step-by-Step Walkthrough: (1) Equip a mid-to-long-range rifle before entering the marsh. (2) Stick to elevated dry paths or swamp watercraft to avoid mud slowdowns. (3) Clear the wetlands ambush from range before advancing through dense brush.
  • Related Links: Donnie's Fence Record · Rifles Database.
Day Release — Vice City Superyacht Mission (Mission #17, Mattia & Kat) RELIABLE REPORTING
  • Starting Location & Quest Giver: The Rusty Anchor bar, Key Lento (Mariana County) — Mattia Tauro & Katerina Rosselli (playable as Jason & Lucia).
  • Step-by-Step Walkthrough: (1) Order a drink at the back of The Rusty Anchor to trigger the opening cutscene where Burim temporarily removes Lucia's ankle monitor. (2) Take the speedboat from the rear dock and drive north to the superyacht anchored off Vice City. (3) Explore the yacht deck (speak to guests near the railing for an optional free pair of unique sunglasses) and head below deck to locate Mattia. (4) During the jet ski race, choose to have Lucia ride with Jason for a Relationship heart boost (winning or losing the race does not fail the mission). (5) Complete the champagne-bottle shooting sequence, then follow Mattia ashore to Vice City nightlife stops (Huxley Motel & Nine1Nine) to finish the mission.
  • Clarification: There is no separate pre-launch "$12M Starlet Superyacht Vault Heist" in Story Mode—Day Release is the verified Chapter 1 Vice City yacht mission.
  • Related Guides: Jason & Lucia Relationship Guide · Vice City Map Pins.
Tourist Trap — Chapter 1 Point of No Return Gauntlet EXISTING SITE DATA RELIABLE REPORTING
  • Role in Story: Hard Chapter 1 Point of No Return — starting Tourist Trap locks you into a back-to-back finale sequence (Tourist Trap → Home Sweet Home → Discharge Time) with no free-roam breaks between missions.
  • Pre-Mission Checklist: (1) Move excess cash into BuckMe Family Savings. (2) Finish all remaining Chapter 1 errands and fence sales. (3) Lock a manual backup save in Slot #03 via the 15-Slot Save Planner.
  • Walkthrough Tips: Blend in during the opening stealth phase to keep heat low, then use Jason and Lucia's crossfire positioning to hold tight crowd bottlenecks once security goes loud.

Chapter 2: Paradise Lost (5 Completed · 6 Pending)

Girls' Trip — Vicki's Motel Confrontation, Delights & Port Gellhorn Car Wash RELIABLE REPORTING
  • Starting Location: Vicki's motel room (follows Just Moved In).
  • Core Objectives: Catch Vicki's "Zombix" pill switch, ride with her to Port Gellhorn, visit Delights strip club, locate Dunk at the car wash, and defuse the confrontation.
  • Step-by-Step Walkthrough: (1) Before paying Vicki $50, check the motel bathroom to catch her swapping in fake "Zombix" pills. (2) Force the bathroom door open (Square / X), win the brief melee prompt, and help her up. (3) Ride passenger with Vicki into Port Gellhorn, escort her into Delights to collect her pay, then defuse the standoff with Dunk at the car wash and get him home safely.
  • Map Connection: Port Gellhorn Map Pins.
Moses — Watkins Auto Parts, WAiNK Scanner & Slim Jim Tutorial (Spoiler-Shielded) RELIABLE REPORTING

⚠ Spoiler Warning: Contains Chapter 2 story progression and vehicle-theft system details.

  • Starting Location: Watkins Auto Parts (MW map icon), Port Gellhorn, Kelly County.
  • Prerequisites: Complete Girls' Trip in Chapter 2; played as Lucia. You must arrive in a modest, low-profile street vehicle—flashy sports cars, supercars, or vehicles parked right outside the garage will not satisfy Vicki's tip.
  • Chronological Walkthrough:
    1. Grab an older, everyday sedan, coupe, or truck off the streets of Port Gellhorn and drive directly into Watkins Auto Parts (MW) to trigger your introduction to Moses.
    2. Follow Moses out behind the garage to the back lot where several test vehicles are staged.
    3. Open your smartphone (D-Pad Up), launch the WAiNK app, and scan the parked vehicles (R1 / RB) to inspect their security tiers (no tracker, standard tracker, or advanced tracker). Older budget cars without trackers are safe to register or fence.
    4. Collect the Slim Jim tool from the workbench beside Moses.
    5. Practice quiet vehicle entry by using the Slim Jim on the designated yard vehicle (such as the Montagne XL) without setting off an alarm.
    6. Complete the personal-vehicle registration prompt: if the car you drove in has no tracker, Moses registers it as your personal vehicle for free (if it carries a tracker, Moses prompts you to bring an untracked vehicle later).
  • Completion Condition: Finish the WAiNK tracker scan, Slim Jim break-in tutorial, and personal vehicle registration walkthrough with Moses.
  • Sourced Rewards & Unlocks: Permanently unlocks the Slim Jim stealth-entry tool, the Scooter Bros phone delivery app for summoning your registered car, vehicle fencing/registration at Watkins Auto Parts, and follow-up vehicle jobs (corroborated by PowerPyx, IGN, GTA Base, and Push Square).
  • Verified Links: Port Gellhorn on the Leonida Map · Vehicles Database · Safe Codes Database (code 2727 for the '74 Vamos).
Slam Dunk — Dr. Bill Stakeout, Kolts Dock Compound & Wild Hog Point Ambush (Spoiler-Shielded) RELIABLE REPORTING

⚠ Spoiler Warning: Contains Chapter 2 story twists, an ambush shootout, and an NPC death.

  • Starting Location: Dunk's Trailer, Tanglewood Lane, Port Gellhorn (Kelly County).
  • Prerequisites: Early-to-mid Chapter 2 progression while Jason is recovering and needs stronger painkillers; played as Lucia.
  • Chronological Walkthrough:
    1. Meet Dunk outside his trailer on Tanglewood Lane, then follow him on foot through the neighborhood (including fence hops) to a local grocery store frequented by retired doctor Dr. Bill Kolts.
    2. Position yourself beside Dunk behind the dumpster across the street until time skips ahead to roughly 6:00 PM and Dr. Bill arrives.
    3. Cross the street into the store behind Dunk to confront Dr. Bill, then trail him out to his Willard Transient where Dunk forces Dr. Bill (and his dog, Peaches) inside.
    4. Take the wheel and drive Dunk and Dr. Bill to the Kolts Gang dock compound in Port Gellhorn.
    5. Once waved through the security gate, drive slowly behind the walking guard toward the office—speeding or erratic driving blows your cover early.
    6. When the Kolts spot the setup and open fire, reverse out of the yard, flee the docks, and clear the minimap search zone to shake the pursuing Kolts units.
    7. After Dr. Bill phones his contact (Declan) and leaps from the moving car—knocking himself unconscious—pick him up, load him into the trunk, and drive southwest of Port Gellhorn to the exchange point near Wild Hog Point (behind Wild Hog Airfield).
    8. When the trade springs a trap and the Kolts shoot Dr. Bill, take hard cover behind your vehicle, use Focus Mode to spot flanking enemies and rooftop shooters, and eliminate the ambush squad (grab dropped SMGs/ammo if needed).
    9. Loot Dr. Bill's body (~$1,000–$1,145 reported), locate Dunk, and leave the area before law enforcement arrives.
  • Completion Condition: Survive the Wild Hog Point shootout, recover Dunk/painkiller lead, and escape the area before police lock it down.
  • Sourced Rewards & Unlocks: Unlocks Dunk's Trailer (Tanglewood Lane) as a permanent stolen-goods fence, plus a follow-up $1,700 BuckMe payout from Dunk the next day (corroborated by IGN, PowerPyx, GTA Base, GTABoom, and MetaForge).
  • Verified Links: Port Gellhorn Region Map · Properties & Fences Database.
Coffee Break — Unlocks Free Jason/Lucia Switching & Bret Vogel Hunting Contracts RELIABLE REPORTING
  • Starting Location: Starlet Motel communal kitchen (immediately follows Breakfast Run).
  • Step-by-Step Walkthrough: (1) Walk with Lucia to the motel kitchen; talk with Wyman (who introduces the Thrillbilly Mud Club) and grab the collectible card pinned to the fridge. (2) Buy a $2 coffee at the machine and sit with Lucia for the planning scene. (3) Return to the car to unlock free character switching between Jason and Lucia (hold Right on D-Pad), then scout the unlocked Port Gellhorn leads (Bowling Alley, Pawn Shop, Mud Club) in any order.
  • Major Unlocks: Free protagonist switching + unlocks Jason's Veteran Hunted quest at Killtelligence Provisions for Bret Vogel's 15 Hunting Target Decks (required for 100% completion).
Checking Out — Chapter 2 Finale, Starlet Motel Raid & Move to Vice City (Spoiler-Shielded) EXISTING SITE DATA RELIABLE REPORTING

⚠ Spoiler & Cutoff Warning: Chapter 2 point of no return—permanently closes the Starlet Motel safehouse. Finish Port Gellhorn errands and lock a manual save in Slot #06 via the Save Planner before starting.

  • Starting Location: Starlet Motel, Port Gellhorn (interact with the bathroom door inside your motel room).
  • Prerequisites: Complete the preceding Chapter 2 main story missions (through Afternoon Delight / Coffee Break), accumulate $15,000, and receive Cal's text/news alert about a murdered couple near Port Gellhorn.
  • Chronological Walkthrough:
    1. Return to your room at the Starlet Motel after Cal's warning and interact with the bathroom door to start packing up.
    2. When hostile forces/corrupt cops raid the motel mid-pack, grab the Moreland 850 Shotgun off the bed.
    3. Fight your way out of the room and push through the motel exterior toward the cafeteria area to regroup with Lucia.
    4. Hold off incoming hostile waves together and follow Jason as he breaks out toward the main road.
    5. Drop the hostile biker charging toward Lucia so she can take his motorcycle while Jason commandeers a car off the street.
    6. Ride and drive in tandem to break line-of-sight and clear the pursuit zone, triggering the transition cutscene toward Vice City.
  • Completion Condition: Survive the Starlet Motel shootout, reach the main road, and lose all pursuers to leave Port Gellhorn.
  • Sourced Rewards & Progression: Concludes Chapter 2 (Paradise Lost) and unlocks the Chapter 3 opener (Welcome to Vice City), where Jason and Lucia link up with Dre'Quan at The Jack of Hearts club (corroborated by PowerPyx, IGN, GTA Base, Eurogamer, and Insider Gaming).
  • Verified Links: Starlet Motel Safehouse Record · 15-Slot Save Planner · Port Gellhorn Map.

Chapter 3 & Chapter 4: Major Story Missions & Heists (3 Completed · 48 Pending)

Credit Check — Chapter 3 Raul Bautista Three-Bank Fleeca Run & Dye Pack Jammer (Spoiler-Shielded) EXISTING SITE DATA RELIABLE REPORTING

⚠ Spoiler Warning: Chapter 3 (Tits, Ass, Drug Money) story heist introducing Raul Bautista's crew and multi-branch Fleeca robberies.

  • Starting Location: Meetup near the Vice Vinyl mod stockyard/shop in Vice City (use the buzzer/intercom by the blue door next to the ladder).
  • Prerequisites: Chapter 3 progression; requires both Jason and Lucia together to launch Raul Bautista's job.
  • Chronological Walkthrough:
    1. Bring both protagonists to the Vice Vinyl meetup marker and buzz the intercom by the blue door to meet Raul Bautista and his crew (Andrea, Karim, and Felipe).
    2. Head upstairs to gear up: select a mask set with Andrea (Old People or Caricature—cosmetic cutscene choice), speak with Karim to collect the Dye Pack Jammer, and grab available supplies/sticky bombs from the tables.
    3. Board Felipe's getaway vehicle and hit the first target: Fleeca Bank – Washington Beach. Control tellers/bystanders, breach security access, and use the Dye Pack Jammer before bagging marked cash bundles.
    4. Chain directly into the two Fleeca Bank – Ocean Beach branches, adapting to tighter security layouts and rising police response as you keep hostages controlled and clear each register/vault.
    5. After clearing the third branch, evade the heavy VCPD wanted pursuit through Vice City alleys and side streets (swapping vehicles if necessary) until you break line-of-sight.
    6. Regroup at the designated drop-off point once the heat clears to complete the operation.
  • Completion Condition: Rob all three designated Fleeca Bank branches, lose the VCPD pursuit, and reach the final drop-off point.
  • Sourced Rewards & Unlocks: Gross marked take of $31,860 (yielding roughly $6,000 net payout to Jason and Lucia after laundering, per GTA Base / community reports), permanently unlocks the Dye Pack Jammer for free-roam Small Banks & Payday Loans robberies, and advances Raul Bautista's Chapter 3 heist arc (corroborated by IGN, PowerPyx, GTA Base, GamesRadar, and Sportskeeda).
  • Verified Links: Small Banks & Payday Loans Guide · Squad Heist Planner · Vice City Map.

Frequently asked questions

How many missions are in GTA 6?

PC Gamer's published list reports 91 story missions across five chapters, while Press Start reports 93 entries from the in-game Story Recap. Rockstar has not published an official count that this site can cite, and we have not yet reproduced either number in-game.

What are the GTA 6 chapters?

Reporting names five chapters: Just Like Paradise, Paradise Lost, Tits, Ass, Drug Money, Vice City, Baby, and Top of the World, Ma. This is independently reported, not confirmed by a Rockstar page we could cite.

Does GTA 6 have Strangers and Freaks missions?

We could not verify that label. Guides describe a few optional or borderline Chapter 2 missions, so this site calls them optional missions until the in-game classification is checked.

Can you replay missions in GTA 6?

This site's existing guidance is that there is no mission-replay menu at launch, so use manual save slots before points of no return. Check the Save Planner for a slot plan.

Can I track missions without spoilers?

Yes. Spoiler-free mode is on by default: mission titles are blurred and can be revealed one at a time or all at once, while the checkboxes still save in your browser.
