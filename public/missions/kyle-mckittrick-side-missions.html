Kyle McKittrick Side Missions Walkthrough (McKittrick Farm)

Kyle McKittrick unlocks in Chapter 2 after inspecting a job flyer (such as the posting outside the Starlet Motel in Port Gellhorn) and visiting McKittrick Farm. Unlike missable Chapter 1 errands, completing Kyle’s strand counts toward the 8-strand Strangers & Freaks Silver trophy.

How to Start & Track Kyle McKittrick’s Strand

  1. Inspect the Flyer Outside the Starlet Motel: Once Chapter 2 opens up Port Gellhorn free roam, look for the posted advert outside the Starlet Motel (or nearby community boards) to add Check Out McKittrick Farm to your map.
  2. Travel to McKittrick Farm: Drive out from Port Gellhorn to McKittrick Farm and speak with Kyle to begin his eccentric farm and stunt-adjacent jobs.
  3. Watch for Phone Calls Between Stages: GTA 6 spaces out stranger chapters deliberately. After completing an objective for Kyle, do a main story mission or free-roam robbery until Kyle texts or calls to unlock the next purple marker.
  4. Verify Trophy Progress on Your Console Dashboard: Because purple icons can stack on the map, check your PlayStation/Xbox tracker for the Strangers & Freaks Silver trophy before and after Kyle’s final stage to confirm the increment.

Note on Overlap with Stunt Strands

Tracker Clarity: Some early launch checklists group McKittrick Farm’s stunt-work intro alongside Vice City’s Papa Audacity sequence. Follow the in-game purple marker chain from McKittrick Farm and confirm your console achievement counter ticks up by +1 when the strand concludes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you trigger Kyle McKittrick in GTA 6?

In Chapter 2, interact with the job flyer outside the Starlet Motel in Port Gellhorn to reveal McKittrick Farm on your map.

Does Kyle McKittrick count toward the Strangers & Freaks trophy?

Yes. Kyle is one of the qualifying strands for the 8-strand Strangers & Freaks Silver trophy.

Can you finish Kyle McKittrick after beating the main story?

Yes, Chapter 2–4 qualifying stranger strands without hourglass timers remain completable in post-story free roam.
