Headliners Mission Walkthrough (Ch. 4): Club VYB Launch & DWNFALL Trophy

Headliners (objective: Help With The Launch Party) is the explosive finale to Dre'Quan Priest's Chapter 4 music arc at Club VYB (Washington Beach). After prepping the DJ lights and VIP floor for The Dimez, rival crew DWNPLY storms the green room—rewarding Brass Knuckles, up to ~$50,000, and the DWNFALL trophy.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Arrive at Club VYB in Washington Beach: Head to Club VYB once Dre'Quan calls to prep the grand reopening launch party for The Dimez.
  2. Complete Club Prep Tasks: Step into the DJ booth as Lucia to test the stage lighting and effects (exit with Circle / B), assign the VIP tables, check on Maleek Rivers, and check on + photograph KD.
  3. Meet Dre'Quan & Escort the VIP Arrival: Meet Dre'Quan near the entrance for the final tour, open the car door, escort Sexxy Red to the dance floor, and clear out unruly patrons to free up a VIP table.
  4. Fight Through DWNPLY's Ambush: When rival crew DWNPLY crashes Club VYB and takes The Dimez hostage in the green room, fight room-to-room through the club alongside Jason and Lucia.
  5. Subdue DWNPLY & Rescue The Dimez: Push into the green room, grab and subdue DWNPLY to free The Dimez, and watch their headline set to claim your cash payout, Brass Knuckles, and the DWNFALL trophy.

Trophy & Melee Weapon Unlock

Automatic Story Trophy: Completing Headliners unlocks both the DWNFALL trophy/achievement and the Brass Knuckles melee weapon. Make sure your weapon wheel has close-quarters firepower (shotgun or SMG) before walking into Club VYB.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you unlock the DWNFALL trophy in GTA 6?

Complete the Chapter 4 mission Headliners at Club VYB by defeating DWNPLY's ambush and rescuing The Dimez from the green room.

What weapon do you unlock in Headliners?

Finishing Headliners unlocks the Brass Knuckles melee weapon alongside a cash reward reported up to ~$50,000.
