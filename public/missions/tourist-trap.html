Tourist Trap Mission Walkthrough: Chapter 1 Point of No Return Gauntlet

Tourist Trap is Chapter 1's hard Point of No Return. Launching it locks you into a continuous three-mission finale gauntlet (Tourist Trap → Home Sweet Home → Discharge Time) with no free-roam breaks between missions. Deposit excess cash into BuckMe Family Savings, finish all Keys errands, and save in Slot #03 before starting.

Step-by-Step Objectives & Walkthrough

  1. Complete Your Pre-Mission Checklist: Move excess cash into BuckMe Family Savings, finish any remaining Leonida Keys fence sales or errands, restock ammo/body armor, and create a dedicated manual save in Slot #03.
  2. Blend In During the Opening Stealth Phase: Keep weapons holstered and follow crowd flow during the opening stealth segment to delay security detection and keep your heat minimal.
  3. Set Up Jason & Lucia Crossfire When Security Goes Loud: Once alarms trigger, avoid sprinting into open lanes. Position Jason and Lucia on opposite angles of tight crowd bottlenecks so incoming guards walk into overlapping fire.
  4. Push Through Home Sweet Home & Discharge Time: Stay stocked on armor and healing items as the story transitions directly into Home Sweet Home and Discharge Time without returning to free roam.

Optional Objectives, Missables & Decisions

Missables & Cutoffs: Any unfinished Chapter 1 Keys errands or fence turn-ins become inaccessible once the gauntlet begins—locking Slot #03 guarantees you can revisit Chapter 1 without restarting the campaign.
Important Decisions: Preparation is the defining choice here: entering with full armor, upgraded rifles/shotguns, and banked BuckMe cash prevents unnecessary restarts across the back-to-back finale.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I free-roam between Tourist Trap and Home Sweet Home?

No. Tourist Trap initiates a locked back-to-back Chapter 1 finale sequence (Tourist Trap, Home Sweet Home, and Discharge Time) with no free-roam breaks.

Which manual save slot should I use before Tourist Trap?

Our 15-Slot Save Planner recommends locking a clean Chapter 1 backup in Slot #03 before starting Tourist Trap.

What chapter starts after the Tourist Trap gauntlet?

Completing the three-mission sequence wraps up Chapter 1 and moves the story to Port Gellhorn for Chapter 2: Paradise Lost.
