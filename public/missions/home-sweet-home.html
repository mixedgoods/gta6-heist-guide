Home Sweet Home Mission Walkthrough (Chapter 1 Finale Gauntlet — Part 2)

Home Sweet Home (reported as Chapter 1, Mission #21) is the second leg of the locked, back-to-back Chapter 1 finale gauntlet (Tourist Trap → Home Sweet Home → Discharge Time). Playing as Lucia Caminos, you race back to Jason’s Key Lento safehouse only to find it ambushed and engulfed in flames.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Approach Jason’s Key Lento Safehouse: Immediately following the climax of Tourist Trap, Lucia arrives at Jason’s house to grab their stash before fleeing the Keys.
  2. Clear the Attackers Inside the Burning House: Enter the safehouse to find it ransacked and set ablaze by rival thugs. Use doorway cover, neutralize the shooters inside, and watch for flames spreading across the floor.
  3. Grab Tomás’ Photo (Missable Opportunity): Before sprinting out, check near the shelf for Tomás’ photo—corroborated launch guides report that grabbing it and reaching Andy’s car unlocks a missable jacket reward.
  4. Bail Out Before the House Blows: Realizing the stash cannot be safely recovered in time, sprint out the front door before the structure explodes.
  5. Slip Past the Key Lento Police Manhunt: Stay crouched, use the darkness and backyard cover to evade closing patrol cones, and steal a vehicle near the edge of the search zone (or slip out on foot toward Andy’s car) to trigger Discharge Time.

Choices, Missables & Verification Notes

Hard Point of No Return & Missable Reward: There is no free-roam break between Tourist Trap, Home Sweet Home, and Discharge Time—and because GTA 6 has no traditional mission-replay menu, missing Tomás’ photo here requires reloading Save Slot #03 (saved before Tourist Trap). Frequently Asked Questions

Can you free-roam between Tourist Trap and Home Sweet Home?

No. Starting Tourist Trap locks you into a back-to-back finale sequence covering Tourist Trap, Home Sweet Home, and Discharge Time.

Is there a missable item in Home Sweet Home?

Third-party walkthroughs (GTABase) report that picking up Tomás’ photo near the shelf inside the house and reaching Andy’s car unlocks a missable jacket reward.

Who do you play as in Home Sweet Home?

You play as Lucia Caminos as she returns to Jason’s Key Lento house and evades the police manhunt.
