Moses Mission Walkthrough: Watkins Auto Parts, WAiNK Scanner & Slim Jim Unlock

To trigger Moses at Watkins Auto Parts (MW icon, Port Gellhorn), arrive as Lucia in a modest, everyday street vehicle (no supercars or cars parked right outside). Follow Moses to the back lot, scan vehicles with the WAiNK app (R1/RB), pick up the Slim Jim, and register an untracked car to unlock the Scooter Bros delivery app.

Step-by-Step Objectives & Walkthrough

  1. Steal a Modest Street Vehicle First: Before pulling into Watkins Auto Parts (MW), grab an older, everyday sedan, coupe, or pickup off the Port Gellhorn streets. Flashy sports cars, supercars, or cars parked immediately outside the shop won't satisfy Vicki's tip.
  2. Follow Moses to the Back Lot: Drive directly into Watkins Auto Parts to meet Moses, then walk with him behind the garage where several test cars are lined up.
  3. Scan Vehicles With the WAiNK App: Open your phone (D-Pad Up), launch WAiNK, and press R1 / RB to scan each car's security tier: No Tracker, Standard Tracker, or Advanced Tracker. Older budget models without trackers are safe to keep or sell.
  4. Collect the Slim Jim & Practice Silent Entry: Pick up the Slim Jim off the workbench beside Moses and use it to quietly unlock the designated yard vehicle (such as the Montagne XL) without setting off the alarm.
  5. Register Your Free Personal Vehicle: Complete the registration prompt: if the car you arrived in has no tracker, Moses registers it as your Personal Vehicle for free. If it has a tracker, return later with an untracked model.

Optional Objectives, Missables & Decisions

Missables & Cutoffs: Arriving in an untracked everyday vehicle saves time because Moses registers it immediately for free during the mission.
Important Decisions: Choosing which untracked car to drive into Watkins Auto Parts determines your first registered Personal Vehicle—pick a durable 4-door sedan or pickup with generous trunk space for early Port Gellhorn robberies.

Rewards, Unlocks & Map References

Permanently unlocks the Slim Jim stealth-entry tool, Scooter Bros phone delivery app, vehicle registration/fencing at Watkins Auto Parts, and follow-up vehicle theft jobs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why won't Moses accept the car I drove to Watkins Auto Parts?

You must arrive in a modest, everyday street vehicle—not a flashy sports car, supercar, or a car stolen right outside the garage lot.

How do I check if a car has a tracker in GTA 6?

Open your smartphone (D-Pad Up), launch the WAiNK app, and press R1 (PS5) or RB (Xbox) to scan a vehicle's tracker tier.

What does the Scooter Bros app do?

Unlocked during Moses, the Scooter Bros phone app lets you summon your registered Personal Vehicle to your location.
