Gear Up Mission Walkthrough (Chapter 1 — Early Weapon Prep)

Gear Up is an early Chapter 1 preparation mission (reported as Mission #3 in Just Like Paradise) that introduces Jason to Leonida’s gun-store catalog and long-arm weapon loadout system before the heavier Keys ambushes begin.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Follow the Waypoint to the Local Gun Store: After your opening Chapter 1 tasks, follow the radar waypoint to the designated gun shop serving the Leonida Keys.
  2. Browse the Long-Arm Catalog: Walk up to the counter and inspect the available long-arm firearms. Unlike sidearms that stay concealed on your person, long-arm rifles and shotguns are your primary answer to open-water and dockside ambushes.
  3. Purchase Your First Long-Arm & Ammo: Acquire the required long-arm weapon and stock up on spare ammunition (plus body armor if you have spare cash) before exiting the store.
  4. Stow Extra Hardware in Your Trunk: Get in the habit early of checking your vehicle trunk storage—keeping a balanced sidearm on your hip and a rifle or shotgun in your trunk saves you from getting caught under-gunned in upcoming missions like Swamped and Tourist Trap.

Choices, Missables & Verification Notes

Budget Tip — Don’t Overspend Before BuckMe: You will soon need $5,000 in BuckMe Family Savings in Chapter 1. Buy the required long-arm and essential ammo during Gear Up, but save heavy cosmetic attachments until after you unlock Donnie’s fence or review our Weapons Database.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does Gear Up happen in GTA 6?

Gear Up occurs very early in Chapter 1: Just Like Paradise (reported as Mission #3) to introduce gun-store purchasing and long-arm weaponry.

Are there any missable weapons during Gear Up?

No missable unique weapons are reported during Gear Up. Standard catalog firearms remain purchasable across Leonida’s 24 Ammu-Nation locations.

Where are extra long-arm weapons stored in GTA 6?

Community-reported loadout mechanics place spare long-arm weapons in your personal vehicle’s trunk, so always check your trunk before leaving your car for a shootout.
