Another Life Mission Walkthrough (Ch. 4): Lucia & Tomás Memory Sequence

Another Life launches automatically in Chapter 4 right after Ocean Beach when perspective shifts to Lucia Caminos. Containing zero combat or wanted heat, this quiet flashback reveals Lucia's domestic life before her arrest with her young son, Tomás.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Pick Up Your Phone: Walk to the table inside Lucia's old home and answer the ringing phone.
  2. Check on Tomás: Head down the hallway to the back bedroom and approach the crib.
  3. Pick Up & Comfort Tomás: Interact with Tomás in the crib, carry him out to the living room, and use the highlighted household prompts to calm him down.
  4. Turn Up the Radio: Walk over to the kitchen/living counter and interact with the radio when prompted.
  5. Dance with Tomás: Press the on-screen prompt (Triangle on PS5 / Y on Xbox) to dance around the living room with Tomás and conclude the flashback.

What Happens Right After Another Life

Straight to Pool Day: As soon as the memory ends, control switches back to Jason at the Y Hotel suite (Room 701) for Pool Day, where you meet Eddie Acosta and unlock his optional party side task.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there any combat in Another Life in GTA 6?

No. Another Life is a peaceful narrative flashback with zero combat, wanted levels, or fail states.

How do you start Another Life?

It triggers automatically right after completing Ocean Beach at the start of Chapter 4 when control switches to Lucia.
