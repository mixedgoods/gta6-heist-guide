Meet the Heders Mission Walkthrough (Ch. 3): Effluvia Dinner & Port SCOOP Supercar Extraction

Meet the Heders is a pivotal Chapter 3 dual-protagonist mission for Jason and Lucia that reconnects them with Brian Heder and his wife Lori at the upscale Vice City rooftop restaurant Effluvia—setting up their introduction to master heist planner Raul Bautista before shifting to Lucia for a cartel port-yard supercar heist.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Wait for Brian's Call & Dress Up: After Cal recommends Brian to crack the encrypted hard drive, wait for Brian's phone call and head back to your apartment wardrobe to change into dinner attire.
  2. Drive to Effluvia Rooftop Restaurant: Drive Jason and Lucia to Effluvia on the east side of Vice City to meet Brian and Lori Heder. During dinner, Brian lays the groundwork for your introduction to Raul.
  3. Switch to Lucia & Infiltrate the Port: Control shifts to Lucia at the dockyard/port facility to recover a cartel-owned Pegassi supercar.
  4. Locate & Crack the Correct SCOOP Container: Navigate the shipping-yard stacks, stay clear of cartel patrols, and identify the marked SCOOP container holding the Pegassi. Break the lock to open the bay.
  5. Break Out, Lose Cartel & Police Heat: Drive the Pegassi straight out of the container, weave past cartel pursuit vehicles, shake any VCPD heat, survive the follow-up ambush, and deliver the supercar to the designated lockup to regroup with Jason.

Port Yard & Supercar Escape Tips

Avoid Container Bottlenecks: Before cracking the SCOOP container doors, clear nearby sightlines and note the cleanest exit lane out of the port gates—the Pegassi accelerates fast, so tight dead-end container alleys are the biggest hazard during the initial breakout.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you trigger Meet the Heders in GTA 6?

After Cal points you toward Brian Heder to crack an encrypted hard drive, wait for Brian's call and return to your apartment to change clothes.

What car do you steal in Meet the Heders?

Lucia infiltrates the port yard, locates the marked SCOOP shipping container, and extracts a cartel-owned Pegassi supercar.
