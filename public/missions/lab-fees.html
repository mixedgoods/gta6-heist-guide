Lab Fees Mission Walkthrough: Moonstone Boutique (0420) & Brian's Lockup (7700)

In Lab Fees, enter keypad code on the back-room laboratory door inside Moonstone Boutique (Key Lento) to collect the acid, then drive south through the Leonida Keys to Brian's Lockup and enter to stash it and unlock ongoing fence access.

Step-by-Step Objectives & Walkthrough

  1. Travel to Moonstone Boutique in Key Lento: Head to Moonstone Boutique in Key Lento with a reliable street vehicle parked cleanly for a quick departure.
  2. Enter Keypad Code 0420 at the Back-Room Lab: Locate the secured laboratory door in the back room and input 0420 on the keypad to gain entry and pick up the acid shipment.
  3. Transport the Acid South Through the Leonida Keys: Drive south along the Leonida Keys highway toward Brian's Lockup. Avoid reckless collisions or attracting police attention while hauling the cargo across narrow causeway bridges.
  4. Unlock Brian's Lockup with Keypad Code 7700: Arrive at Brian's Lockup, enter 7700 on the keypad door, and stash the acid inside to complete the mission and open Brian's fence services.

Optional Objectives, Missables & Decisions

Missables & Cutoffs: Note that MetaForge reporting conflicts with earlier site records on whether Brian remains open past Chapter 1 (some reports state Chapter 1 only with low payouts). Sell any spare early loot before hitting Chapter 1's point of no return in Tourist Trap.
Important Decisions: No branching dialogue or ending choices occur inside Lab Fees. Focus on clean driving along the Keys causeways so you don't trigger unnecessary sheriff heat.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Moonstone Boutique keypad code in Lab Fees?

The verified keypad code for the Moonstone Boutique back-room laboratory in Key Lento is 0420.

What is Brian's Lockup keypad code in GTA 6?

The verified keypad code for Brian's Lockup in the southern Leonida Keys is 7700.

Is Brian's Lockup a good fence for high-value loot?

Community and outlet reporting notes Brian pays lower rates than later Chapter 2–3 fences like Dunk, Tyrone, or Yasmani, so use Brian mainly for early Chapter 1 turnover.
