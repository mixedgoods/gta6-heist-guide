Chapter 5: Top of the World, Ma — Complete Finale Guide (7 vs. 10 Missions Resolved)

Chapter 5: Top of the World, Ma is the final story chapter of Grand Theft Auto VI. Launch guides have famously disagreed on whether Chapter 5 has 7 missions or 10 missions—below we reconcile that discrepancy, map out the corroborated mission order under spoiler shields, and flag the exact Protective Custody point of no return so you can protect your manual save slots.

Why Do Guides Say 7 vs. 10 Missions in Chapter 5?

The Chapter 5 mission-count discrepancy between major launch outlets is a tracking-granularity difference, not missing content:

7-Mission Core Count (PC Gamer / Red Bull)

Major outlet mission lists that report 91 total story missions (22 + 11 + 26 + 25 + 7) count only the 7 primary set-piece and story missions: Bird in the Hand, Taking Liberties, VC Baby, Doggie Bag, Operation Savage Glory, Protective Custody, and the finale (The End of the Road).

10-Mission Sequential Count (Full Mission Trackers)

Granular sequential checklists count 10 entries because they separately index the three transitional legal-fee and wrap-up beats involving attorney Abe Gonzalez between Operation Savage Glory and Protective Custody: Honest Abe, Unfinished Business, and Pay Abe's Fee.

Editorial Reconciliation: Both counts describe the exact same campaign finale. On GTA6HeistGuide, we preserve the 7-mission core count for consistency with the 91-mission tracker while documenting all 10 sequential entries below so players never wonder where a mission went.

Point of No Return, Save Slots & Post-Credits Free Roam

  1. Manual Save Before Sunset (End of Chapter 4): Starting Sunset locks you out of open-world free roam through the transition into Chapter 5. Keep a dedicated manual save in our 15-Slot Save Planner.
  2. Manual Save Before Protective Custody: Right before entering Abe Gonzalez's office for Protective Custody, the game displays an explicit Proceed / Postpone warning prompt. Choosing Proceed locks you into back-to-back finale missions straight through the credits.
  3. Post-Credits Free Roam: Corroborated reports (GTABase, PowerPyx) confirm that after the credits roll, GTA 6 returns you to Leonida right before the finale state so you can finish 100% completion, collectibles, and explore alternate endings via your manual save.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many missions are in GTA 6 Chapter 5?

Chapter 5: Top of the World, Ma has 7 core story missions (Bird in the Hand, Taking Liberties, VC Baby, Doggie Bag, Operation Savage Glory, Protective Custody, and The End of the Road), or 10 sequential entries if you also count the three Abe Gonzalez transitional beats (Honest Abe, Unfinished Business, and Pay Abe's Fee).

When is the final Point of No Return in GTA 6?

The final Point of No Return is Protective Custody in Chapter 5. Before entering Abe's office, the game prompts you to Proceed or Postpone—always create a manual save slot first.

Can you free roam after beating GTA 6 Chapter 5?

Yes. Corroborated launch guides confirm that after the credits roll, the game drops you back into Leonida so you can continue free-roam exploration and 100% completion.
