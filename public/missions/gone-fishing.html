Gone Fishing Mission Walkthrough (Chapter 1 — Keys Smuggling Run)

Gone Fishing is your first full open-water gameplay mission in Chapter 1: Just Like Paradise. Working alongside Donnie in the Leonida Keys, you skim contraband bricks from a waterborne shipment before surviving a Kingfishers ambush in Pelican Valley.

Step-by-Step Objectives & Walkthrough

  1. Meet Donnie at the Keys Docks: Launch from the Leonida Keys marina with Donnie to intercept the floating contraband drop. Use the channel run to get comfortable with shallow-water steering and throttle control around mangrove sandbars.
  2. Skim the Contraband Bricks: Pull alongside the shipment markers and collect the packages as directed before the Kingfishers patrol closes in near Pelican Valley.
  3. Survive the Pelican Valley Ambush: When the drop turns hostile, immediately take hard cover behind the wooden dock pilings. Watch your radar closely—Kingfishers gunmen flank from both the dock walkway and the water side.
  4. Rescue Donnie & Exfiltrate: Clear the immediate shooters pinning Donnie down, pull him clear of the crossfire, and pilot the boat back through the Keys channels to safety.

Tactical Tips & Donnie Cutoff Warning

Chapter 1 Fence Cutoff Ahead: Gone Fishing introduces Donnie's early smuggling network, but Donnie's Trailer Fence closes permanently after Swamped. Make sure you sell any early stolen goods before triggering Swamped.
Map Connection: Explore Leonida Keys (30 verified pins) and Grassrivers / Pelican Valley to spot nearby boat launches, safehouses, and early-game cash stops.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does Gone Fishing happen in GTA 6?

Gone Fishing is the first full gameplay mission in Chapter 1: Just Like Paradise, immediately following the opening Prologue sequence.

Who ambushes you during Gone Fishing?

Members of the Kingfishers gang ambush Jason and Donnie during the contraband pickup in Pelican Valley.

Are there any missables inside Gone Fishing?

No collectibles are permanently missed during Gone Fishing itself, though Donnie's fence closes later in Chapter 1 after Swamped.
