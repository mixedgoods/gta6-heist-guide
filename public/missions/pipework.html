Pipework Mission Walkthrough (Chapter 1 — Brian’s Rent Collection & Mandy)

Pipework is a memorable Chapter 1 rent-collection job assigned by Brian Heder via phone call. Jason heads over to collect from Brian’s eccentric tenant Mandy, only to get roped into an awkward kitchen-sink plumbing repair with a dialogue choice and a driveway cigar payout.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Answer Brian’s Call & Drive to Mandy’s House: After Brian calls about overdue rent, follow the waypoint to Mandy’s blue Keys house (identifiable by the low chain-link fence and American flag out front) and speak with her while she gardens.
  2. Grab the Bathroom Plunger: Follow Mandy inside. When she brings up her clogged kitchen sink and starts flirting, cross the hall into the bathroom and pick up the plunger beside the toilet.
  3. Plunge & Wrench the Kitchen Sink: Return to the kitchen sink and follow the right-stick / action prompts to plunge the drain. When the clog refuses to clear, duck underneath the sink and hold the trigger prompt to turn the wrench while Mandy talks.
  4. Handle Mandy’s Flirting & Grab the Cigars: Whether you humor Mandy’s banter or shut her down, she loses her temper and throws Brian’s rent payment—a bundle of cigars—out onto the driveway. Walk outside, pick up the cigars off the ground, and head back to finish the job.

Choices, Missables & Verification Notes

Choice Impact & Relationship Note: Corroborated walkthroughs (Eurogamer, GamesRadar) report that both dialogue responses—entertaining Mandy or shutting her down—end with Mandy storming off and tossing the cigars outside. If you are playing a loyal Jason run for Jason & Lucia’s relationship meter, shutting her down is the cleaner roleplay option.
Evidence Note: Mission title and Chapter 1 placement are tracked in existing site data; the Mandy rent-collection premise, plunger/wrench sink steps, and cigar driveway pickup are corroborated across Eurogamer, GamesRadar, Beebom, and Sportskeeda.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the plunger in the GTA 6 Pipework mission?

Walk across the hall from Mandy’s kitchen into the bathroom; the plunger sits on the floor right next to the toilet.

Does your choice with Mandy change the Pipework reward?

No. Corroborated guides report that whether you entertain her chatter or shut her down, she throws the cigars onto the driveway for you to collect.

Who gives the Pipework mission?

Brian Heder assigns Pipework to Jason via a Chapter 1 phone call.
