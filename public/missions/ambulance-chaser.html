Ambulance Chaser Mission Walkthrough (Ch. 2): Avoid the Missable Relationship & Rent Penalty

Ambulance Chaser (also listed in some checklists as Move the Ambulance) is a missable Chapter 2 mission for Lucia Caminos in Kelly County. You must locate and destroy the stolen Chapter 1 hospital escape ambulance before starting Slam Dunk—ignoring it lets police find the vehicle, triggering both a relationship hit with Jason and higher Starlet Motel rent.

Why You Should Never Skip Ambulance Chaser

Missable Mission Alert: Because GTA 6 does not include a mission replay menu at launch, missing Ambulance Chaser permanently locks in the negative outcome on that playthrough. Complete it right after Moses and before accepting Slam Dunk.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Head to the Barn East of Starlet Motel: Travel to the wooden barn just east of the Starlet Motel where the hospital getaway ambulance from Chapter 1 was stashed. Enter via the main double doors or climb the exterior staircase to the upper floor.
  2. Flush Out the Scavengers: Inside, local junkies and looters are rummaging through the ambulance. As soon as they spot Lucia, they scatter—one group flees on an ATV while another drives off in the ambulance itself.
  3. Pursue on the Nagasaki Street Blazer: Jump onto the red Nagasaki Street Blazer quad bike left behind in the barn and stay close behind the fleeing ambulance heading south.
  4. Wait for the Crash & Optional Loot: You do not need to shoot out the tires—keep up the pressure and the AI driver will eventually lose control and slam into a tree further south. Before burning it, you can optionally hop into the back of the wrecked ambulance to grab leftover medical supplies.
  5. Torch the Ambulance: Pick up the red gas canister sitting on the ground right behind the wreck. Pour fuel over the ambulance (R1 / RB), step clear, and press the Ignite prompt (Triangle / Y)—or shoot the gas can/rear doors from a safe distance to detonate it instantly.
  6. Clear the Blast Zone: Leave the area immediately before Kelly County deputies arrive to investigate the explosion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Ambulance Chaser missable in GTA 6?

Yes. Corroborated guides (IGN, PowerPyx, GTABase) confirm that if you advance to Slam Dunk without destroying the ambulance, police discover it automatically.

What happens if you skip Ambulance Chaser?

Skipping the mission causes a relationship penalty between Jason and Lucia and raises your Starlet Motel rent.

How do you blow up the ambulance in Ambulance Chaser?

Grab the red gas canister behind the crashed ambulance, pour fuel with R1/RB, and press Triangle/Y to ignite it—or shoot the gas can from a safe distance.
