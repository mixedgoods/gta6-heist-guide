Watch It Mission Walkthrough (Ch. 4): Cruda Ambush & Royce Jones

Watch It is a solo-Jason Chapter 4 mission for Dre'Quan Priest. A Crowex watch swap on the top deck of the Cruda parking lot goes sideways when Dre'Quan's watch turns out to be fake, leading to a rooftop shootout, a free outfit pickup at Nola, and a pivotal meeting with VYB club owner Royce Jones.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Rendezvous with Dre'Quan: Answer Dre'Quan's phone call, meet him at the marked store, head upstairs while he finishes up, and return to the car.
  2. Drive to Cruda Parking Lot Top Deck: Head south to the Cruda parking lot, climb the spiral ramps to the roof, and park next to the Crowex watch dealer's vehicle.
  3. Survive the Fake-Watch Ambush: When the dealer spots Dre'Quan's counterfeit Crowex, knock out the first two men immediately. Take cover as a reinforcement car pulls up and extra gunmen rush the stairwell.
  4. Escape Before Police Arrive: Clear the hostiles, jump back into your car, and exit the parking garage immediately to stay ahead of incoming VCPD heat.
  5. Pick a Free Outfit at Nola: Drive to Nola near the south end of Ocean Beach and select one of the two mannequin outfits—Dre'Quan buys your pick and takes the other.
  6. Meet Royce Jones at Doight d'Or: Drive north to the Doight d'Or hotel and follow Dre'Quan to the back patio. Royce explains that Club VYB cannot reopen until blackmail involving rap star Dante Fresh is resolved—directly unlocking Fresh Evidence.

Watch the Stairwell Flank on Cruda Roof

Don't Tunnel-Vision the Reinforcement Car: Half the enemy wave rushes up the rooftop stairs behind your initial cover. Shift sideways behind a concrete pillar so you can watch both the ramp and the stairwell.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have to pay for the outfit at Nola in Watch It?

No. Choose either of the two displayed mannequin outfits at Nola in Ocean Beach and Dre'Quan covers the bill while taking the other.

What mission unlocks after Watch It?

Meeting Royce Jones at the Doight d'Or hotel sets up the Dante Fresh mansion infiltration in Fresh Evidence.
