Breakfast Run Mission Walkthrough (Chapter 2 — Hank’s Waffles Robbery Spree)

Breakfast Run is the classic Bonnie-and-Clyde style Chapter 2 robbery spree featuring Hank’s Waffles. Triggered after Jason finishes recovering following Slam Dunk, Jason and Lucia chain together rapid diner, liquor-store, and thrift-store stickups before outrunning Port Gellhorn police.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Rest Up & Answer Jason’s Call: After finishing Slam Dunk, return to the Starlet Motel and wait for Jason to recover to full health and call you to start the spree.
  2. Drive to Hank’s Waffles: Pull the getaway car up outside Hank’s Waffles with an easy exit angle back onto the main road.
  3. Hold Up Hank’s Waffles (1-Minute Timer): Walk inside as Lucia, draw your weapon, and keep aiming at the staff to keep intimidation high while they empty the registers. Scoop every cash stack off the counter before the generous 1-minute timer runs down.
  4. Chain the Liquor Store & Thrift Store Stops: Follow the automatic mission prompts to hit the local liquor store and secondary thrift/mart stops using the same fast hold-up loop—do not linger once the clerk drops the cash.
  5. Escape the Police Pursuit: Jump back into the car with Jason and break police line of sight across Port Gellhorn’s side streets and industrial alleys to complete the mission.

Choices, Missables & Verification Notes

Store Intimidation & Getaway Tip: Keep your weapon aimed steadily at clerks without firing unnecessary shots—shooting civilians escalates your wanted stars faster. For general free-roam stickups after this mission, read our Shops & Convenience Store Stickups Guide.
Evidence Note: Breakfast Run’s Chapter 2 placement ahead of Coffee Break is tracked in existing site data; the post-Slam Dunk trigger, Hank’s Waffles 1-minute register timer, multi-store chain, and trust/vehicle outcome are corroborated across PowerPyx and Eurogamer.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you unlock Breakfast Run in GTA 6?

Complete Slam Dunk in Chapter 2 and wait for Jason to recover and call you at the Starlet Motel.

Is Hank’s Waffles the diner from the GTA 6 trailer?

Yes. Breakfast Run features the Hank’s Waffles diner stickup alongside follow-up liquor and thrift store robberies in Port Gellhorn.

What mission unlocks right after Breakfast Run?

Breakfast Run leads directly into Coffee Break at the Starlet Motel kitchen, which unlocks free character switching and Bret Vogel’s hunting decks.
