Clear Skies Mission Walkthrough (Ch. 1): Pilot Pete Escort & Biker Ambush

Clear Skies pairs Jason Duval with Donnie on a high-risk aerial smuggling escort for Brian Heder. You must mask Pilot Pete's radar signature by flying directly above his aircraft, then rescue Donnie when the Final Chapter biker gang ambushes a Devil's Dozen deal.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Meet Brian at the Southwind Tiki Bar: Travel to Southwind to get the briefing from Brian and link up with Donnie.
  2. Water Takeoff to Intercept Pilot Pete: Taxi the seaplane off the roadside area into open water for a clean takeoff, then climb toward the southeast intercept zone to spot Pilot Pete's inbound aircraft.
  3. Mask Pilot Pete's Radar Signature: Match Pete's airspeed and hold position directly above his fuselage to cloak his radar ping all the way down to the swamp airstrip.
  4. Land at the Northwest Airstrip & Follow Donnie: Touch down at the second airstrip further northwest and walk with Donnie to meet his Devil's Dozen contact.
  5. Survive the Final Chapter Ambush & Rescue Donnie: When Final Chapter bikers crash the deal and grab Donnie, snap into cover immediately. Trigger Focus Mode (L3 + R3) to line up clean headshots, clear the bikers, and escort Donnie out safely to collect your $1,400 payout.

Radar Escort & Biker Ambush Tips

Use Focus Mode Immediately on Ambush: The Final Chapter bikers swarm quickly around Donnie. Pop L3 + R3 as soon as gunfire starts to drop the closest shooters before Donnie takes heavy damage. Also note that Pilot Pete later unlocks repeatable Airdrop Delivery side jobs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much money do you earn for Clear Skies in GTA 6?

Completing Clear Skies awards $1,400 upon rescuing Donnie and escaping the Final Chapter biker ambush.

How do you mask Pilot Pete's radar in Clear Skies?

Fly your seaplane directly above Pilot Pete's aircraft and match his airspeed until he safely reaches the swamp airstrip.
