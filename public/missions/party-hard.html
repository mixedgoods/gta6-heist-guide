Party Hard Mission Walkthrough (Ch. 4): Megamundo Network HQ Rescue

Party Hard (objective title: Work Personal Security) is an early Chapter 4 mission given by Valentina. To pay for legal representation, Jason and Lucia run VIP security for telenovela star Andrés de León at the Megamundo Network HQ opening party—until revolutionary group FAP turns the tower into a hostage siege.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Accept Valentina's Security Contract: After setting up legal help in Lawyer Up, report to Valentina for personal security duty protecting telenovela star Andrés de León.
  2. Work the Megamundo Opening Party: Escort Andrés inside Megamundo Network HQ, mingle on the event floor, and check on VIP guests as directed.
  3. Respond to the FAP Hostage Attack: When armed members of the revolutionary group FAP storm the broadcast party, take cover immediately and avoid exposing hostages to crossfire.
  4. Locate the Security Radio: Navigate through the locked-down interior floors to recover a working radio and coordinate between Jason and Lucia.
  5. Clear Gunmen & Rescue César Navarro: Push systematically from room to room using cover-to-cover peeks, neutralize the remaining FAP militants, and secure Megamundo CEO César Navarro along with Andrés to finish the mission.

Watch Your Crossfire Around Party Guests

Hostage Safety Inside Megamundo HQ: FAP gunmen take cover directly behind event partitions near civilians. Use precision semi-auto bursts or headshots rather than explosives so you don't fail the rescue objective.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who do you protect in Party Hard in GTA 6?

You start on personal security for telenovela star Andrés de León and end up rescuing Megamundo CEO César Navarro and party guests from FAP gunmen.

What mission leads into Party Hard?

Party Hard follows Lawyer Up in Chapter 4 as part of Jason and Lucia's work for Valentina to secure legal representation.
