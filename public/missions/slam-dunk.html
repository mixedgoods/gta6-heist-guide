Slam Dunk Mission Walkthrough: Dr. Bill Stakeout, Kolts Docks & Wild Hog Point

Slam Dunk starts at Dunk's Trailer on Tanglewood Lane in Port Gellhorn (played as Lucia). Stake out Dr. Bill Kolts at the grocery store, drive slowly through the Kolts Gang dock compound, recover Dr. Bill after he jumps from the car, and survive the Wild Hog Point ambush to unlock Dunk's Trailer as a permanent stolen-goods fence plus a next-day $1,700 BuckMe payout.

Step-by-Step Objectives & Walkthrough

  1. Meet Dunk on Tanglewood Lane & Walk to the Grocery Store: Speak with Dunk outside his trailer and follow him on foot through the neighborhood (hopping fences) to a local grocery store visited by retired doctor Dr. Bill Kolts.
  2. Stake Out From Behind the Dumpster: Take cover beside Dunk behind the dumpster across the street until the clock skips to roughly 6:00 PM and Dr. Bill arrives.
  3. Confront Dr. Bill & Drive to the Kolts Dock Compound: Enter the store behind Dunk, trail Dr. Bill to his Willard Transient (where Dunk loads Dr. Bill and his dog, Peaches, inside), and drive to the Kolts Gang dock compound.
  4. Drive Slowly Inside the Security Gate: Once waved through the gate, creep slowly behind the walking guard toward the office—speeding or swerving blows your cover early.
  5. Reverse Out & Shake the Kolts Pursuit: When the Kolts spot the setup and open fire, reverse out of the dockyard, clear the minimap search zone, and lose the pursuing gang cars.
  6. Load Dr. Bill Into the Trunk & Head to Wild Hog Point: After Dr. Bill calls Declan and jumps from the moving car—knocking himself out—load him into the trunk and drive southwest of Port Gellhorn behind Wild Hog Airfield.
  7. Survive the Wild Hog Point Ambush Using Focus Mode: When the trade springs a trap and the Kolts shoot Dr. Bill, take hard cover behind your vehicle, activate Focus Mode to tag flanking and rooftop shooters, and clear the squad (grab dropped SMGs if needed).
  8. Loot Dr. Bill & Escape Before Cops Arrive: Loot Dr. Bill's body (~$1,000–$1,145 reported), locate Dunk, and leave the airfield perimeter before law enforcement locks down the road.

Optional Objectives, Missables & Decisions

Missables & Cutoffs: Don't leave Wild Hog Point without looting Dr. Bill's body first—it's an easy ~$1,000+ cash pickup toward your Chapter 2 $15,000 goal.
Important Decisions: No branching endings occur here, but maintaining stealth speed behind the dock guard avoids taking unnecessary gunfire before the getaway.

Rewards, Unlocks & Map References

Permanently unlocks Dunk's Trailer (Tanglewood Lane, Port Gellhorn) as a stolen-goods fence, yields ~$1,000–$1,145 on-site loot, and pays a follow-up $1,700 BuckMe transfer from Dunk the next day.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock Dunk's fence in GTA 6?

Complete the Chapter 2 mission Slam Dunk starting at Dunk's Trailer on Tanglewood Lane in Port Gellhorn. Dunk permanently unlocks as a stolen-goods fence afterward.

How much money do you make from Slam Dunk?

Corroborated reports place Dr. Bill's loot at roughly $1,000–$1,145 plus a $1,700 BuckMe payout from Dunk the following day.

Why did I blow my cover at the Kolts dock compound?

You must drive slowly behind the walking guard once waved through the security gate; accelerating or driving erratically triggers the shootout early.
