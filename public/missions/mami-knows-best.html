Mami Knows Best Mission Walkthrough (Ch. 3): Mia Caminos & The Excavator Rampage

Mami Knows Best (listed as Mami in some early mission trackers) is a standout Chapter 3 character mission for Lucia Caminos. After meeting her mother Mia Caminos for coffee in Peacock Bay, Lucia helps Mia's new real-estate venture by clearing out a property—and hopping into a heavy excavator when local gang members try to intimidate them.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Meet Mia at Los Bollos: Head to Los Bollos in Peacock Bay to catch up with Lucia's mother over coffee and hear about her new real-estate hustle.
  2. Drive to the Listing Property: Follow Mia over to the neighborhood house she is trying to flip.
  3. Clear the Front Lawn: Walk around the yard and haul away the junk and clutter blocking the property.
  4. Wreck the Gang's Turf with the Excavator: When local gang members show up to cause trouble, climb into the on-site excavator and use the boom and bucket to smash up the rival crew's property until they back down.
  5. Return to Mia's Office: Drive back to Mia's real-estate office to complete the mission.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 'Mami' the same mission as 'Mami Knows Best'?

Yes. Early chapter lists abbreviated the title to 'Mami', while full launch walkthroughs (IGN, PowerPyx, GTABase) confirm the in-game title is Mami Knows Best.

Where does Mami Knows Best start?

Meet Lucia's mother Mia Caminos at Los Bollos in Peacock Bay, Vice City during Chapter 3.
