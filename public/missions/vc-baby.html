VC Baby Mission Walkthrough (Ch. 5): Mattia's Choice & Penthouse Escape

VC Baby (Mission #111, also listed as Talk to Mattia) breaks out inside Giorgio Gallo's penthouse at Tequesta Retreat when Kat suddenly goes into labor during a security lockdown. You face a direct branching choice to help Mattia—complete with a credits consequence if you refuse.

Help Mattia vs. Refuse (Credits Consequence)

Branching Outcome: Accepting Mattia's plea launches the penthouse stealth escape and hospital run below. If you refuse to help, Mattia desperately jumps off the balcony, breaks his leg, and alters one of the game's end-credit scenes. Drop a manual save if you want to see both outcomes!

Step-by-Step Walkthrough (Helping Mattia)

  1. Follow Mattia & Agree to Help: Speak with Mattia at Giorgio's penthouse as Kat goes into labor and choose to help sneak him past Giorgio's lockdown.
  2. Wait for Lucia's Pool Distraction: Hold tight until Lucia distracts guard Dardan by tossing his phone straight into the penthouse pool.
  3. Steal Dardan's Key Fob: The moment Dardan rushes toward the pool, swipe the key fob right off the couch where he was sitting.
  4. Return to the Car Elevator: Slip back to Mattia by the car elevator without alerting penthouse security.
  5. Drive Mattia to the Hospital: Exit the building, weave cleanly through Vice City traffic while shaking any pursuit, and deliver Mattia safely to the hospital entrance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if you refuse to help Mattia in VC Baby?

Refusing causes Mattia to jump off the penthouse balcony and break his leg, which changes a scene during the game's end credits.

Where is Dardan's key fob in VC Baby?

Wait for Lucia to throw Dardan's phone into the pool, then grab the key fob directly from the couch where Dardan was sitting.
