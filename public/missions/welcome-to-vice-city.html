Welcome to Vice City Mission Walkthrough (Chapter 3 Opener — Boobie Ike & Bayside Apt)

Welcome to Vice City is the opening mission of Chapter 3: Tits, Ass, Drug Money. Immediately after escaping Port Gellhorn in Checking Out, Jason and Lucia cruise into Vice City, meet Dre’Quan Priest and Boobie Ike at The Jack of Hearts in Crosstown, and unlock their new Vice City safehouse at Bayside Apartments (Room 201).

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Drive Into Vice City & Call Dre’Quan: Seamlessly starting in a stolen car on the highway, follow the route into Crosstown, Vice City while Jason talks with Dre’Quan Priest over the phone.
  2. Enter the Back Door of The Jack of Hearts: Pull up to The Jack of Hearts strip club and walk around to the rear entrance to meet Dre’Quan and his boss, Vice City mogul Boobie Ike.
  3. Make the Play Money Choice: Dre’Quan offers Jason and Lucia fake bills to blend in on the club floor. Choosing Take Money lets you walk the ground floor tossing bills (R1 / RB) before texting/meeting Dre’Quan by the stairs; choosing Decline Money skips the minigame and sends Jason straight upstairs with Dre’Quan while Lucia handles the floor off-screen.
  4. Claim Bayside Apartments (Room 201): After Boobie Ike sets you up with a Vice City apartment, drive over to Bayside Apartments, park outside, and enter Room 201 to establish your new Chapter 3 safehouse and reopen Vice City free roam.

Choices, Missables & Verification Notes

Chapter 3 Unlocks Right After This Mission: Settling into Bayside Apartments opens the richest sandbox phase of GTA 6. Right after Welcome to Vice City, prioritize unlocking Vice City’s three Chapter 3 fence contacts (Tyrone in La Perle, Yasmani for jewelry, and Randy in Balsero for wish-list vehicles) and create Save Slot #06 in the Save Planner.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should you take or decline the play money in Welcome to Vice City?

It is a pacing choice with no confirmed negative penalty. Taking the money lets you play the brief club-floor minigame (R1/RB); declining skips straight to the upstairs meeting with Dre’Quan.

What safehouse unlocks in Welcome to Vice City?

Boobie Ike grants Jason and Lucia use of Bayside Apartments, Room 201, serving as your new Chapter 3 base in Vice City.

Who do you meet at The Jack of Hearts?

You meet Dre’Quan Priest and his boss, Boobie Ike, who anchor the early Chapter 3 Vice City storyline.
