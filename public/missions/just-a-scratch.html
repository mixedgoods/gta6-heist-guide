Just a Scratch Mission Walkthrough (Chapter 1 — Free Repair & Respray)

Just a Scratch (also referenced as Fix Your Car) is an early Chapter 1 tutorial mission where Jason brings his beat-up Vapid ’70 Ganado to Joey’s shop (Coastal Auto Care) in Key Lento. It unlocks your basic vehicle repair and cosmetic respray flow—and gives a subtle early relationship boost for Lucia.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Set Waypoint to Coastal Auto Care: After finishing Gone Fishing and early Chapter 1 prep, open your map and mark the Fix Your Car / Just a Scratch icon at Joey’s garage (Coastal Auto Care) in Key Lento.
  2. Drive Jason’s Vapid ’70 Ganado to the Shop: Pull up in front of the garage bay and hold D-Pad Up (as reported in Default controls) to greet Joey.
  3. Pull Inside & Select Full Engine Repair: Slowly roll the truck into the service bay and choose Full Engine Repair, which Joey comps for free during this mission.
  4. Apply a Fresh Respray: Open Cosmetics > Respray and pick a clean paint job for the truck. Taking care of the ride triggers a positive relationship note (“Lucia will appreciate this”) ahead of her release in Coming Home.

Choices, Missables & Verification Notes

Relationship & Early Customization Tip: Fixing and repainting Jason’s vehicle pairs directly with the other pre-release preparations in Jason’s Key Lento Safehouse Repairs & Errands. If you want the highest possible early trust meter before Chapter 2, complete both Just a Scratch and the optional errands prior to Coming Home.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have to pay for the repair in Just a Scratch?

No. Corroborated walkthroughs report that Joey provides the Full Engine Repair free of charge during this Chapter 1 mission.

Does Just a Scratch affect Jason and Lucia’s relationship?

Yes—launch guides note that cleaning up and respraying Jason’s truck contributes a positive relationship boost when Lucia returns.

Where is Coastal Auto Care located?

Coastal Auto Care (Joey’s mod shop) is located in Key Lento in the Leonida Keys.
