Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Point of No Return in Chapter 1?

Tourist Trap is the Chapter 1 finale gauntlet that locks you into the transition to Port Gellhorn (Chapter 2). Create a manual backup on Save Slot #03 before starting it.

Which fence closes permanently in Chapter 1?

Donnie's Trailer Fence in the Leonida Keys closes permanently after completing Swamped.

What are the keypad codes in Chapter 1?

0420 opens the Moonstone Boutique lab and 7700 opens Brian's Lockup during Lab Fees.
