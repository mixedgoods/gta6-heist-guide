Jason & Lucia Abilities, Focus Mode & Character Differences in GTA 6

How Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos actually play in Grand Theft Auto VI Story Mode: the shared Focus ability (L3+R3 / LS+RS), combat vs. environmental scanning, character switching, partner commands, exclusive missions, and what carries across both characters.

What Are Jason and Lucia's Special Abilities? (Focus Mode)

Unlike GTA V—where Michael, Franklin, and Trevor each had a distinct superpower—both Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos share the same core special ability called Focus, alongside core Health, Stamina, and Focus meters. Activate Focus by pressing L3 + R3 simultaneously on PlayStation 5 (or LS + RS on Xbox Series X|S).

Focus in Combat (While Aiming)

Activating Focus while aiming down sights slows down time (similar to Red Dead Redemption 2's Dead Eye) and color-codes enemy weak points:

  • Red highlights: Lethal critical zones for rapid takedowns.
  • Yellow highlights: Non-lethal / incapacitating points (hands, legs, weapons)—crucial for shooting guns out of guards' hands in The Cartel Heist to keep the Professional rating.

Focus Outside Combat (Scanner & Hunting)

When you are not aiming a weapon, Focus works like an environmental scanner (similar to RDR2's Eagle Eye):

  • Loot & Clues: Highlights interactive objects, hidden valuables, and robbery clues.
  • Hunting Vitals: Reveals animal tracks and vital zones so you can land clean, single-shot kills for Pristine pelts (Animals & Wildlife Guide).
  • Pursuits: Helps spot police positions and tracks during high-heat escapes.

Jason Duval vs. Lucia Caminos: Key Gameplay Differences

Because both protagonists share the universal Focus mechanic, the main differences between playing as Jason or Lucia come from their narrative backgrounds, exclusive side missions, and story-locked chapters rather than separate RPG skill trees:

Special Ability Focus (L3+R3 / LS+RS) Focus (L3+R3 / LS+RS)
Background & Flavor Former military / drug runner; firearm-oriented narrative framing. Ex-con / street fighter; close-quarters & high-stakes heist framing.

Relationship & Profile Your joint choices shape the Relationship meter (up to Hot Together 4/4) and Criminal Profile, altering safehouse interactions and dialogue. See the Relationship Guide.

Character Switching & Partner Commands

Free-Roam & Mid-Mission Switching

Outside locked story segments, you can switch seamlessly between Jason and Lucia while the other maintains an independent routine in Leonida. During joint missions and heists, GTA 6 frequently allows on-the-fly swaps—such as driving a getaway car as one character and switching to the passenger seat to suppress pursuers as the other. Certain narrative missions temporarily lock you to one protagonist.

Partner Menu (Hold Right on D-Pad)

When Jason and Lucia are operating together, hold Right on the D-Pad to open the Partner Menu and issue tactical follow, wait, or positioning commands. For the full PS5 and Xbox button layout, see our GTA 6 Controls Guide.

What Is Shared vs. Separate Between Jason & Lucia?

  • Unique Weapon Variants (Shared): Once either protagonist picks up a Unique Weapon Variant (such as Assassin Nipper .38, Crabshack VF5, or missable Donnie's H&L .38), it unlocks permanently for both Jason and Lucia via vehicle trunks and Ammu-Nation lockers (Weapons & Unique Variants Guide).
  • Buck Me Family Savings vs. Liquid Cash (Watch Ch. 1 Lockout): Buck Me Family Savings carries over across Chapter 1, whereas unbanked liquid cash is lost at the end of Chapter 1 (Just Like Paradise)—spend liquid cash on weapons or outfits before finishing Chapter 1. In free-roam, pocket cash caps at $20,000.
  • Active Carry Loadout (Managed Separately): Each character has a restricted active weapon wheel, with excess firearms and spare body armor stored in personal vehicle trunks and safehouse lockers.

Jason & Lucia Abilities FAQ

What are Jason and Lucia's special abilities in GTA 6?

Both Jason and Lucia share the universal Focus ability (L3+R3 on PS5 / LS+RS on Xbox). While aiming, Focus slows time and highlights lethal (red) and non-lethal (yellow) weak points; outside combat, it highlights clues, valuables, and animal vitals.

How do you activate Focus mode and partner commands in GTA 6?

Click both analog sticks together (L3 + R3 / LS + RS) to activate Focus. Hold Right on the D-Pad when traveling together to open the Partner Menu.

What is the gameplay difference between playing as Jason vs. Lucia?

They share Focus, Unique Weapon unlocks, and core stats, while differing in character-exclusive Strangers & Freaks (like Jason's Veteran Hunted hunting unlock), story-locked missions, and dialogue/safehouse dynamics driven by your Relationship level.

Do Jason and Lucia share Unique Weapon Variants and money?

Yes for Unique Weapon Variants and Buck Me Family Savings. Active weapon wheels and pocket cash ($20,000 free-roam cap; Chapter 1 liquid cash resets) must be managed per character.
