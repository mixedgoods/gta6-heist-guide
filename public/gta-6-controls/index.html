GTA 6 Controls Guide: Every PS5 and Xbox Button Explained

A searchable controller reference with a PS5 / Xbox switch. Bindings are labelled by how well they are sourced, and anything that could not be backed up is listed separately instead of guessed.

Evidence status. Last verified November 23, 2026 · launch build 01.002.001 · PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. This site has not run in-game tests for this page; nothing here is labelled "in-game verified". Each fact carries a label and the full ledger is kept in the project's source register. Bindings come from published guides, not from this site's own in-game controls screen.
Always confirm on your own screen. In-game, open Pause Menu › Settings › Control Scheme (the path reported by guides) to see the exact bindings for the preset you are using. Presets change bindings, so a guide for one preset can be wrong for another. This page describes the Default scheme only.
Platforms. Rockstar currently lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S as the platforms for Grand Theft Auto VI. This site works in any browser, but no PC game control layout exists to document.

Frequently asked questions

How do you sprint in GTA 6?

Guides report that sprinting is done by clicking the left stick (L3 on PS5, LS on Xbox) in the Default scheme, rather than tapping a button repeatedly. Confirm in Settings › Control Scheme.

How do you open the weapon wheel in GTA 6?

Guides report holding L1 on PS5 or LB on Xbox. This is labelled reported, not in-game verified by this site.

How do you use the phone in GTA 6?

Guides report D-pad Up opens the phone. This site's cheat guide uses the same entry point before holding L1/LB and tapping R1/RB for the cheat terminal.

How do you switch between Jason and Lucia?

We are not publishing a binding: two sources disagreed and nothing corroborated them. Check your own Control Scheme screen.

Are there hidden controls in GTA 6?

None are verified and we do not publish unverified combinations.

Does GTA 6 have a PC control layout?

No. Rockstar currently lists only PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S as platforms.
