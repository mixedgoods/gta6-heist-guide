Frequently asked questions

Can you buy houses in GTA 6?

No. This site's data lists 4 story safehouses unlocked through the story and 0 purchasable houses.

Can you buy businesses in GTA 6?

No passive-income businesses are reported to be buyable in Story Mode. This is a report, not a Rockstar statement.

Can you buy garages in GTA 6?

Yes. The database lists 8 purchasable garages, 4 story garages and 1 Ultimate Edition bonus garage. Prices are not confirmed.

Can you buy boats in GTA 6?

We found no evidence either way, so it is unconfirmed.

What are the seven fence contacts in GTA 6?

Five stolen-goods fences (Brian, Donnie, Dunk, Tyrone, Yasmani) and two vehicle buyers (Moses and Randy).

How do I buy a garage in GTA 6?

Guides report that a real-estate phone app unlocks early in the story and lets you buy garages. Exact steps and prices are not confirmed.
