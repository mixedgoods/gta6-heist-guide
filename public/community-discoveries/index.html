Evidence & Verification Standards

A screenshot alone never automatically proves every claim in a submission. Every report follows a strict editorial review path:

  • Awaiting Review — newly submitted; private in the moderation queue and never shown publicly.
  • Needs More Evidence — held in review until clearer instructions, coordinates, or corroborating proof are provided.
  • Community Reported — approved for publication as a player report; not yet reproduced by GTA6HeistGuide.
  • Verified in Gameplay — independently reproduced and verified in gameplay by an authorized reviewer.
  • Officially Confirmed — backed by an authoritative official Rockstar Games or platform source.
  • Rejected — inaccurate, duplicate, unverified spam, or outside editorial standards (never published).
