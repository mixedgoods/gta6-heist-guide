GTA 6 Vehicle Guides: Fastest Cars, Getaway Picks, Storage and Customization

A search-focused guide built on the existing 33-vehicle database. It separates what is reported from what is tested, and it will not name a "fastest" car until that has been measured.

Evidence status. Last verified November 23, 2026 · launch build 01.002.001 · PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. This site has not run in-game tests for this page; nothing here is labelled "in-game verified". Each fact carries a label and the full ledger is kept in the project's source register. Every one of the 33 vehicles in the database still lists top speed, acceleration and handling as "Not yet confirmed". This page therefore publishes no speed numbers and no "fastest" ranking.
Why there is no top-10 list. A fair ranking needs repeatable in-game runs: the same route, game build, upgrade state and several repetitions. None have been run by this site, so ranking cars would be guessing. When testing happens, the method, build number and results will be recorded here before any ranking is shown.

How to store and keep vehicles in GTA 6

COMMUNITY REPORTED REPORTS CONFLICT Reports disagree on the details, so treat this as a summary of what is reported, not a how-to.

  • Reports say early-game cars can be parked at safehouse spaces but may be lost if heavily damaged, abandoned during a mission or after you are busted or wasted.
  • One set of reports says stolen vehicles can be registered (for example via a Pay 'n' Spray or a contact unlocked after the Chapter 2 mission "Moses") to become personal vehicles that can be recovered and summoned with the ScooterBros phone app; another set talks only about garages. We could not confirm either in-game.
  • Purchasable garages: the properties data lists 8 purchasable garages (prices not confirmed) plus 4 story garages and the Ultimate Edition Paradise Garage. See the property ownership guide and the Properties database.
  • Selling cars: Moses and Randy are the two vehicle buyers. Moses is reported to accept only untracked vehicles; Randy is reported to buy from a wish list of high-end vehicles. See the money guide.

Frequently asked questions

What is the fastest car in GTA 6?

This site has not measured top speeds, so it does not name one. Every vehicle in the database lists top speed as "Not yet confirmed". Rankings will be added only after repeatable in-game tests with a recorded build number.

Where can I find supercars in GTA 6?

The database reports Vice City and Vice Beach luxury districts for the Super class. These are regions, not exact coordinates, and are not in-game verified here.

How do I store stolen cars in GTA 6?

Reports disagree: some describe registering stolen vehicles to make them recoverable personal vehicles, others describe parking at safehouses or garages. We could not verify either, so check in-game and see the property guide for garages.

Can you customize vehicles in GTA 6?

Guides report customization shops such as Rideout Customs and One-Eyed Willie's. Prices and performance effects are not confirmed here.
