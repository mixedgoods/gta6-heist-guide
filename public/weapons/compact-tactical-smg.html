Recommended Tactical Pairing

With its side-folding brace, extended vertical stick magazine, and optional suppressor mount, the Compact Tactical SMG bridges the gap between concealable pistols and bulky long guns. It shines inside narrow bank lobbies, pawn-shop backrooms, and vehicle drive-bys when breaking VCPD pursuit.
