Black Eagle Import Kalinga .50 — Unique Pistol Location & Guide

The Black Eagle Import Kalinga .50 is a high-caliber unique variant of the Fairview Kalinga .50 Pistol featuring fixed custom attachments and a yellow-text loadout tag. Find it locked inside the Black Eagle warehouse at the Black Eagle store in Balsero by reading the keypad code off a nearby computer.

How to Unlock the Black Eagle Import Kalinga .50

  1. Head to Balsero’s Black Eagle Store: Drive to the industrial district of Balsero (also home to Randy’s vehicle wish-list drop and a Top Fun van) and locate the Black Eagle warehouse.
  2. Check the Nearby Computer for the Keypad Code: Before forcing your way in, inspect the terminal/computer near the warehouse office to read the keypad combination.
  3. Enter the Code & Claim the Pistol: Input the code on the warehouse keypad, step inside, and pick up the Black Eagle Import Kalinga .50.
  4. Manage Loadout via Trunk & Ammu-Nation: Store it in your vehicle trunk for heavy-armor firefights or re-equip it anytime from any Ammu-Nation locker.

Distinction From Gil’s Kalinga .50

Two Unique Kalinga Variants: Do not confuse the Balsero Black Eagle Import Kalinga .50 with Gil’s Kalinga .50 Pistol earned during Chapter 3’s Safe Word mission. Collecting both expands your unique sidearm collection toward 50 Weapons.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the Black Eagle Import Kalinga .50 in GTA 6?

Inside the Black Eagle warehouse at the Black Eagle store in Balsero; you unlock it using a keypad code displayed on a nearby computer.

Can you swap attachments on unique weapon variants?

No—unique yellow-text variants come with fixed pre-customized finishes and attachments, though they are permanently saved to your Ammu-Nation weapon locker.

How is this different from Gil's Kalinga .50?

Gil's Kalinga .50 is a Chapter 3 story reward from Safe Word, while the Black Eagle Import Kalinga .50 is a free-roam warehouse pickup in Balsero.
