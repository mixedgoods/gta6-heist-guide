GTA VI Beginner's Guide: First Hours, Systems, Saves & Mistakes to Avoid

Practical Day-1 guidance for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S players. Every tip below carries an explicit evidence label and connects directly to our free interactive tools.

1. WHAT TO DO FIRST

Lock Your First Manual Save & Set Up BuckMe

GTA VI does not include a mission-replay menu at launch and relies on a single overwriting autosave (Existing Site Data / Reliable Reporting). Open the pause menu as soon as free-roam starts in Chapter 1 and create a manual save in Slot #01. Once you reach the early Chapter 1 mission Buck Me at Fleeca in Key Lento, move spare cash from Checking → Family Savings via Buck Yourself so Wasted/Busted fees cannot drain it.

2. CORE GAMEPLAY SYSTEMS

Wanted Heat, Trunk Storage & Physical Deposits

Heavy weapons and duffel bags are managed across your personal loadout, safehouse lockers, and vehicle trunks (Reliable External Reporting). After stick-ups or free-roam robberies, deposit loose cash at physical ATMs before risking a shootout, and track fence contacts like Brian's Lockup (7700) and Donnie (available before Swamped) in the Properties & Fences Database.

3. JASON & LUCIA DUO PLAY

Using Jason and Lucia Effectively

Jason and Lucia maintain separate inventories and character-specific activities—for example, Bret Vogel's 15 Hunting Target Decks are Jason-only contracts (Existing Site Data / Community Reported). Before starting a heist or long exploration run, verify both protagonists' gear, check which character is active if a weapon seems missing, and keep both stocked with armor and suppressors.

4. RECOMMENDED SETTINGS FIRST

Settings New Players Should Adjust Immediately

GTA VI targets 30 FPS on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S at launch with no 60 FPS performance mode (Reliable External Reporting). Before diving into gunfights, tune stick deadzones, aim acceleration, camera follow height, and HDR/HGiG calibration so 30 FPS camera panning feels responsive rather than sluggish.

5 Beginner Mistakes to Avoid in Your First Playthrough

  • Relying on the single autosave: Always save manually before major story transitions like Swamped, Tourist Trap, Checking Out, and story heists.
  • Activating cheats on your main story save: Cheats disable trophies/achievements and saving for that session—isolate cheat testing to a dedicated sandbox slot (e.g., Slot #15).
  • Confusing Lab Fees keypad codes: 0420 opens the Moonstone Boutique back-room lab (Key Lento); 7700 opens Brian's Lockup further south in the Keys.
  • Carrying large unbanked cash early: Use physical ATMs and BuckMe Family Savings after early store stick-ups so hospital/bail fees don't wipe out your early bankroll.
  • Ignoring controls & troubleshooting updates: Install Rockstar's November 23, 2026 patch (PS5 01.002.002 / Xbox 1.0.2.2) to avoid launch autosave and mission-call blockers—see our Troubleshooting & Patch Notes Center and Controls Guide.
