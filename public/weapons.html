Tactical Gear, Body Armor & Plate Carriers

GTA 6 adopts a more grounded equipment system than GTA V, treating armor and carry gear as physical inventory items rather than invisible infinite buffers:

Body Armor & Plate Carriers CORROBORATED

Body armor and plate carriers can be bought at weapon retailers—including Ammu-Nation, Big Shots, and Chuck's Tackle & Shot—for roughly $250, with multiple colorways (black to camouflage). Once purchased, armor is equipped and managed from your inventory / items loadout tab and absorbs incoming bullet damage until depleted.

Vehicle Trunk & Safehouse Loadout Storage CORROBORATED

Because active carry capacity is restricted, extra long guns, spare armor plates, and tactical packs are stored in your personal vehicle's trunk or safehouse weapon lockers. Always re-arm from your trunk before entering Illegal Operations or major heists. See our Vehicles & Trunk Guide.

Tactical Helmets & Military Gear VERIFYING

Players frequently search for bulletproof tactical helmets and full military gear sets. While cosmetic headwear and tactical-style clothing exist in shops, verified headshot-blocking ballistic helmets or tiered military plate-rig stats have not been independently confirmed in Story Mode. We will update this section only with verified in-game testing.

Robbery Tools & Utility Gear CONFIRMED

Key utility items unlock through story progression: the Slim Jim (unlocked in Chapter 2's Moses mission in Port Gellhorn), Scuba Gear, Metal Detector & Arras Bullhead Speargun (Chapter 1 Island Dreams), and the Advanced Tracker Immobilizer (Chapter 3 Randy's Wish List).

Weapon Acquisition, Unlock Requirements & Customization

Owned vs. Unowned (Dropped) Weapons

Firearms bought from gun stores or unlocked via missions/contracts are owned weapons—they persist in your trunk and weapon locker and can be customized. Guns picked up off random enemies are treated as unowned: they cannot be modified at shops and are typically discarded once their initial magazine runs dry.

Weapon Customization Rules

Standard owned weapons can be customized at gun store workbenches with compatible attachments (such as suppressors, optics/scopes, and extended magazines—exact options depend on weapon model and shop stock). By contrast, Unique Weapon Variants cannot be cosmetically modified because their curated attachments are locked in place.

Weapons & Tactical Gear FAQ

How do you get Donnie's H&L .38 in GTA 6 and is it missable?

During Chapter 1's Don Gone mission in Key Lento, open the trunk of Donnie's red car after the fight with the three Aviators and grab Donnie's H&L .38 before destroying the car. Because there is no mission replay menu, missing it makes it permanently unobtainable on that save.

How do body armor and plate carriers work in GTA 6?

Purchase body armor / plate carriers at Ammu-Nation, Big Shots, or Chuck's Tackle & Shot (~$250, multiple colorways) and equip them from your inventory/items loadout tab. Extra armor can be stored in your personal vehicle's trunk.

Are polymer vs. metal rounds confirmed in GTA 6 Story Mode?

No. A polymer-versus-metal ammunition swap system and exact specialty ballistic multipliers have not been independently verified in Story Mode. Confirmed mechanics cover standard firearm calibers, explosive ordnance, and caliber matching for Pristine hunting carcasses.

Can you customize Unique Weapon Variants or dropped enemy guns?

No. Unique Weapon Variants have locked curated attachments (though they unlock permanently for both protagonists), and dropped enemy guns are unowned and cannot be modified at gun stores.
