Quick Answer: GTA 6 Vehicle Trunk & Cargo Storage by Class

In GTA 6, extra long guns, body armor, and heist gear are stored physically in your vehicle trunk rather than an unlimited weapon wheel. Based on our verified 33-vehicle dataset (vehicles.json), SUVs & Pickups are the highest-capacity haulers reported so far, while exact per-car slot counts for coupes and sports cars are not yet independently confirmed.
