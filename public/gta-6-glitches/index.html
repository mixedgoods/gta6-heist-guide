How to Read the GTA 6 Glitch & Patch Status Tracker

GTA 6 glitches change with every update, so this page tracks each one as a status rather than a one-off guide. Every entry names the platform, the game build it was tested on, the last test date and the evidence behind the status. A glitch is never marked patched or working without observed version evidence.

What the four statuses mean

  • Confirmed Working — reproduced on the listed platform and build.
  • Testing — under active testing; results may be inconsistent or hardware-dependent.
  • Confirmed Patched — reproduced earlier but no longer works after an update (the status history records the build).
  • Unverified — reported by players but not reproduced; treat as a rumor until upgraded.

How to use the tracker

  • Use the search box to find a glitch by name, location or money amount, and the status buttons to show only working or patched entries.
  • Switch on Platform Specific Only to show glitches that apply to a single console.
  • Open an entry for step-by-step instructions, source evidence and its status history before you attempt it.
