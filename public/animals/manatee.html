Tactical & Completion Role

The Manatee is a gentle, Docile marine mammal gliding through warm Leonida Keys coves and coastal springs. Unlike deep-water sharks, manatees are easy to observe in calm, clear nearshore water—making them one of the most accessible marine species to log early in Chapter 1.

  1. Start from Jason's House in Key Lento or launch the Crest Kayak right from the waterfront dock.
  2. Paddle slowly into sheltered warm coves, seagrass flats, and clear coastal springs around the Leonida Keys.
  3. Avoid high-speed boat wakes that disturb shallow coves; calm water makes manatee silhouettes easy to spot.
  4. Log the sighting toward your 100% Animals Compendium (100 of 255 species required) during Chapter 1 exploration.
