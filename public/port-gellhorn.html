Port Gellhorn Overview & Surrounding Areas

Port Gellhorn is a working-class Gulf Coast port town and industrial hub in Kelly County on the western side of Leonida. After the Chapter 1 transition out of the Leonida Keys, Port Gellhorn becomes Jason and Lucia's primary base of operations throughout Chapter 2, anchored by the Starlet Motel safehouse.

Key Sub-Areas & Landmarks

Port Gellhorn blends highway motel strips, pawn shops, working docks, and scrap corridors: Draper Island / Brooks Boatworks to the north, the US-9 commercial strip (Kingpin Bowling) to the south, the western motel corridor south of the railroad bridge (La Cascara Motel), Tanglewood Lane (Dunk's Trailer), Watkins Auto Parts (Moses), and the Gellhorn International Raceway area.

Surrounding Regions & Corridors

From Port Gellhorn, highway and rail routes connect northeast toward Ambrosia (via the Kelly County / Ambrosia rail corridor) and north into Mount Kalaga National Park, southeast toward the Grassrivers wetlands, and east across the state toward Vice City—where the story relocates after the Chapter 2 finale, Checking Out.

Chapter 2 Cutoff Warning: Completing Chapter 2's final mission (Checking Out, which requires building $15,000) permanently closes the Starlet Motel safehouse as an active Chapter 2 base. Lock a dedicated manual save in our 15-Slot Save Planner (Slot 06) before finishing Chapter 2 if you want an easy return point.

How to Unlock All Three Codes In-Game at Once

Reported across post-launch guides (Vice, Space4Games, GamesRadar): enter Brooks Boatworks in northern Port Gellhorn through the gap in the east-side fence, climb the exterior stairs of the gray building, go inside, and study the wall map to automatically log 0300, 6823, and 6969.

District Verification Note — Why 6769 Is Not Listed Above: Players often search for 6769 alongside Port Gellhorn safe codes, but Sunrise RV Park (6769) is a Kingfishers gang operation in Grassrivers / Mariana County (Pelican Valley near Gullet Marsh and Cottonmouth Lake), not Port Gellhorn. Banks, ATMs & Money-Making Opportunities

Port Gellhorn is designed around early-to-mid-game street hustles rather than late-game multi-million-dollar vaults. Use these verified cash loops during Chapter 2:

ATMs & the $20,000 Pocket-Cash Cap

Free-roam pocket cash caps at $20,000. Bank your robbery and vehicle-boosting earnings at physical ATMs located outside commercial strips, gas stations, and motels—or at BuckMe terminals—so you do not lose overflow or carry unbanked cash into firefights. Exact standalone ATM pin coordinates in Port Gellhorn are not yet individually mapped on our interactive map.

Local Banks, Pawn Shops & Convenience Stores

Port Gellhorn's commercial corridors include rob-able convenience stores, pawn shops, and local small bank/payday-loan branches. Unlike the three Kolt & Associates safes above, free-roam store and small bank safes use randomized codes per playthrough—check back-office monitors, sticky notes, or intimidate clerks/managers to open them.

Kolt & Associates Illegal Operations

Clearing Kingpin Bowling (0300), Brooks Boatworks (6823), and La Cascara Motel (6969) delivers fast Chapter 2 cash and contraband toward the $15,000 needed for Checking Out. See our Armored Trucks & Gang Ops Guide for tactics.

Low-Heat Vehicle Boosting (Moses)

After completing Moses, scan everyday sedans, coupes, and trucks with the WAiNK app to confirm they have no tracker, then deliver them to Watkins Auto Parts for steady, low-heat cash (community-reported ~$1,000–$2,000 per untracked car).

Port Gellhorn Shipyard, Docks, SCOOP Containers & Cargo Ship Status

Port Gellhorn's waterfront and shipyard draw heavy search traffic from players hunting for a sealed shipping container or rusted cargo ship. Because community reports frequently mix up different industrial objects, here is the verified breakdown of what is actually in the shipyard and docks:

Yellow SCOOP Containers CORROBORATED

SCOOP containers are distinct, padlocked yellow shipping containers placed at regional yards across Leonida, including spots around Port Gellhorn (reported near local businesses and the raceway via GTABase and post-launch guides). You cannot pry them open on arrival in Chapter 2: opening padlocked SCOOP containers requires the Cut-Off Tool (grinder), introduced during Chapter 3's Meet the Heders dockyard mission.

Rusted Cargo Ship & Sealed Container UNVERIFIED LEAD

While Port Gellhorn's working waterfront features industrial docks, maritime vessels, and coastal wrecks, no standalone "rusted cargo ship" secret vault, fixed coordinate, or guaranteed sealed-container reward has been independently verified at the Port Gellhorn shipyard. Ordinary dockyard shipping containers are non-interactive cover/scenery, and the pre-launch "$8.2M Pier 4 gantry crane heist" does not exist.

Verified Shipyard & Waterfront Access Points

  • Brooks Boatworks (Northern Docks / Draper Island): CORROBORATED — Enter through the east-side fence gap and climb the gray building's exterior stairs to log all three Kolt & Associates codes (0300, 6823, 6969) and crack safe 6823. View Brooks Boatworks on Map →
  • Kolts Gang Dock Compound: CORROBORATED — Industrial waterfront yard visited during Chapter 2's Slam Dunk mission (delivering Dr. Bill Kolts before the Wild Hog Point ambush).
  • Gellhorn Outfitters (Docks Armory): CONFIRMED — Waterfront Ammu-Nation stocking shotguns, RPG ordnance, ammo, and body armor before dock runs. View Dock Armory Pin →
  • Port Gellhorn Industrial Bay & Strip-Mall Garage: CONFIRMED — Purchasable garages suited for staging untracked cars and duffel-bag haulers (view in Properties Database).
  • Container Crane vs. Cargo Ship vs. SCOOP Containers: Do not confuse dockyard container cranes (no heist), ordinary scenery shipping containers (unopenable), padlocked yellow SCOOP containers (Cut-Off Tool from Ch. 3 Meet the Heders), or unverified rusted cargo ship rumors. For confirmed major Story Heists, see The Cartel Heist (Chapter 3) and VCIA Takedown (Chapter 4).

Port Gellhorn Frequently Asked Questions

What are the verified safe codes in Port Gellhorn?

There are three corroborated fixed gang-operation safe codes in Port Gellhorn (all Kolt & Associates): 0300 (Kingpin Bowling), 6823 (Brooks Boatworks), and 6969 (La Cascara Motel). Inspect the wall map upstairs inside the gray building at Brooks Boatworks to log all three automatically.

Is safe code 6769 in Port Gellhorn?

No. Code 6769 belongs to the Sunrise RV Park dock building safe (Kingfishers gang) in the Grassrivers / Mariana County area (Pelican Valley near Gullet Marsh and Cottonmouth Lake), not Port Gellhorn.

Where are the fences and stolen vehicle drop-offs in Port Gellhorn?

Moses (Watkins Auto Parts, MW icon) buys untracked stolen cars after Chapter 2's Moses mission. Dunk (Dunk's Trailer on Tanglewood Lane) buys stolen goods after Chapter 2's Slam Dunk mission.

Are there fixed safe codes for Port Gellhorn convenience stores, pawn shops, or small banks?

No. Free-roam convenience stores, pawn shops, and local small bank safes use randomized codes per playthrough. Look at manager monitors, desk sticky notes, or intimidate store clerks to get the active code.

Is there a rusted cargo ship or sealed container at the Port Gellhorn shipyard?

No standalone rusted cargo ship secret vault or fixed container coordinates have been independently verified at the Port Gellhorn shipyard. Padlocked yellow SCOOP containers are corroborated across Leonida (including Port Gellhorn areas) and require the Cut-Off Tool from Chapter 3's Meet the Heders mission, whereas ordinary dock shipping containers are non-interactive scenery.

Is there an $8.2M Pier 4 gantry crane heist at the Port Gellhorn shipyard?

No. That was pre-launch speculation and is not part of the launched game. In-game Port Gellhorn dock activity centers on Brooks Boatworks (6823), the Kolts Gang dock compound in Slam Dunk, and local Chapter 2 robberies.

How do ATMs and cash limits work in Port Gellhorn?

Free-roam pocket cash caps at $20,000. Deposit cash from robberies, gang safes, and Moses car drops at physical ATMs around commercial strips, gas stations, and motels (or BuckMe terminals) while building the $15,000 needed before Chapter 2's finale, Checking Out.
