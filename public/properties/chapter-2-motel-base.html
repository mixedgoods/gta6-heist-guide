Strategic Value & Heist Staging

Permanent Closure Warning (Checking Out)

Missable Cutoff Alert: The Chapter 2 Motel Base (Starlet Motel) permanently closes after completing Chapter 2's finale, Checking Out. Grab the collectible card from the motel refrigerator after Coffee Break, clear your stash, and lock Save Slot #06 before triggering the raid.

Chapter 2 Port Gellhorn Hub

During Chapter 2 (Paradise Lost), this motel room gives you a bed save, trunk staging in the exterior parking bay, and rapid access to Port Gellhorn pawn shops, Moses at Watkins Auto Parts, and the three Kolt & Associates safes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Chapter 2 Motel Base stay open all game?

No—it permanently closes as a story safehouse after completing Chapter 2's finale mission, Checking Out.

What is missable at the Chapter 2 Motel Base?

Collect the card on the motel refrigerator after Coffee Break, grab the Moreland 850 Shotgun on the bed during Checking Out, and clear your stash beforehand.

Can you buy another house in Port Gellhorn after it closes?

No—GTA 6 Story Mode has 4 story-unlocked safehouses and no purchasable story houses, though you can buy Port Gellhorn's Strip-Mall Garage and Industrial Bay.
