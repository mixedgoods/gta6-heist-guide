Why Watson Bay Location Matters for Heist Staging

Built-In Gun Cabinet

Unlike basic parking bays, Paradise Garage includes a dedicated gun cabinet so you can configure long-gun trunk loadouts and swap rifles right beside your Vice City getaway cars.

Port & Downtown Access

Positioned in Watson Bay, it gives fast access to both Downtown Vice City bank routes and coastal escape piers without fighting Vice Beach traffic.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you unlock Paradise Garage in GTA 6?

Paradise Garage is unlocked via GTA 6 Ultimate Edition ownership.

Where is Paradise Garage located?

It is located in the Watson Bay district of Vice City.

Can Standard Edition players still buy garages in Vice City?

Yes—all players can unlock 8 purchasable garages via the smartphone real-estate app after Daily Driver, including Downtown Vice Parking Bay and Little Cuba Lockup Garage.
