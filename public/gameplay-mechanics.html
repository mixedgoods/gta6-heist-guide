CONFIRMED HEIST MECHANICS & META

Rockstar has overhauled criminal mechanics in GTA 6. Master realistic loot weights, vehicle trunk armories, and dual-operative sync tactics.

Physical Realism

Loot Bag Weight & Sprint Penalties

Category: Movement Physics • Source: Trailer & Mechanics Analysis

Unlike GTA V where players carry infinite gold with zero movement changes, GTA 6 implements realistic weight physics:

• Gold Bullion: -25% sprint speed & double stamina consumption.
• Uncut Cartel Diamonds: 0% speed penalty (Maximum profit-to-weight ratio).
• Fiat Currency Bundles: -8% sprint speed.

Strategy Tip: Assign heavy gold pallets to hired specialist NPCs or heavy enforcer squadmates while your point man carries high-value bearer bonds.

Arsenal Stowage

Vehicle Trunk Weapon Armory

Category: Loadout Logistics • Source: RDR2 Evolution

The "invisible weapon wheel" holding 40 guns is gone. In GTA 6, players carry two sidearms and one long gun on their sling.

Heavy thermal lances, plasma drills, RPGs, and LMGs must be physically stored inside your getaway car's trunk. If your getaway vehicle is impounded or blown up, your secondary heavy weapons are lost.

Co-Op Synergy

Lucia & Jason Dual-Operative Sync

Category: Character Traits • Source: Confirmed Trailers

In solo story mode and 2-player co-op, Jason and Lucia possess distinct operational specialties:

• Lucia: Advanced lockpicking, faster keycard swipe bypasses, and calmer hostage control.
• Jason: Precision sniper cover, vehicular pit maneuvers, and heavy suppression fire against armored SWAT responders.

Law Enforcement

Smart Police AI & CCTV Forensic Tracking

Category: Response AI • Source: Patent Analysis

Police AI no longer spawns randomly behind you. In Leonida:

• Vehicle Description & License Plates: Leaving a heist in a vehicle with spotted license plates alerts traffic cameras across Vice City.
• Clothing & Disguises: Changing clothes in safehouse garages sheds 2 stars instantly.
• K9 Units: Police search dogs can track scent through swamp mangroves.
