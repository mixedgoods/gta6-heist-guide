Physical Realism

Loot Bag Weight & Sprint Penalties

Category: Movement Physics • Source: Trailer & Mechanics Analysis

Unlike GTA V where players carry infinite gold with zero movement changes, GTA 6 implements realistic weight physics:

• Gold Bullion: -25% sprint speed & double stamina consumption.
• Uncut Cartel Diamonds: 0% speed penalty (Maximum profit-to-weight ratio).
• Fiat Currency Bundles: -8% sprint speed.

Strategy Tip: Assign heavy gold pallets to hired specialist NPCs or heavy enforcer squadmates while your point man carries high-value bearer bonds.

Arsenal Stowage

Vehicle Trunk Weapon Armory

Category: Loadout Logistics • Source: RDR2 Evolution

The "invisible weapon wheel" holding 40 guns is gone. In GTA 6, players carry two sidearms and one long gun on their sling.

Heavy thermal lances, plasma drills, RPGs, and LMGs must be physically stored inside your getaway car's trunk. If your getaway vehicle is impounded or blown up, your secondary heavy weapons are lost.
