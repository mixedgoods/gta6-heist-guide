## Core Honesty & Community Rules (Read Before Posting)

1. **No False Activity Claims:** Do NOT claim "hundreds of players are already submitting" or "our active community has mapped everything." Be transparent: the discovery network just opened and is inviting founding contributors.
2. **No Automated or Bulk Posting:** Every draft below requires explicit human review and manual posting. Never use bots or cross-post identically across 10 groups.
3. **Respect Platform Rules:** Follow subreddit self-promotion ratios (e.g., r/GTA6 rules on external links and flair), Facebook group admin rules, and Discord channel guidelines (post only in designated promo/resources/discovery channels).
4. **No Rockstar Affiliation:** Always make clear GTA6HeistGuide.com is an independent fan-built reference and community tool, unaffiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
5. **Privacy & Attribution:** Contributors choose whether to remain anonymous or receive a public handle credit. Evidence links (screenshots/clips) are reviewed by moderators before anything goes live.
