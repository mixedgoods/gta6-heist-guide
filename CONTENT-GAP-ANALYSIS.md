## Topic-by-Topic Gap Matrix (Compared Against Existing Canonical URLs)

Per SEO best practices, existing canonical hubs were upgraded rather than creating competing duplicate URLs.

| Topic / Search Intent | Existing GTA6HeistGuide URL | Gap Identified vs. Competitors | Action Taken |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Beginner's Guide** ("what to do first", saving, BuckMe, Jason & Lucia, starter settings, mistakes) | `/gta-6-tips-and-tricks/` | Previously 659 words focused mostly on tips/tool cards without a structured beginner onboarding walkthrough. | **Upgraded** with `#beginners-guide` covering all 7 beginner questions, linked to Save Planner, Controls, Settings, Money, and Map. |
| **Complete 100% Completion Guide** (35 requirements, 55 trophies, stunts dual threshold, verified animals) | `/gta-6-100-completion-tracker/` | Strong interactive tracker, but lacked a condensed editorial strategy roadmap and previously allowed unconfirmed reference animals to inflate `comp:35`. | **Upgraded** with `#completion-guide` roadmap + strict verified-only animal completion filtering. |
| **Mission Walkthrough Library** (chapter-by-chapter spoiler-shielded guides) | `/missions/` | Previously stated 0 walkthroughs published even for missions already documented on-site (*Buck Me*, *Lab Fees*, *Tourist Trap*, *Credit Check*, *The Cartel Heist*, *VCIA Takedown*). | **Upgraded** with `#mission-walkthroughs` spoiler-shielded `<details>` library covering 6 documented missions across Chapters 1–4 without publishing empty templates. |
| **Collectibles & Hidden Locations** (Cards, Flamingos, Animals, Underwater, WAiNK, Stunts, Hidden Interiors) | `/gta-6-collectibles/` | Had counters, but needed category-by-category editorial identification guidance tied to verified databases and map. | **Upgraded** with `#collectible-categories-guide` covering all 7 collectible/hidden categories with honest unverified-coordinate labeling. |
| **Fastest Money Methods** (Story Heists, Banks, Jewelry, Pawn Shops, Fences, Contraband, Glitches vs. Normal) | `/gta-6-money-guide/` | Lacked a unified side-by-side comparison table separating normal gameplay from community-reported exploits with requirements, gear, payouts, and risks. | **Upgraded** with `#money-methods-matrix` linked to Heist Payout Calculator, Heist Planner, and Properties Database. |
| **Official Patch Notes & Known Issues** (Update `01.002.002` PS5 / `1.0.2.2` Xbox — Nov 23, 2026) | `/gta-6-troubleshooting/` | Previously stated no official patch notes were published for `01.002.002` / `1.0.2.2`. | **Upgraded** with `#official-patch-notes` listing all 7 official Rockstar Support fixes plus ongoing community-reported known issues. |
| **Wildlife Taxonomy & Evidence Separation** | `/animals.html` | Real-world reference photos were conflated with in-game hunting/location claims and contained naming errors (*White River Otter*, *Stingray / Manta Ray*). | **Audited & Fixed** all 49 entries with scientific names, 3-tier evidence labels, and separated reference browsing from 100% completion. |
